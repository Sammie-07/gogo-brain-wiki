# #teamgogo Training — Kim Fraser on Farming, Open Houses and Listing Dominance in a Down Market, plus eXp MCP and REMLO

> Weekly [[#teamgogo]] Wednesday training (Oct 14, 2026), hosted by [[Gogo Bethke]]. Featured guest [[Kim Fraser]] (introduced as eXp's #1 solo agent) on farming, open houses and listing marketing in a 3–5 year "winter." Also: [[Rafael Mino]] on the eXp MCP, [[Jason Dupree]] on REMLO and market charts, [[Nick Krem]] on [[Halo]] AEO websites, [[Kristy Waker]] on next week's eXpCon recap.

## Key Takeaways

### Team housekeeping (folds into [[Downline Community Architecture]])
- **The Hub is mandatory.** Slack is gone. The Hub is where agents reach brokers, eXp corporate and each other, and where invites to stages, private events and celebrations go out.
- **Team Gogo map:** agents are added automatically, but an eXp data glitch dropped some. Search for yourself; if you're missing or wrong (pin, email, phone), fill out Rafael's form and he fixes it the same day.
- **Main team chat** gives direct access to Gogo, her team and other agents, with links before every live call.
- **Re-onboard.** Without the onboarding call, agents only get what eXp offers. Agents who joined 4–5 years ago should re-onboard: about half of today's offerings came later. Use the "onboard your agent" button on the Team Gogo site.
- **Partner With Me page:** Gogo's team builds an agent-attraction page in about 2 minutes.
- Schedule: #teamgogo trains Wednesdays at 1 PM, tech call Wednesdays at 3 PM (where the MCP setup is taught). Attend on a computer so you can open links live.

### eXp MCP and AI tools (folds into [[AI for Real Estate]])
- **The eXp MCP** (announced at eXpCon, Salt Lake City) connects eXp University, the eXp Dashboard and your CRM (BoldTrail, Lofty and others) to Claude or ChatGPT. Copy the connector link and paste it into your AI's connections. Gogo has it on both.
- **Name the MCP in the prompt.** Gogo asked for a 90-day plan for a new eXp agent: generic answer. Adding "use the eXp University MCP" rebuilt it around eXp tools (e.g. FastCap).
- Other asks: this month's revenue share, lifetime transaction count, a new agent's join date, upline and level relative to you. Works in a normal chat; no custom GPT or project needed.
- **Jason Dupree's macro keypad:** each button fires a saved AI prompt, opens a set of websites or loads documents, instead of pasting prompts from a Word doc.
- **Nick Krem's "3–5 clicks" rule:** if an AI tool takes more than 3–5 clicks to start working, it isn't mature enough to invest time in.

### AI-findable websites (folds into [[Personal Branding]])
- [[Halo]] now has a built-in **AEO score** with a "fix it" button (adds FAQ, breadcrumbs, site names). It checks Cloudflare's SEO/AEO/GEO guidance every Friday, updates sites itself and blogs 3 times a week.
- Nick's stats: 37% of searches start in an AI tool, 62% of Google searches end with zero clicks, AI-referred leads are 440% more likely to convert. "AI is the modern referral."
- Pricing for Gogo's people: free start with 5 free edits, then $99/mo (white-label versions sell for ~$1,500/mo). In the Team Gogo training, agents built a site by text in 8.5 minutes on average.
- **Gogo:** your eXp website isn't findable by AI. Not findable = you don't exist = you aren't a choice = no commission. Kim had a deal this year from a client who found her through Claude.

### Mortgage income and the "rates are high" close (folds into [[Wealth Building]] / [[Negotiation]])
- **REMLO:** do at least **6 buy-side transactions a year** and earn **0.5% on mortgages you generate** in all 50 states ($1,500 extra on a $300K loan). $0 cost; free in-house training under 2 weeks. Uses: out-of-state contacts you can't serve on the real estate side, and HELOCs for past clients sitting on equity.
- **Gogo's "rates are high" close:** ask buyers what their parents paid when they were kids (14–16%), then what their childhood home is worth today.
- Jason's charts for social posts: national Housing Price Index map, appreciation since 2009, 36-month appreciation in the top 20 cities, rate history back to 1976.

### Farming and direct mail (folds into [[Lead Generation]])
- **Kim's scale:** 4 farmed communities, ~15,000 mailers a month. 20 of her 52 listings this year came from the farm, including $3–3.5M homes.
- **In-house printing:** a leased commercial printer (~$1,350/mo) and a home production room; a newsletter printed two-up costs under 10¢ each. Monthly marketing spend fell from $15–16K to under $8K.
- **Budget:** ~$1 per piece (~$1.25 with postage), so $300/mo covers ~300 homes. Split with a lender partner (equal space) to reach ~500. Farm a small area consistently rather than a big one thinly.
- **Two touches beat one.** Her neighborhood-magazine ad ran ~10 years but only took off once paired with the newsletter.
- **Give a farm 1–2 years.** People now move every 10–12 years instead of every 7.
- **Mailing:** EDDM for whole routes (bundles of 100 with a facing sheet; no opt-outs), bulk mail for a targeted list (e.g. waterfront only, top competitors removed). Done-for-you newsletters with comps cost ~$1.20 each (~60¢ with a lender split).
- **Newsletter contents:** just listed / just sold, days on market, a QR code per listing to a landing page with video and Matterport.
- **Low budget:** hand-delivered door hangers, or a digital farm (neighborhood Facebook page, giveaways, a permission-based email newsletter, [[Krista Mashore]]'s "digital mayor" approach). Print works better for Gen X and older, digital for millennials and younger. New-build neighborhoods are hard to farm (builder concessions undercut resales).

### Open houses (folds into [[Open Houses]])
- Kim does **80–90 open houses a year** after 20+ years in the business. In Q4, run them Friday through Sunday if you need to meet people.
- Up to **20 directional signs with balloons**. People tell her she seems to be the only agent working.
- **No listings? Borrow other agents'.** Early on she held open houses every Saturday and Sunday, sometimes two a day.
- Her family and team hand-delivered 4,500 open-house door hangers linking to the home's video.
- Her first month at eXp: two $4M listings, both sold to buyers from her open houses, which she almost skipped in 113-degree heat.
- **Gogo's open-house-to-listing play:** before, knock on neighbors' doors and invite them to "choose your neighbor"; after it sells, call the neighbors (numbers from title) to say it sold and ask about their plans. That wins listings that weren't hers.
- Most agents won't door-knock 5-5-10 or 10-10-20, so those who do stand out.

### Listing marketing and pricing (folds into [[Negotiation]])
- **"Do not do anything halfway":** heavy glossy cardstock, brochures instead of flyers in the farm, a booklet for luxury listings, Matterport, cinematic video and a QR code on everything.
- **Video on every listing** ($500–$2,000). If nobody in your market does it, that alone sets you apart.
- **Perma-flyer:** an acrylic sign-post flyer with a QR code (~18×24 in., under $25); zip-tie it on if your market has no flyer boxes.
- Design matters less than print quality and QR links (Canva, eXp templates, a $10 Etsy template).
- **Overpriced sellers:** decide whether that yard sign in your farm is worth it. If the list price starts 5–10% high, get a written price-reduction plan.
- **Hard conversations:** walk the home "with the eyes of a buyer" and name smells, pets and condition.
- Buyer agents have the edge in this market. New agents start buyer-heavy, move to 50/50, then go listing-heavy to control their schedule.

### Lead-source tracking and picking lanes (folds into [[Goal Setting]])
- **Track every deal's source.** Kim's ~75 deals this year: farming 20, past clients 15, agent referrals 9, sphere 8, open houses 5, one from Claude, plus outgoing referrals.
- **About 10 ways to make money in real estate; pick 3, at most 5, that you'll do consistently.** One channel isn't enough in this market. Kim's lanes: farming, open houses, past clients; next year she adds sphere events and YouTube.
- Skip tactics you hate (she skips cold door-knocking and expireds).
- **Multi-state licensing:** "You're not a tree." Build out-of-state referral partners.
- For an agent new to a market: start a YouTube channel on the area, shoot B-roll, tag the location on every post (Gogo). Strangers hire a new agent more easily than friends do.

### Resilience and market cycles (folds into [[Mindset]])
- **Real estate is a roller coaster.** Kim sold 101 homes in 2005 and 19 in 2009. Her eXp volume: $81M (2021), $57M (2022), $36M (2023), $52M (2024), $57M (2025), $110–120M projected (2026).
- eXp leadership calls this a 3–5 year "winter"; Brian Buffini predicts ~400K fewer agents.
- **Know the macro story** for clients: years of near-zero rates pushed prices up and created today's affordability problem. Normal appreciation is 3–5% a year.
- Seasons of life: with kids at home Kim intentionally ran at $15–20M a year. Losses are recoverable (a short sale cost her ~$1M; she rebuilt over 10 years).

### Goal rewards (folds into [[Goal Setting]])
- **Gogo:** people only move toward pleasure or away from pain. She uses pleasure: a reward sized to the goal (a belt, a massage, Bora Bora). Make it outrageous and actually claim it, so your brain wants that feeling again.
- Make number decisions, not feeling decisions. "Nothing works unless you do."
