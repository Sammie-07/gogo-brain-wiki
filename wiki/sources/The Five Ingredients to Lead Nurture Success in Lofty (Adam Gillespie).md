The core teaching: the **"5 Ingredients to Success Pie,"** five automations every lead gets the moment it enters the CRM so nurture happens hands-free and the agent is only ever the *human in the loop*. Built around [[Lofty]] but portable to BoldTrail/KVCore, Commissions Inc, etc. Opens with a [[Jason Dupree]] mortgage/market minute. Original Zoom VTT stored in Clippings.

## Key Takeaways

### The thesis
- **"The lead is not the win. The booked appointment is the win"** — and the road to the appointment isn't the same length for every lead. Agents spend to *generate* leads and then let them die because there's no complete nurture system; the lead "doesn't go cold and die, it just buys with somebody else."
- **AI is back-of-house, the human is front-of-house.** Never let AI voice dialers / chatbots / the Lofty AI *talk to* your leads — "the general public does not like artificial intelligence." (The only two industries where AI voice answering is welcome are cable and cell-phone companies, "because nobody likes to talk to them.") Since 2022 the human craving for human touch is the highest in 50 years. Use AI to *write the messaging*; then **jump in as the human in the loop** when a lead raises their hand.
- **Automation is not the relationship** — it just removes the repetitive work so you can have the real conversation. The CRM makes you *consistent* → more conversations → more time. Time is the real payoff ("I watched my kids grow up from inside my office, and it sucked").
- **Kill the canned library smart plans** ("canned spam") — old psychological sales techniques that don't work on modern society and don't know your voice, story, or tonality. **Everything should be custom.** The higher-leverage move is to **clone yourself into a custom GPT** (trained on your voice) and let it draft the messaging.

### The 5 Ingredients to Success Pie (applied automatically to every lead)
1. **Property alerts** (buyers) / **market reports & snapshots** (sellers). The "chips and salsa" you drop before they forget you. **Send to your own IDX site, not the MLS** — you get website traffic (helps Google/Bing indexing; Lofty auto-indexes listings with Google), *and* you can monitor on-site behavior (favorites, showing requests) which fires follow-up automations. **Sweet spot 70–150 listings** — too many and undecided buyers won't choose; keep the search narrow enough to stay useful, then refine it *after* the first conversation ("take their order").
2. **Informational email drip** — note the word *informational*, **not conversion**. A **long-term buyer/seller informational drip: 2 emails a month for 2 years**, walking them through the whole buy/sell process (do I need a pre-approval? how do showings work? how is a FICO score determined?). Never force a decision, never "you're wasting money on rent." "You're the only one that kept communicating with me, but you're also the only one that didn't sell me." A closing came back **6 years** later.
3. **Automated text campaigns** — to **get the appointment, not to qualify** (qualification happens in the conversation). First text **5 minutes** after the lead comes in, aligned to *how* they came in (open house / FB ad / PPC / landing page). Then daily for ~**10–12 days**, then a **30-day pause**, then it fires back up referencing the timeframe. **No answer ≠ no — only an unsubscribe is a no.** "Leads that don't respond are not bad leads, they're just not ready yet."
4. **Holiday e-cards** — personality-driven, ~**20 holidays a year**, **updated once each January**. The tell-tale example: a **Valentine's card** ("heart-shaped hot tub, melted chocolates, heart floaties") generated with **Gemini** from a photo of himself, sent to **2,000 clients** — none said "ready to buy," all said "you're hilarious," and nobody forgot him. Personality is what makes people comfortable; a "dumb" salesy holiday card gets ignored.
5. **Call schedule** — the least favorite but required. **36 calls over 603 days** (front-loaded: ~1/day for 10 days, then tapering to 1/2 months). The average lead **doesn't answer until the 7th attempt** — most "bad leads" are just leads nobody actually tried on. Simple appointment-setting script; the *only* daily manual task is: open the CRM, see who needs a call, make the call.

### Mechanics & guardrails
- **Auto-pause / human-in-the-loop:** the moment a lead replies or books, the smart plan **auto-stops** so a canned text never talks over a live conversation ("we literally just talked yesterday… is this guy dumb?"). Stops on reply, on booking, on opt-out; notifies the agent when attention is needed.
- **Every conversation ends by setting the next one** — tell the lead exactly when you'll talk again and set that task, which pauses the call cadence automatically. "I do what I tell you I'm gonna do, which a lot of agents don't."
- **Body language = 80% of communication** — keep calls short, get them to **Zoom or in person** so they see you're a real human ("holy shit, Adam's a real dude").
- **You need only a few smart plans, not 50** — one nurture set per lead *type* (first-time buyer, downsizer, new build, open house). Same treatment for everyone: property alert + email drip + text campaign + holiday cards + call schedule.
- **Build once in ~4 weeks** (1 hr/day): wk1 audit + property alerts, wk2 email/text campaigns, wk3 holiday cards + call tasks, wk4 connect/test/launch. **Test on yourself first** — put yourself in the CRM before you test on a real lead.
- **"Stop relying on your memory"** — you will not remember to touch hundreds/thousands of leads; the CRM carries that weight.
- **Platform reality:** works in **Lofty** (best), **BoldTrail/KVCore** (clunky, built for brokerages), **Commissions Inc.** — anything that can auto-email + auto-text. **FUB can't auto-text** (one weird 866-number auto-text nobody answers). **GoHighLevel lacks IDX/MLS.** "Stop giving Zillow your data."

### Open house → legal right to contact (QR capture)
- Take a tablet with a **QR code**; skip paper flyers ("save the trees"). Scanning routes to a **Lofty landing page / forced-registration form** (name, number, email). On submit they **agree to be contacted** — a paper sign-in sheet gives you **no legal right** to text/call; the QR opt-in does. It also **auto-tags the address**, runs **email verification**, and **searches their social**, then triggers the open-house smart plan. Lofty gives **unlimited landing pages** — the modern version of Gogo's old KVCore trick (paste a listing link to Facebook → forced registration → CRM); the woman who trained the team on Lofty years ago couldn't show it, but it *is* doable via a Lofty landing page + forced registration.

### Why Lofty > BoldTrail (Adam is not employed by Lofty)
- Lofty is **built for the individual agent** and non-techy people; BoldTrail/KVCore is **clunky, built for brokerages** to oversee everyone. Lofty smart plans are a simple **flow chart** (trigger → wait → action). Built-in co-pilot AI ("what are my top 3 leads right now?"), everything (calls/emails/texts/tasks/automations) on one people page.
- **Data ownership:** anything you put in an eXp-provided program belongs to eXp (per the ICA) — which is why you must **request permission to export your leads** (they never say no, and eXp doesn't market to them). Adam has turned down multiple Lofty job offers so he can keep **criticizing** it honestly.
- **eXp "CRM of choice":** pick one, **switch once a year**. If you chose wrong and aren't far in, you can push eXp to switch early. Lofty direct ≈ **$399/mo**; Adam's white-label ≈ **$100/mo cheaper**. If you're not doing **6–10 transactions/yr**, that budget is better spent on lead gen into whatever CRM you already have. **Lofty website $49/mo** (vs. free basic); supports **custom HTML** — Adam **vibe-coded a branded mortgage calculator** (pulls tax/insurance by zip via free API, embedded Calendly CTA) on ChatGPT.

### AI as the personal execution layer
- **Clone yourself** (custom GPT trained on your voice, since ~2022): writes blogs, YouTube scripts, SWOT analyses, and long replies "cussing and sounding just like me" in ~3 minutes instead of 20–30 — compounded time savings. [[Alexis Carrera]] is mid-build on her own AI clone (via **Claude** and **ChatGPT**) to attach to her Lofty so nurture sounds like *her*.
- **Multi-language nurture:** duplicate smart plans tagged by language (English/Spanish) fire the right one off a tag; a custom GPT trained on informal Russian + **HeyGen** video translation lets him serve a 5-property repeat client in his own language.
- Presentation built in **Gamma.app** from a single prompt (~5 min).

### Adam's coaching model
- **"Done-with-you"** one-on-one: meet weekly 60–90 min inside your Lofty, build smart plans together tailored to your lead sources, homework between sessions (e.g., clone yourself), and after a couple of months "you actually know how to use Lofty" and don't need him. Free demo before anyone buys their own Lofty; free **"Getting Started with Lofty"** series on his YouTube channel.
