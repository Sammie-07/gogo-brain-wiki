# The Chaos-Free Agent — Google Workspace as Your Operating System (Kalie Kinsella)

> GGTC / GoGet'Em Community training (host: Natalia). [[Kalie Kinsella]] ("That Google Girl," a Google Workspace consultant and ex-transaction-coordination business owner) shows how to run a real estate business as a CEO on Google Workspace instead of "manic chaos." ~1h39m. Second appearance for Kalie (complements her Google Business Profile training). No NDA. Exact date not stated (mid-Sep 2026).

## Key Takeaways

### Mindset — run it like a CEO
- Agents live in "manic chaos" (notebooks, sticky notes, scattered tools). The fix is **centralization + micro-habits**, not more tools. **"Everything in three clicks or less"** (ideally two).
- **You are a CEO of your independent business** — spend your time on revenue-generating work; store/automate/outsource the rest.
- **Time = money = the means to your "why."** Optimize time via organization.
- **Don't hire an assistant to fix a "you" problem**, and **never train your own assistant** ("operations people talk to operations people, realtors talk to realtors"). A real org-building assistant is an **$80–100K/yr** hire needing ~6 months runway — fix your systems first and reinvest in revenue.
- **Tool + habit:** a tool without the habit is "a fancy car you can't drive."
- **AI stance:** agents not using tech "will be blown out of the water within 6 months," but AI should replace the **mundane/repeatable**, not you; don't let AI set up your systems (you know your business better).
- **Anti-shiny-object:** don't chase every tool a big name endorses — audit what you already have.

### Google Workspace vs free Gmail (foundation)
- Workspace = email on a **custom domain**; `name@gmail.com` = no security, no storage, missing business tools. Workspace is **under $15/mo** (2 TB storage; 3,000+ tools). **Use Business Standard, not Starter** (Starter can't do mass email). Use the **Chrome** browser and the **official Google apps** (Gmail, Tasks, Keep, Calendar), not Apple Mail / third-party.

### The apps (each paired with a habit)
- **Gmail — labels & filters:** stop dragging; build **filters** that auto-route on arrival. **Nested "mommy labels"** (parents: Reference / In Progress / Back Office). Inbox type → **Unread First** once under ~10–20 unread. **Starred = the to-do list.** Agent use: auto-forward **Skyslope** notices (EMD, missing docs) to your TC; route each active client to a label; never delete transactional email. Advanced auto-rules (e.g., delete promos >30 days) need **Gemini AI Studio** or Zapier.
- **Google Tasks** ("my brain"): **"if it's not on my task list or calendar, it doesn't exist to me."** One-click "Add to Tasks" from an email keeps the email linked; repeating tasks; surfaces atop Google Calendar. Habit: **task it the moment it enters your head** — before any call/text/email. Follow through on small promises (CMA-by-Friday example) or you break trust.
- **Google Keep** (digital sticky notes): ideas with **no deadline/action** — planning, a "sniper list" of prospects, referral partners, templates, links, meeting notes. Labels, checklists, reminders, pinning, share-by-email. Prefer Google **Password Manager** over Keep for passwords.
- **Google Drive:** central storage; reorganize weekly. **Native "Request e-signature"** (right-click → Share) **replaces DocuSign/Dotloop** (unless the brokerage requires them); also replaces Dropbox/Trello/Asana/Airtable.
- **Google Calendar:** put **everything** (personal + professional, color-coded, integrated) on it. **Appointment Schedule = a free, unlimited Calendly replacement** (Calendly now charges beyond one scheduler); auto confirmations + reminders (her cadence: **1 day, 1 hour, 10 min before**); custom intake per link. **#1 hack — calendar blocking:** Sunday-night review, add "block" events so the scheduler never double-books. Creed: "on time = late; 5–10 min early = on time."
- **Google Forms:** don't build a form per use — use **Sections + "go to section based on answer"** to route one intake (Buy vs Sell) → one linked Sheet. Turn on new-response email alerts; custom confirmation message = a follow-up touchpoint. Replaces Jotform/Cognito/Typeform (~$20–30/mo).
- **Google Sites:** free responsive sites — an **agent resource center** (buyer/seller presentations, vendor list, **2–3 lenders, never one = steering**) and **per-listing landing pages** (swap photos/desc on a template in ~5 min; you own the traffic/data). Google favors its own products, so a Google Site can out-rank a brokerage-hosted site that captures leads for the brokerage.
- **Contacts:** import/tag your CRM list as a **backup you own** (goes with you if you leave the brokerage). If your CRM is only fancy contact storage (~$250/mo), cancel it — a CRM is only worth it if you use 80–90% of its automation.
- **Mass email / newsletters** (Business Standard): HTML **Layouts** built into Compose (branded, auto **unsubscribe** for compliance); **BCC a named Contacts list**; **~2,500/day** limit (maybe 5,000 — unverified); split large lists. Replaces Constant Contact / Flodesk.
- **Gemini + Google AI Studio:** Gemini is included (can replace a standalone AI assistant); **AI Studio** builds workflows/automations from a plain-English outcome (preferred over the coder-oriented Google Labs).

### The economics
- Audit bank statements for every subscription Google replaces; she's saved agents **up to $7,000/yr**. Implement **one tool at a time** for two weeks, then add the next ("take down a forest one tree at a time").
- **Her offers:** 1:1 consult **$175/hr** (screen-share/organize live); **done-for-you Workspace setup, flat $500** (DNS/authentication so mail doesn't hit spam, custom domain, migration). Note: you **can't migrate Google Business Profile reviews** from @gmail to Workspace but **can claim ownership** under the new account — don't DIY ("mess it up and it's gone forever").
