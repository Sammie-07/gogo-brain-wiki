# The Circle Coaching — Team Accountability and Firing, Team Profitability, AI Agents and Idea Pipelines, and Stage CTAs (de-identified)

> Private [[The Circle|Circle]] coaching call (Oct 27, 2026). **NDA: raw transcript not retained; all member PII removed.** Only [[Gogo Bethke]]'s generalizable teachings are captured.

## Key Takeaways

### The "Pitch Your Ideas to Gogo" meeting (folds into [[Real Estate Teams]])
- A monthly team meeting on the calendar: every team member brings 1–3 ideas (one only if it's big). Gogo listens and approves or kills each.
- Why: she no longer logs into her tools (GoHighLevel, ManyChat, ClickFunnels), so she doesn't know what's new; "we all have blinders," and staff see what she can't; and it shows the team cares about the product. Spotting every broken site or automation herself had made her feel nobody cared.
- **Staff also audit what's already running,** because owners rarely check whether an idea worked. Finding: her whiteboard and numbers-explainer videos perform best, so the team handed her 10 whiteboard topics to film.
- Ideas raised: a Circle chatbot, Black Friday and birthday sales planned early, Pinterest for flyers and buyer/seller guides, a post orchestrator, a co-sponsor dashboard, Circle ads, downline success-story interviews.

### An AI agent for every role (folds into [[AI for Real Estate]])
- Every staff member and VA gets their own AI agent; she hired a recent GoGet'Em trainer to teach the whole team.
- **Job 1:** research that role's corner of the industry every night and deliver a morning report (social: new Instagram/Facebook/LinkedIn features and yesterday's viral content; tech: new GoHighLevel and ManyChat features).
- **Job 2:** do part of the job, aiming for "100 times more" output per role.
- **Job 3:** continuity: staff take days off and manage the bot from their phone, and a trained bot can teach a replacement after a promotion, departure or illness.
- **Post orchestrator:** she posts once on Instagram and it republishes to LinkedIn, Facebook and YouTube Shorts; next it covers all her brand accounts plus Pinterest, overseen by a social media manager.
- She mined her own 2018–2019 agent-attraction posts with Claude into a report for new post ideas, and uses the eXp MCP to find downline wins (doubled production, first-time ICON) to interview and cut into shorts for organic recruiting.

### Automated coaching funnel (folds into [[The Circle]])
- A chatbot plus second brain: a follower takes a 10-question bottleneck survey; the brain generates a blueprint-style report (modeled on the Circle clarity-call blueprint) and delivers only half. The full report requires a clarity call. Works for any coach or digital-product seller.
- Paid ads for the Circle will feed this funnel. Her only current ad spend: two $10/day ads the week before Open to the Public (~$140/month), purely for exposure.

### Co-sponsorship assets and booking pages (folds into [[Co-Sponsorship and Agent Attraction System]])
- A co-sponsor dashboard in development: site-building steps, marketing materials, a monthly promo calendar, social and email copy, and a traffic tracker (e.g. 100 visitors, 1 agent signed).
- Open to the Public and Why Not eXp sign-up links sit about 3/4 down every co-sponsorship site. Both run the last Wednesday of the month (1 PM and 8 PM), skipped in November and December.
- **Partner page:** one handoff straight to a calendar (no extra hops or forms). Shoot the website video horizontal; vertical cuts go to social.
- **Booking-page rules:** remove "X spots left" (it implies a group call, and agents won't show if others might learn they're thinking of switching). Add a strict description: for licensed agents who want to join eXp and name you as sponsor; not free coaching or brain-picking; only eXp is discussed; watch both videos (yours and Gogo's) first; no-shows aren't rescheduled.
- **Keyword DM posts:** CTA "Let's talk eXp," not "Let's talk"; #eXp in the first sentence; drop hashtags for audiences you don't want (e.g. #newagent); one keyword for the co-sponsorship page, another for your own downline page.
- Per Gogo, eXp agents can't run agent-attraction ads without a team, so she recruits organically only (her statement; confirm with eXp).

### Accountability, firing and replacing (folds into [[Real Estate Teams]])
- **Hire slow, fire fast.** One rotten team member spoils the batch; never tolerate "crazy."
- **Written consequences in the team contract:** two write-ups by email; the third means "you fired yourself," no conversation needed. "It's just like raising kids. If there is no consequences, they can just keep doing it."
- Team members reply to Gogo within 5 minutes during business hours; apply the same rule to leads.
- People without your drive won't match your speed; if they had it, they'd lead their own team.
- **Plan before the problem:** never give one person enough leverage to collapse your team status (e.g. a two-licensed-agent requirement). Line up the replacement first, and train them yourself: it's easier to teach than to unteach bad habits.
- **Reframe a release:** "graduate" them to a solo spot in your downline; they stay a business partner.
- Alternative: a 90-day trial requiring one transaction a month.
- **Stop paying another agent on your own properties:** license a family member (with a TC doing most of the work), keep the commission as a business expense and earn revenue share on it. A TC report of commissions paid out makes the case.

### Team profitability reality (folds into [[Real Estate Teams]] / [[Wealth Building]])
- Most mega/ICON teams aren't profitable; the best she knows nets ~13% (~$130K take-home on $1M GCI).
- A solo top producer matches that with ~10 transactions at a $13K average, with "zero headache," no liability and no one damaging your local reputation.
- Two very solid producers beat 30 agents who look good on paper ("30 headaches"). "We didn't work this hard to be this stressed."

### Money flow and entity structure (folds into [[Wealth Building]] / [[Tax Strategy]] / [[Legal Protection]])
- **Leverage ladder:** systems first (cheapest), then VAs, then employees. First hire an assistant; add a TC at a minimum of 24 transactions.
- **Where each dollar goes:** 50–60% to you, 15–25% to operations, 10–15% to marketing. Organic-only marketing zeroes that line and raises profit.
- **Pay yourself first** by automatic monthly transfer; leave at least $2,000 in the operating LLC and sweep the rest to the holding company. Structure: living trust → holding companies → operating LLCs.
- **Personal board of directors:** director of operations, CPA, financial adviser, tax attorney, asset planner and business adviser, meeting together quarterly.

### Low-ticket products as hand-raisers (folds into [[Social Media and Email Marketing]])
- Even when the goal is coaching, sell a low-ticket product: her $49 tracker bundle (lead, greatness, money and investment trackers) makes money and captures an email, so every buyer is a hand-raiser. Plan annual promos (Black Friday, birthday sale) early.

### Selling from stage (folds into [[Public Speaking and Stage Presentations]])
- Confirm the organizer allows selling; most don't, and if they do, sell.
- ~1 minute per slide (a 30-minute talk ≈ 30 slides).
- **Put the offer in the middle, not the end,** nonchalant ("if you want more, scan this"), and keep talking with it on screen ~30 seconds.
- Collect email or phone through a form and send the link (e.g. via GoHighLevel) instead of relying on a QR link that vanishes.
- Use a deadline (e.g. midnight tonight) when the price isn't available elsewhere.
- End with a free gift tied to a 7-day email follow-up that leads back to the offer; a 10% coupon on day 7 is the last touch.
