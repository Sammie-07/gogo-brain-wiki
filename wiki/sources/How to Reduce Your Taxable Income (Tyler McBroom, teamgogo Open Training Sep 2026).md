### Tax training (Tyler McBroom)
- Treat your accountant as a strategist, not a form-filler. Plan **during** the year (ideally Aug–Oct) and **before** you earn the income, buy or sell property, or start a company; after Dec 31 almost nothing can change.
- From his free reviews: 97% of clients overpaid or had the wrong structure; 76% paid roughly double. **Invest the savings** rather than inflate your lifestyle (one client investing ~$10K/month of savings projects to ~$34.9M by 70).
- **Step 1 — S-corp election:** pay a reasonable salary and take the rest as distributions to avoid self-employment tax on the distributions. Break-even ≈ **$60K net profit** (Gogo: about six transactions). An LLC can elect S-corp status, retroactive to Jan 1 if it existed all year.
- **Step 2 — Augusta Rule:** your company rents your home for up to **14 days** a year (e.g. a monthly board meeting) at a fair rate; the income is tax-free to you. Use either this or a home office, not both.
- **Step 3 — Hire your kids:** a parent-owned sole proprietorship can pay children under 18 without payroll tax, up to the standard deduction (**$16,100** in 2026) income-tax-free, for real work, on W-2, on a schedule. Gogo pays hers in 12 autopay installments.
- **Step 4 — Cost segregation + bonus depreciation + REPS:** a cost-seg study moves ~20–30% of a rental's value into year-1 deductions; with Real Estate Professional Status the losses can offset agent income. It's a deferral (recapture on sale), so the advice is never sell — borrow against the property. Don't hold buy-and-hold rentals in an S-corp.
- **Step 5 — "Freedom Fund" (advanced, high profit only):** a second S-corp with a Nov 30 fiscal year bills the main company for payroll and marketing, deferring income year to year. Tyler's proprietary strategy; see the caveats.
- **Vehicles over 6,000 lb GVWR**, >50% business use, placed in service by Dec 31: bonus depreciation on the business-use share. *"Don't even show me anything unless it weighs 6,000 pounds."* (Gogo)
- **Meals:** record restaurant, date, amount, who and why on the receipt and photograph it ([[Kristy Waker]]'s tip: iPhone Photos → Utilities → Receipts finds them automatically). Local meals must be with someone. **Home office:** a room used 100% for business, deducted by square-foot share. **Country-club dues and entertainment aren't deductible.**
- **Trusts** are for liability, not tax; viral trust schemes are either fraud or cost $100K+ and only make sense around $30M net worth.
- **Know your numbers** (Gogo counts "down to the penny") but hire out the data entry. Tax is on profit, not commissions.
