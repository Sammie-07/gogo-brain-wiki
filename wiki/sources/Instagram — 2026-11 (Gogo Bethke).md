## Key Takeaways

### Nov 1 — "Run the numbers" (Reel caption, [permalink](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfM1E7STV7y/))
- She does **2-10 calls a week** with agents where she runs their numbers: "I love math! I love numbers! Cause it allows us to make a bottom line decision and not a feelings decision."
- Her name for feelings decisions: "but my broker is so nice" and "I'm very loyal!!" (her reply: "I'm loyal to my FAMILY!"). "When we learn to make NUMBERS decisions, it's when we start functioning like a CEO!!"
- Year-end test for any agent doing **30+ transactions**: add up what you actually paid. At most brokerages that is a cap "usually in the $20K+, plus royalty fee, brokerage fee, marketing fee," which can reach **$50K-$65K+ a year**.
- Her eXp claim: the most you pay is $5K, and at year end the cap ($16K) is deposited into your retirement account as stock, so eXp "will pay you to work here (by earning your cap back with 30+ deals per year)."
- Call prep she asks for: current cap, your cut, transaction volume, transaction amount, where the deals come from, average % per deal. She then compares the agent's year as if done at eXp. CTA: comment or DM "partner."
- Bottom line: "if you are a #topproducer 30+ deals a year and you are still paying a cap you can't earn back you are at the wrong brokerage."
