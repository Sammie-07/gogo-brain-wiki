# Turning YouTube Viewers into Consultations (Alexis Diaz)

> A [[GoGet'Em Community]] (GGTC) **member-taught** training (~52 min) by [[Alexis Diaz]] — a Central Florida agent who has closed **163 transactions / $52M+ since 2023 (15,000+ subscribers), 100% from social**, all via YouTube. Where [[Jesse Dau]]'s [[Win with YouTube (Jesse Dau)|"Win with YouTube"]] covers the *algorithm and funnel*, Alexis covers the **trust → consultation → client → raving-fan** conversion: the video blueprint, community-building (weekly YouTube Live), authenticity, and the 30-day start plan. Raw transcript stored in Clippings.

## Who Alexis Diaz Is
- Central Florida (Orlando / Ocala) agent; team **Evolve Estates Group**; editing team in Colombia. Originally Staten Island, NY (half Puerto Rican / half Dominican), moved to FL ~13 years ago; **6 kids** (3 biological + an adopted sibling group of 3 via foster care); ex-Walt Disney World secretary who became an agent 7 years ago (COVID pivot). Started on a team cold-calling, hated it, learned YouTube from a class at her office and "took off running."
- **Results:** ~**163 transactions** since 2023, **23 under contract**, **$52M+ closed**, **15,000+ subscribers** — all from social; **YouTube for ~3.5 years.** "Virality was never the strategy — consistency was."
- Brand: **"the I got you girl."** Tagline "look no further, I got you"; the **cow-vs-buffalo storm** analogy ("I'm your buffalo — I run *toward* the storm so we get out faster"). A signature **"Monarch School of Real Estate"** end-of-video segment breaking down estimated down payment, closing costs, and builder incentives.

## Key Takeaways

### Trust is the strategy; views aren't the win
- People **don't hire you off one video** — they hire the agent they've been getting to know for months. YouTube lets them experience your expertise, personality, honesty, and consistency *before* the consultation; they **binge-watch**, follow you across platforms, and by the time they call they "already spent hours with you." **Become the choice before they're ready.** Consistency **compounds** — it means *dependable*, not daily.

### Know your avatar; serve, don't post
- Don't create for everyone ("speak to everyone, you speak to no one"). Pick who you want to work with (first-timers / relocation / investors / veterans / luxury / new-construction). Alexis's avatar = **relocation buyers**, especially New Yorkers moving to Central FL (shared language/culture; she avoids the hand-holding of first-timers).
- Ask deeper questions: **what life moment are they in, what do they want to feel, what are they afraid of getting wrong, what future are they creating.** Stop asking "what should I post," ask "**how can I serve**" — *content is the vehicle, service is the strategy; specific content creates specific leads.*
- **Answer the real question behind the search.** Relocation buyers want more than listings: car/home/health insurance comparisons, property taxes, HOA pros/cons, and Florida-specific **CDD fees** northerners have never heard of. "Give them more value — tell them what other agents leave out."

### Authenticity is the advantage
- **Be human and relatable** — she left a doorknob-falling-off blooper in a video ("don't we all have a few loose screws?"), calls a big pantry "Costco-mom approved." *"If I removed your name and logo, would people still know the video was yours?"*
- **Educate, don't sell.** "Your job is not to sell the house — it's to **educate the buyer and protect the decision**." Turn **features into benefits** ("3-car garage → fit both cars + bike + tools + boxes without paying for storage; how will this improve my life?"). **Have an opinion** — call out a bad floor plan or builder tile that stops short of the ceiling; *"neutral is safe, but neutral is forgettable — be memorable."*
- **New-construction guidance that builds trust:** which upgrades to do with the builder (structural / tray ceilings / lot location — can't change later) vs. cheaper aftermarket; the true cost beyond base price (lot premiums, design options, taxes, insurance, HOA, CDD); *always recommend a **third-party inspection** even on a brand-new home* (a CO-approved house was found with no attic insulation); and **"don't go to the community without me — the builder's sales agent works for the builder."**

### Build a community, not just an audience
- An audience watches; a **community participates.** Alexis runs a **YouTube Live every Sunday for 2 years** (30–60 people) — pushed past the "what ifs," asks **open-ended questions**, brings value guests (mortgage lender, credit specialist), and lets regulars talk to each other in the comments. Even non-buyers become referral sources.
- **Turn viewers into raving fans:** they refer *before you ask*, defend your reputation (fans answer nasty comments so you don't have to), and your name surfaces the moment a friend mentions moving. "Fans are created by consistent value." Keep in touch with past clients; start a referral program.
- **Trust is built between the videos** — cross-platform (Instagram for behind-the-scenes/stories), comment replies as two-way conversation, and community/"just sold" posts that **tell the story and the outcome** ("here's the closing costs I got my clients / what we went through / the result"), not a bare "just listed / just sold."

### The 5-step video blueprint (used for 3.5 years, ~150+ homes)
1. **Hook** — spark curiosity, speak to the avatar ("if you don't want another winter up north and you're ready for a brand-new home with no HOA — look no further, I got you").
2. **Intro** — who you are + who you help, *every* video (redundant, but there are new viewers each time).
3. **Tease value** — why keep watching ("stay to the end for Monarch School of Real Estate — down payment, closing costs, builder incentives").
4. **Story / tour** — storytelling that helps them feel, understand, and imagine (she uses **AI staging** to fill empty rooms — "picture a big sectional and a 100-inch TV here"). **What you *say* matters** — YouTube pushes your spoken words to the right people, so weave in relatable, avatar-aligned lines ("the kitchen is the heart of the home… I'm an island girl, born in Staten Island").
5. **Clear call to action** — make the next step obvious and **low-friction** (one link, one number, one way to respond; a contact box on screen; her preferred = **book directly in the calendar**). "Structure doesn't make you robotic — it keeps the viewer from getting lost."
- Add **social proof** (client video testimonials — "people want proof") and always say **how you're different** (past experiences/stories). Remember: enough business for everyone; not everyone will call; **not everyone is for you** — the right people call, and "that's not my girl" is fine.

### Run it like a business
- **Measure the business, not the views** — track views → conversations → consultations → clients → **closings**. Consultations on low views is still a win (views ≠ consultations).
- **A/B test one thing at a time** — what worked 3 years ago may not now (her thumbnails evolved colorful → amplified house photo → her face). Watch competitors and borrow what works, ego aside ("they probably copied someone too"); don't fix what's already working.
- **First 30 days:** *Wk 1* define direction (avatar / style / niche); *Wk 2* build a **content cushion of 4 finished videos before publishing #1** (YouTube rewards consistency; no instant gratification); *Wk 3* create the routine (**1 video/week, same day & time**, start minimal); *Wk 4* review & improve. Pick a content mix (home tours / market / lifestyle / educational / luxury / affordable / land) and combine at most a couple — she settled on home tours + education, 2×/week (Mon & Tue, 8pm).
- **Q&A nuggets:** honesty doesn't scare buyers or invite lowballs (she won't showcase builders she dislikes, won't trash them either, and frames cons with pros — "15 min from the store now, but development drives prices up"); an unfocused channel (mortgage + podcast + tours) should **niche down**, and since YouTube favors new channels, starting fresh with a crystal-clear focus is fine (she relaunched Veronica Figueroa's team channel new → 1,000+ subs, consistent lead flow); use **Claude / ChatGPT to craft hooks for your avatar**; taglines emerge naturally from how you already talk.
