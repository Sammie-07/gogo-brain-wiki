### Carrie Soave — the AI-run company (main event)
- **Run your business as a company.** She frames **7 income-producing departments** (Market Intelligence, Marketing & Visibility, Database & Relationships, Lead Conversion, Sales, Transaction Delivery, Client Expansion) + **4 capacity-protecting departments** (Operations, Finance, HR, Management). Her own company runs 9 departments with 3 (now 4) humans at ~$250K/month, and won't add humans until $500K/month.
- **The gap is implementation, not adoption:** *"82% of agents now use AI, [only] 17% say it made an impact… we don't have an adoption problem, we have an implementation problem."*
- **Don't build on rented land.** The model layer is volatile (models get banned, deprecated, changed). Your systems, context, data, and guardrails must live **outside any single model** — on your desktop + personal cloud/Drive — so you can swap models without downtime. *"Your AI systems must live outside of Claude or ChatGPT."*
- **The 5-step "build your AI brain / personal wiki" exercise** (see quote below for the master prompt): (1) have Claude/ChatGPT interview you about your whole business and generate a comprehensive personal wiki; (2) save every file to desktop **and** cloud (your "digital brain"); (3) connect all business tools via Customize/plugins (context); (4) open it as a Cowork/work **project**, attach the wiki, and set it to auto-check your apps and **update the wiki on the last day of each month** (your first autonomous workflow); (5) bonus — ask for the top 5 highest-impact autonomous workflows to build next. *(Directly parallels this Obsidian brain.)*
- **Program's 4 phases for commission agents:** (1) CRM management, speed-to-lead, pipeline reactivation, better drips/funnels; (2) marketing & socials (website AEO/GEO/SEO, funnels, ads) + AI twins (HeyGen + Higgsfield on autopilot); (3) client-experience workflows for buyers/sellers; (4) operations dashboard (transactions, commissions, due dates, expenses). 30+ workflows with ready system prompts.
- **Content workflow (as a model):** Friday = deep per-platform competitor/trend research + pull her own AI/meeting transcripts ("good content is real content") → an ideation plan waits in Drive for Monday human approval; Monday = generate the week (5 talking-head scripts, 5 carousels, 5 static posts, 5 AI-twin videos, 3 long-form LinkedIn, ~15 tweets), filed to per-category Docs + linked to Asana tasks with owners/deadlines; Thursday = analytics. A **human approval gate** is mandatory.
- **Client experience is the moat:** *"the only way to build a strong brand with longevity is to massively elevate your client experience"* — and AI is how you deliver it at scale. **Speed-to-lead** (<5 min): almost no hands went up; AI voice callers/bots solve it.
- **Cost framing:** ~$30K/month human payroll ≈ $300–$1,000/month in tech. Live **"Atlas"** demo: her AI CEO queried her ops lead's Asana in real time (86 open / 57 done in 2 weeks) and diagnosed a *capacity-design* problem, not laziness; her agents have live feeds to GoHighLevel, trackers, Thrivecart, Stripe, banking, Zoom/Fathom, and a dialer.
