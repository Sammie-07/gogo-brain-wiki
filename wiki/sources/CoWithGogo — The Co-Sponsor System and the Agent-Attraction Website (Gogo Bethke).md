# CoWithGogo — The Co-Sponsor System and the Agent-Attraction Website (Gogo Bethke)

> A "CoWithGogo" event (Sep 14, 2026) where Gogo taught her **co-sponsor / agent-attraction system** end to end — the eXp sponsor/co-sponsor mechanics, the FLA/FLQA revenue-share math, and the AI-built website funnel. Hosted with [[Kristy Waker]], Ferny, and [[Rafael Mino]]. High-reference source.

## Key Takeaways

### Why co-sponsor
- eXp lets an incoming agent name two uplines (primary **sponsor** + **co-sponsor**). Co-sponsorship lets any agent plug into Team Gogo's ready-built machine. It's for **"orphan" agents**: no upline, upline left, upline does no trainings/masterminds/events/community, or joined so long ago only Glenn Sanford is above them.
- **"Platform vs multiplier":** eXp is the platform (same benefits everywhere); [[#teamgogo]] is the multiplier (the family/benefits layer).
- Naming philosophy reiterated: **"Agents on the GOGO," not "Gogo's agents"** — Team Gogo International Family of Agents.

### The sponsor/co-sponsor mechanics (precise)
- Structure: **Sponsor → Co-sponsor → oncoming agent.** The oncoming agent gets access to the **sponsor's** upline (not the co-sponsor's) — so to give a recruit Team Gogo benefits, **Gogo must be primary sponsor**.
- **Both sponsor and co-sponsor get full revenue share** on the recruit; the recruit lands in both downlines. Position doesn't change rev share.
- Difference: **sponsor gets the FLQA; co-sponsor gets a $4,000 cap bonus** (agent caps year 1). Gogo *prefers CO* — she + husband hold ~70 FLQAs (most in the company), so she'd rather have the bonus.
- **One-time access rule:** to get access for *yourself*, make Gogo primary once (you go CO under her) → both get access; you give up that one FLQA but get the bonus. After that, Gogo goes CO and you keep your FLQAs. Only want the *recruit* to have access? Gogo goes CO every time.
- Already in Gogo's downline → no co-sponsorship needed (sponsoring auto-gives benefits). You can't co-sponsor with anyone in your own downline. Nobody changes their existing sponsor.

### FLA / FLQA / revenue-share levels
- **FLA** = Frontline Active (joined, paying $85/mo). **FLQA** = Frontline *Qualifying* Active = closed & collected **≥ $5,000 commission** (holds 6 months).
- **Every 5 FLQAs opens the next level** (5→L2, 10→L3, 15→L4, 20→L5…); **30 FLQAs opens all 7 levels** (retirement goal).
- Current bonus: **L1–3 open for everyone now** (remove it → need 15 for L3). **Capping** agent → L1–5 open. **ICON** → all 7 open.

### The website funnel
- **jointeamgogo.com/create** → two paths (Team Gogo partner page, must use eXp email / Not-Team-Gogo co-sponsor page). AI builds a live site in seconds; men blue, women pink; URL **jointeamgogo.com/[name]** (the attribution slug).
- Page: your intro video → Gogo's video → "Let's Talk eXp" Calendly → 3-way call button (Gogo closes) → Step 1 & 2 apply (Team Gogo app, no cut ever + eXp app). Built-in Open-to-Public + Why Not eXp, always current. Edit at `.../[name]/info`; co-sponsor WhatsApp chat for support (business hours).
- **Monthly loop:** Open-to-Public training (last Wed, 1pm ET — bait; post about it but make people reach out to you, don't give the link) → follow up → Why Not eXp (same night, 8pm ET; Gogo presents/runs numbers/handles objections; end slide = "reach out to whoever invited you") → prospect returns → you close (or 3-way call) → send to Step 1 & 2. **Gogo never follows up — the inviting agent must.** 3-way call rule: watch videos → book → decide.
