# The Circle Coaching — Keyword Automation, Scarcity Positioning & Box-in-a-Box Structure (de-identified)

## Key Takeaways

### Lead-capture automation
- **ManyChat keyword discipline:** set generic keywords (buyer/seller) to fire on **every post** and in **both DMs and comments** — not scoped to one post. Name property-specific keywords by the **address/street/feature**, never a reusable "tour," so a forgotten link-swap doesn't send people to a stale listing. DMs matter because buyers want privacy (they won't publicly comment "buyer"); this is why [[ManyChat]] (both DM + comment) beats [[GoHighLevel]]'s built-in (DM *or* comment only) — "that's half my business."
- **"Eat the process for breakfast":** test every funnel end-to-end as the client — comment the keyword → get the message → click the link → land on the calendar → book → confirm the confirmation + reminders fire *and* it lands on your calendar. Homework isn't done until the whole chain works; the last step is where the money is made (the client actually shows up).
- **Tag everything:** tag contacts by source (open house), intent (buyer/seller), location/city, year, and specific property. Proper tags are where everything starts — they let you segment sends (invite all buyers to a Santa photo shoot; email everyone tagged to a location when local news breaks).

### Content & nurture
- **Evergreen vs. disappearing content:** active-listing videos vanish; build **evergreen** buyer material from your niche (relocating-to-town basics, new-construction-for-relocation, specific-neighborhood guides). Generate it by feeding your niche into your **second brain / [[Claude]]** ("it knows you"). Park evergreen pieces in **Instagram Highlights + pinned posts** (Instagram Plus ~$3.99/mo → up to 6 pins) and **run $20/day ads** to them — they work because they're evergreen and not someone else's listing (no permission needed).
- **Give-first local-news email:** send tagged open-house/buyer lists location-specific value ("the new hospital created 400 jobs — thought of you since you're looking here") layered on top of the generic every-other-week buying-process drip. Give, give, give with a soft consult CTA — no hard sell.

### AI usage & compliance
- **Local-news AI agent:** build an agent (in [[Claude]]) that scans your farm town's news each morning (layoffs, new hires, new restaurants/schools/hospitals, a big employer moving in) and reports to you; you decide what becomes a post or email.
- **Privacy compliance:** licensed realtors must **never upload client PII to free/general AI** — it feeds a shared brain, effectively a breach and a brokerage-policy violation. Use **paid, private AI only.**
- **AI email assistants need oversight:** they mis-file, mark things spam, or leave replies in draft you never see. Budget ~1.5–2 months to get a rhythm before trusting the flow.

### Positioning — automation ≠ impersonal
- **Unavailability signals status.** The objection "I want it personal, they can just DM me" misreads today's buyer, who wants a professional who runs their business *like* a business (the hair-appointment analogy: you want the 2:30 slot, not to chit-chat). The more instantly available you are, the more it signals you're broke with nothing but time; a real booking process makes you look legit and lets tire-kickers weed themselves out. Gogo's ~99% agent-attraction close rate rides this: prospects watch ~9 videos, then book ~2.5 weeks out — by the time they arrive they've convinced themselves. **"Build for big, don't build for small."** Every minute given to an unqualified stranger online is taken from your family.
- **Cost vs. ROI reframe:** most people ask "how much does this cost?"; few ask "**how much money will this make me?**" One tool at ~$29/mo against a single average commission pays for itself for *years* — evaluate every business investment that way.

### Team & recruiting
- **In-person agent attraction:** if online attraction feels awkward, lean into your style — attend CE classes with your name tag, talk to everyone, stop gatekeeping knowledge, collect cards; join the local board / county real-estate Facebook groups and attend their coffee/happy-hour events. **Vet prospects by production:** look up a prospect's **24-month** production (Home Snap / MLS tools, same-state only) before investing time — a high producer won't join, a 1–2-deal agent you can genuinely help. (It's a 2-year figure, so divide.)
- **Hybrid VA meeting tech (no echo):** only **one device** logs into Zoom and is projected to the TV; in-person people watch the TV (not logged in, or logged in with mic + sound **off**); overseas VAs join from their own computers and hear via the host mic; anyone can screen-share.
- **Team goal-setting:** interview other teams for their cadence; use a KW-style **"411"** goal doc; have agents **plan their own days** (more ownership/control); pair with the **Greatness Tracker** and reverse to "how many more closings to hit the income goal."

### Calendar hygiene
- **24–48-hour minimum booking buffer** so no one grabs your calendar same-day while you're out showing / with no signal.
- **Consults ≥30 minutes** (15 is too short to introduce yourself, understand the client, and pull the MLS).
- Watch for a **synced all-day "busy" event** silently blocking every open slot.

### Asset protection (box-in-a-box)
- **LLC per property named by the address** → group by state/region into a named **holding company** → holding owned by the **family living trust**; you personally own nothing (trustee while alive, kids become trustees at death). Each **income stream** (real estate, digital product, coaching) gets its own LLC → holding.
- **Sweep-above-minimum cash method:** keep only the bank's no-fee minimum in each property account (e.g. ~$2,000) and **auto-sweep everything above it to the holding** — a suit against one property LLC can only reach that ~$2K, not the swept assets.
- **Per-entity debit/credit card + checking account**; in-house **pre-bookkeeping → master spreadsheet → CPA**. Ask your CPA whether to run books at the **LLC level (safer — Gogo's preference)** or holding level (holding-level exposes it all if sued). Get a **"blueprint"** from your formation attorney (Anderson Advisors). "It's a hassle to set up, easy to maintain, it's a tax write-off, and you sleep good at night." *(Disclaimer: "I'm no financial advisor.")*
