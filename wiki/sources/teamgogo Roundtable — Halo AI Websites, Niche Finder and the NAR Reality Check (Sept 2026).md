## Key Takeaways

### Jason Dupree mortgage minute (Sep 16)
- Source MBA weekly data: **rates 6.97% and rising** (Fed expected to move a quarter point that day). **Purchases down ~19% YoY; refinances down ~65% YoY.** **HELOCs up sharply** — the only product rising (people pulling equity to pay down credit cards). Not expected to dent current owners' home values.
- **MBS Highway** — free to all Team Gogo REMLOs: **List Reports** (market to your past-client database) plus a new **gamified buyer-prep tool** — budgets, down-payment/closing savings plans, area research, a **5-year net-worth plan**; keeps buyers engaged 1 month–3 years out, alerts the agent when a client saves/views a home, surfaces refi opportunities, weekly engagement report. Guest link shared for any Gogo agent/coaching client (REMLO or not).
- **REMLO recap:** dual license (buyer's agent + lender), lender attached "by the hip." Pay **0.5% of the mortgage** (~$2,000 on a $400K loan). **Paid on HELOCs; NOT on USDA** purchases — but those clients drop into your **refinance bucket** so you're paid on the eventual refi.

### Halo (Nick Krem) — deepens [[Halo]]
- Run entirely by **texting** ("51–52 AIs in one"). **Free trial: text "Gogo" to 415-726-3224** (or QR); free site in <5–10 min with **5 edits**. **$99/mo → 2 full sites, 1,000 pages each**, hosted, free `*.whosey.sites` subdomain (custom domain recommended for SEO/GEO).
- **AEO site checker:** brokerage / **Bold Trail (KVCore)** sites score an **F — they block AI crawlers**, making agents invisible to AI search.
- **Case study [[Melissa Albert]]** ("Living in Kentucky," Bowling Green): built a **102-page** relocation site in **<2 weeks**, scored **99/100**. Auto-blogs 3×/week; text a YouTube link → adds videos; pulls IG reels; a **"Living Local"** automation ("every Wednesday pull the top local events and update") made her the local go-to in Facebook groups; asked Halo to **audit the whole site for federal fair-housing/anti-steering** and it rewrote copy + added statements/logos site-wide.
- **Integrations:** webhook to any CRM + **direct GoHighLevel sync** — keep [[GoHighLevel]] as the backend (invoicing, e-sign, funnels, nurture); Halo captures, GHL nurtures.
- **Ninja tactic:** take a stranger's question in a Facebook group → text it to Halo → it writes a full blog/page → post the answer → become the authority. Also: feed Halo a competitor URL to clone structure.
- **Roadmap:** Founders-member cutoff **by October**; a **worldwide referral network** (text "I need someone in [area]") — Gogo requested a Team-Gogo-branded pool ("we feed each other's families first"); an **AI training library** (ask an AI over every training video).

### The NAR production reality (Gogo) — deepens [[Real Estate Math]]
- NAR realtor production: **2024 — 71% sold zero homes; 2025 — 71.1%; 2026 — 84% sold zero.** → **Selling ONE home = top 16% nationally.**
- Existing-home sales ~flat: **~4.06M (2024) · ~4.06M (2025) · ~3.98M YTD (2026).** Thesis: **"we're selling the same number of homes ±10% every year — the question is who's selling it."** Market share consolidates to teams, top producers, Zillow-lead and **locally-branded "household name" agents.** "Instead of 100 agents selling one home, one agent sells 100."
- **Solo-agent percentile ladder:** 12/yr = top 2%; 20–29/yr = 0.81%; 30–50/yr = 0.45%; 52–100/yr = 0.31%; 100–300/yr solo = 0.11%.

### Niche Finder + the Second Brain (Gogo) — deepens [[Niche Finder]] and [[AI for Real Estate]]
- **Niche Finder** = a $24 questionnaire tool running on Gogo's **Second Brain** (everything she's produced — videos/trainings/bootcamps/blogs/posts, transcribed — loaded into **Obsidian** as a knowledge graph; clusters into buckets like Personal Branding and Social Media). Purpose: scale her expertise; coaching clients can "chat with her" any time via a dashboard.
- **Output pipeline:** query the brain → identify your niche → optimized **social bios (3 options)** → **90-day plan in three 4-week phases (Foundation → Momentum → Scaling)** → SEO keywords (paste into Halo) + internal links + **12-week content calendar** + **6-month tracker**.
- **Fill-it guidance:** be brutally specific — lead sources, price range, buyer type (veteran/first-time/downsizer/lake), plus personal backstory (origin, languages, faith, spouse, kids, hobbies, prior career) so it clusters like-minded people. **Run it separately for production vs Agent Attraction** (two client types → two plans); niches change with life phase.
- **Live example (Melissa Johnson, Winter Park FL):** niche = "Winter Park Downsize Specialist" (empty-nester/former-teacher, serves first-time buyers + downsizers $600K–$1.5M); 8 marketing ideas incl. dog-walk farm (25 handwritten cards + QR), horse-barn flyers, teacher-network seminar, estate-attorney/financial-planner referral coffees, SEO blog bank. (Note: distinct from Halo user Melissa Albert.)
