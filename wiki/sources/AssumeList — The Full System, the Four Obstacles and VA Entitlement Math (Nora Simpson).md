# AssumeList — The Full System, the Four Obstacles and VA Entitlement Math (Nora Simpson)

## Key Takeaways

### Nora Simpson & AssumeList (the deep-dive)
- **AssumeList is a full system, not just a search tool:** (1) US-wide property search, (2) client/buyer sub-accounts, (3) a Learning Center, (4) a **transaction-management team**, (5) a **mail-campaign tool**. The search "is a really nice part, but not even close to the majority of the value."
- **Coverage:** **57 MLSs across 27 states** (Northwest MLS / WA took over a year — MLS territorialism + compliance). Not New York, not Alaska. **Off-market property data added monthly** for new counties — email the counties you want to nora@assumelist.com (she'll pick someone else's county if you don't ask).
- **eXp enterprise license:** [[eXp Realty|Leo Pareja]] pays for every eXp agent's free account. **983 eXp agents have signed up; "we're going to run out of seats soon."** Register at AssumeList.com/eXp → "I already have a code" → daily-expiring code + eXp email + primary business email. The code expires by design (negotiated with eXp leadership) so agents **"pay with attention"** by attending a Q&A rather than getting a dead code they never use.
- **Ground truth (vs. misinformation):** *All VA and FHA mortgages are federally required to be assumable at the seller's request, regardless of servicer, if the buyer is creditworthy. Full stop.*

**The four obstacles to assumptions (and how AssumeList solves each):**
1. **Hard to find them** — owners don't know they have an assumable loan; realtors don't know to ask or which loans qualify. → search platform + monthly off-market data.
2. **Marketplace misconceptions** — **banks fund misinformation** (thousands of paid blogs with confusing/conflicting info) because when a low-rate loan is paid off they re-lend at 6.7–6.9%. Google/Gemini/ChatGPT return half-truths. → the Learning Center gives regulation-based ground truth.
3. **Bank obstruction** — servicers give the runaround ("Rocket Mortgage doesn't do assumptions" — said by a human rep and an AI, which is illegal). Banks use **manual underwriting** (not the automated approval they'd use for a new 6.7% loan) to hunt for a reason to decline, and sometimes do the DTI math wrong. → AssumeList pushes back, corrects the math, and escalates to the **VA Inspector General** (regulatory/executive branch, not courts). In one case the VA IG threatened to revoke a bank's license to service **$1T+** of VA debt; the SVP called and now that bank closes assumptions in **≤25 days**.
4. **Cash-gap financing** — the gap between the assumable loan balance and the purchase price.

- **Transaction management:** shepherds agent + buyer + seller; **never a turndown, never longer than 60 days.** **$2,300 flat fee** — **$200 upfront** at engagement (only after a pre-approval, so no wasted money), **$2,100 at clear-to-close**; not part of closing costs. Cheaper than competitors' 1% of price on any home above $230K.
- **Assumables are for financially-qualified people to SAVE money**, not a no-down path for the broke. Target the **73% of homeowners who would move today if they could keep their rate** (2026 Yahoo Finance survey); **80% of homeowners have a sub-6% rate.** **99% of realtors have never closed an assumption** — so a day-one agent immediately knows more than 99% of their market. "Burning platform": every month the seller pays the mortgage, the **cash gap grows** (example: $129K this month → $132K next).
- **Cash-gap example (Houston, VA 2.65%, $129K gap):** 10% down → save **>$500/mo**, **blended rate ~4.2%** vs. 6.3–6.7% market. Strategy = **primary + secondary**: buyer gets pre-approved for a normal loan (conventional/VA/FHA) at today's rate to cover the gap. Preferred lenders **Ryan & Kelly at Barrett Financial** (Arizona; licensed 49 states, not NY). Nora has interviewed 150+ lenders and fired 7.

**VA entitlement depth (mostly unknown even to vets):**
- Non-VA buyers **and non-VA investors** can assume a VA loan.
- **Entitlement can be split** — leaving entitlement behind on one property doesn't burn all of it.
- Entitlement tied up = the **original loan amount** (or refinance amount), NOT the remaining balance — both are public info. AssumeList has a **VA entitlement calculator on every VA property** (unique in the industry).
- **2nd-time VA-use funding fee:** 3.3% (zero down) → **1.5% at 5% down** → **1.25% at 10% down**.
- **The 4× rule:** the VA guarantees the bank 25% of a VA loan, so a vet buying above their remaining entitlement can **self-guarantee with cash** = 25% of the gap. Putting $99K down can unlock **~$396K additional buying power** (a ~$808K VA purchase). County loan limits vary (all TX ≈ $832K in 2026; CA/VA/NJ/NY higher).
- **Investor angle is VA-only:** when the vet leaves entitlement behind, the 12-month owner-occupancy is already satisfied; an **FHA** assumption **restarts** the 12-month owner-occupancy, which investors won't do.

- **Mail-campaign tool:** filter (rate, property type) → select VA/FHA → generate **handwritten, hand-addressed, real-stamp "golden letters"** (best practices baked in from Nora's 18 years) with templates; **skip-trace owner contact info** (email, mobile, landline, Do-Not-Call flag); custom-polygon map search; investor buyer-subaccounts can't see off-market data.
- **Referral directory:** agents nationwide list a coverage area; Nora personally routes referrals ("who's your favorite agent in X county?"); **AssumeList takes no fee** on agent-to-agent referrals.
- **Marketing coaching:** reach out to everyone you know (they know people you don't); Nora will **co-host an assumable webinar** for an audience the agent gathers; get buyers pre-approved (soft pull). Nora leads a **Q&A 5 days/week, 11am ET** (homes.assumelist.com/learn) — bring buyers/sellers; early arrivals prioritized.

### Jason Dupree mortgage minute (Sep 9)
