# #teamgogo Training — Melissa Albert Builds a Seller Lead Website by Text with Halo (Nov 2026)

> Weekly [[#teamgogo]] Wednesday training (Nov 11, 2026, ~73 min; posted to YouTube Nov 12). [[Melissa Albert]] (Living in Kentucky Team, Bowling Green) built a seller lead website live by texting [[Halo]]; [[Nick Krem]] covered the tech; [[Jason Dupree]] gave the mortgage minute. [[Gogo Bethke]] opened with housekeeping and added the delegation, review and lead-capture rules. Halo figures are Halo's own.

## Key Takeaways

### Housekeeping (folds into [[#teamgogo]])
- Every agent needs the eXp Hub app: it is now the only way eXp communicates. Don't email brokers; "by the time they get to the emails, weeks."
- Check the #teamgogo map periodically (name, email, phone, every licensed state). Be in the new Team Gogo main chat: Gogo couldn't tag anyone for a Michigan open house on a $668K listing, "an $18,000 commission I'm trying to give you."
- Onboard every new agent through the onboarding call, or they miss the Team Gogo, Honey Badgers and Fast Forward Movement benefits.
- Wednesdays: 1 PM training, 3 PM tech call to set up what was taught. No more Why Not eXp or Open to the Public until January.
- Personal: she couldn't ship hand-me-downs home to Romania from Tampa, so she asked the shipping company to open a Tampa location, found a bilingual woman at church and started the business for her. "If it doesn't exist, you create it." She is its first customer.

### Mortgage minute ([[Jason Dupree]], his figures)
- With no year-over-year CPI comparison (last year's shutdown), he expected rates to keep rising and mentioned talk of a price correction of up to 20% next year. Motivate sellers now; stay close to buyers who wait. Temporary rate buydowns are "the magic phrase" into 2027; a new REMLO program for listing-heavy agents is coming.

### AI as a referral network (folds into [[AI for Real Estate]])
- [[Nick Krem]]'s stats: 43% of people say AI understands them; 39% say it understands them better than most people; 74% would trust AI more than their best friend on a buying decision. "This is not an online lead. This is a referral."
- Melissa: about a third of consumers now start their search on AI, and ~70% of Google searches end without a click. ChatGPT now has free business profiles; get verified.
- Nick's next wave: physical AI and always-connected agents (he named Grok bots) doing tasks for consumers, so the AI filter decides who gets recommended.

### Building by text (folds into [[Halo]] / [[Personal Branding]])
- Melissa texted Halo for "the ultimate prompt," answered its questions (style, brand), and had a full seller site built and hosted in under an hour, scoring **95 on an AEO site checker out of the box**. She fed it a PDF export of her old Canva site (Halo couldn't read Canva directly) and PDFs of notable sales, and edited by talk-to-text ("give me five headline options").
- At UPW, after a deal fell apart, she built a single-property site for an 18-unit mobile home park in about 30 minutes (answering investors' questions, rent upside vs MLS medians, due-diligence package, kept off her homepage so tenants wouldn't see it) and had two leads that day.
- Her listing strategy: "each listing is its own brand launch," speaking to the buyer's pain point. Builder gave her a flagship home because of her marketing.
- **Listing sites are an SEO/AEO/GEO goldmine (Nick):** about half of Halo's top-traffic sites are listing sites. $99 one-time for life, unlimited edits, still blogs three times a week; turn it into a just-sold and it becomes a neighborhood SEO portfolio. Text Halo your Zillow or Realtor.com link to turn listing photos into video. Main sites are $99/month for two sites.
- Halo vs GoHighLevel: **"You still need both."** Halo builds the site and captures leads (it has no CRM); a GoHighLevel Lead Sync in Halo's Marketplace sends forms into GHL for follow-up. Halo rebuilt one agent's GHL site from its sitemap and fixed ~125 bugs.

### Gogo's rules (folds into [[Real Estate Teams]] / [[Lead Generation]])
- **Your VA still sets it up.** "I'm not going to be sitting there and setting up the website. It is not my time best spent." She has never logged into GoHighLevel, her Grok bot or SkySlope: "you do not make money there." VAs learn by asking AI for step-by-step instructions and bring questions to the 3 PM Wednesday #teamgogo tech call.
- **Review what auto-posts.** The VA sends her the link to every auto-blog so she can skim it: "It takes you 15 years to build something... a VA can post something that can ruin your reputation in three seconds."
- **Never give guides away.** "Why would you ever give them anything for free?" Gate every guide behind name, email and phone (Halo can build the capture form and redirect), because that's how they raise their hand.
- **Know your ideal client so the blogs use their words.** Area, price, life stage, acreage, lake, schools: the exact words they search are what get you found. "If you're talking to everybody, you're talking to nobody." Early on she worked with "anybody for a commission," which meant nobody chose her as the expert. Your listing-side and buyer-side ideal clients can differ; Melissa keeps a separate site for each.
- Gogo glossed AEO as "AI engine optimization" and GEO as "Google engine optimization"; GEO is usually defined as generative engine optimization.
