# Halo — The AI Website Agency You Run by Text (Nick Krem and Eric Post)

> A live #teamgogo / [[GoGet'Em Community]] workshop (Sept 2, 2026) where guest speakers [[Nick Krem]] (AI strategist) and [[Eric Post]] (founder/architect of [[Halo]]) demo an AI "marketing agency" you operate entirely by **text message** — building an AEO/GEO/SEO-optimized real estate website live for team member [[Melissa Albert]] in ~10 minutes. This is the fully-demoed execution layer under the brain's [[AI for Real Estate|AEO/GEO thesis]] — the "text your AI web developer, ~$99/mo" tool [[Kirby Chan]] referenced.

## Key Takeaways

- **Halo = an AI marketing agency run by text, not a chatbot.** One chat assistant (your "AI chief marketing officer") commands 30+ "AI employees" — web designer, video production, graphic designer, copywriter, researcher, SEO/AEO/GEO strategists, marketing coordinator. It used **51 different AIs in the last 30 days** and picks the best model per task, so you need no APIs or separate subscriptions.
- **You delegate, you don't operate.** Text it a request ("here's my new listing, here's the address"; "translate my whole site to Spanish"; "turn this selfie into a professional headshot") and it does the work and reports back. Persistent memory builds your profile over time; your IP is not used to train models.
- **Three site types: niche, authority, property** (landing pages, event sites, recruiting/downline sites coming). Strategy = the **hub-and-spoke authority graph**: `yourname.com` is the hub; niche + property + agent-attraction pages are spokes, all cross-linked, which is what makes AI recognize you as the local authority and recommend you.
- **Everything is optimized for AI search (AEO/GEO/SEO) from creation** — "built by AI for AI." Handles `llms.txt`, JSON, schema so you don't have to. **Most brokerage/CRM sites actively block AI crawlers** and are "built for yesterday," so agents blogging there may never be crawled.
- **14 compliance "gates" before anything publishes** — fair-housing auditor, fair-lending auditor, compliance/broken-link/redirect checks — so output is "a thoughtful generation, not AI slop." First-generation images are **AI-generated (copyright-safe)**; you can swap in your own photos/video by texting them.
- **Pricing:** **$99/mo** = 2 full sites, **1,000 pages** across them, unlimited edits, unlimited automations, nationwide referral network (coming). **Free trial via Gogo:** a live hosted site + **5 edits** (text the word "Gogo" to the number / scan the QR).
- **Content pillars (Eric's reframe):** (1) friction points / **life triggers** (job, baby, downsize), (2) the **education** they need as a result, (3) **why choose you.** "Riches are in the niches" — now more true than ever because there's ~**15,000× more content online** than 18 months ago.
- **Nick's Agent-Attraction "ninja tactic":** text Halo daily to research the #1 question a producing agent asks when considering a brokerage change; you text back your answer; Halo writes a niche blog answering it → you become the cited source. One user got **two appointments on day one.**
- **Jason Dupree's Sept 2 mortgage minute:** non-QM is the fastest-growing mortgage segment ($108B last year → **$175B expected, +62%**); DSCR / bank-statement / renovation-HELOC products; a 3-step investor-onboarding play (LLC → LLC bank account → HELOC on primary as down payment). REMLO recap: full-service agent, licensed lender in all 50 states, **~$1,800–$2,200 extra per file.**

## The Halo Product (Eric Post / Nick Krem)

- **Run by text.** ~300–400K text messages in/out with users over 3 months; ~**92 incoming texts per user**. You can also work from a mirrored web dashboard (same functionality). "Idea at 2AM → text it → done by the time you wake up."
- **Automations (unlimited):** e.g., "refresh my site at the start of every month (new hero, seasonal welcome)"; "blog 3× a week, AEO-optimized"; "every Friday update all my market stats and cite sources." It proposes automations and asks yes/no, or you delegate them proactively.
- **Marketplace (push-button add-ons):** lead quiz (300+ built by users; among the best converters), scroll/hover animations, hero video, CRM webhook, **GoHighLevel**, Zapier, Google Analytics. Controllable by text ("add beautiful animations to my site").
- **Does NOT auto-post to social** — social platforms penalize syndicated content, and third-party auto-posters "get zilch results." Halo makes the **asset**; you copy-paste (the "waterfall" removes the friction of *what to make*, not the posting). Gogo's workaround: add a VA as an Instagram additional user (no password shared) to post the Halo-made content from within the app.
- **Also a coding agent + translator:** rebuilds a site from a pasted sitemap; connect Google Search Console, paste the errors, and it fixes them; one-toggle full-site translation (Spanish/Russian/Dutch…) for underserved-language niches. 21 built-in style systems; accessibility-ready; automatically mobile-optimized.
- **Roadmap (shown as live, not vaporware):** in-app **community**, a **nationwide referral network** ("is there an agent in [city]?"), and an **AI training library** that reads every training video, knows your niche/market, and coaches you on demand.

## The AI-Powered Consumer (the "why now")

- **LendingTree (via Jason):** 72% of consumers would use AI for at least one part of buying/selling; **37% would let AI manage the entire home purchase** with minimal human involvement.
- **Nick's stats:** 37% of the world starts searches with an AI tool; **57.4% of all internet traffic is now AI bots/agents** (humans are the minority of web crawling); **82% of Americans used AI for housing information last year**; ChatGPT crossed **1B weekly active users** (Eric).
- **The real disruptor is the AI-powered consumer** in a knowledge-based service industry — not "Zillow with AI." "We have to skate to where the puck is going." (Eric Post)
- **Why AI gets math wrong:** LLMs are "built to produce plausible language, not fundamental truth" — they give the *illusion* of doing math. Jason's honors-math example: ChatGPT got his son's problem wrong twice. Lesson to share with clients: AI + human trust/ease beats AI alone.

## Jason Dupree — Sept 2 Mortgage Minute

- Jobs report "blah"; rates steady ~2 weeks; no new Middle East escalation — holding pattern.
- **Non-QM is the fastest-growing segment:** $108B (last year) → **$175B expected** this year (**+62%**). Non-QM = non-qualified mortgages: self-employed / bank-statement / P&L loans, **DSCR** (the big investor product), and **renovation HELOCs** (borrow against post-reno future value).
- **DSCR rates can beat Fannie/Freddie on investment properties** because the agencies pour resources into owner-occupied loans; DSCR has gone from a "weird product" to the norm. Focus on **investors** — they buy on good deals regardless of market.
- **Trust + Ease** are what the AI-powered consumer wants. On social: **break the fourth wall** — not polished, "never a news desk"; walking/parked-car videos feel authentic. (Riff on Gogo skipping her blowout that day.)
- **3-step investor onboarding** (eases a homeowner into becoming an investor without scaring them): (1) start an **LLC** (recommends **Wyoming** — cheap, Delaware-like protections); (2) open a **bank account in the LLC's name** (avoid commingling); (3) start a **HELOC on the primary residence** as down payment (closes in as little as 5 days, but wait to close until under contract so no interest accrues). **DSCR allows closing in the LLC.**
- **REMLO recap:** become a full-service agent + licensed lender in all 50 states; **~0.5% of the loan** (~$500 per $100K; #teamgogo REMLOs average **$1,800–$2,200 extra per file**). Gogo has used REMLO personally 3+ years (~4th–5th DSCR with Jason; just got clear-to-close on a Michigan investment home).
