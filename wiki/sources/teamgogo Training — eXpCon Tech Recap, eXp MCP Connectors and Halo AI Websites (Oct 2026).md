# #teamgogo Training — eXpCon Tech Recap, eXp MCP Connectors and Halo AI Websites (Oct 2026)

> Weekly [[#teamgogo]] Wednesday training (Oct 21, 2026), hosted by [[Kristy Waker]] with an eXpCon recap of new eXp tools. [[Rafael Mino]] walked through connecting the eXp MCP to Claude; [[Jason Dupree]] gave the mortgage minute; [[Nick Krem]] covered [[Halo]]. Team agents shared their own AI builds. Gogo did not teach on this call.

## Key Takeaways

### eXp MCP connectors (folds into [[AI for Real Estate]])
- eXp's MCP connectors link Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Grok to your logged-in eXp data, so you can ask "when was my last deal," "how much did I make on 123 Main St," "how long until I cap or ICON," "when is the next FastCap." No more handing an AI your credentials.
- **Setup (Rafael):**
  1. eXp Hub → App Store → the new **MCPs** tab.
  2. Pick a connector and choose your AI (for Claude, select web, desktop and mobile).
  3. Copy the configuration URL.
  4. Claude → profile → Settings → Connectors → Add → Add Custom Connector; paste the URL and give it a short name.
- Add connectors one at a time in each AI, with short names ("eXp Hub," "eXp University") so they're easy to call. Expect 3–4. The toolkit has video guides per AI and a prompt library filterable by connector.
- Connectors mentioned: eXp Hub, MyeXp (rev share and organization), eXp University, Canva, CRMs (Lofty, BoldTrail), and divisions (Luxury, Land and Ranch, Sports and Entertainment). Roughly 9–10 exist (per Kristy).
- Example prompts: "Show my revenue share organization and tell me which frontline agents are close to FLQA." "Catch me up on the Hub: unread notifications, group posts, conversations, plus a to-do list of unanswered questions." A 90-day plan for a brand-new agent with zero pipeline.
- Naming the connector in the prompt (e.g. "/exp MCP") skips the tool-permission prompt.
- **Verify before sharing:** live demos pulled a wrong headshot and trailing-365-day (not year-to-date) production. If the AI can't connect a tool, it will build the Zapier zap for you.

### Ops automation with MCP (folds into [[Downline Community Architecture]] / [[Productivity]])
- Kristy keeps all #teamgogo tracking in one Claude project with the eXp MCP connected. When a new agent goes active it automatically updates the spreadsheet, adds them to GoHighLevel, sends the welcome email, and adds them to the Facebook group, calendar and Google Drive. This replaced a daily manual export and its errors.
- "Use the eXp MCP connector and build me a family tree of my downline" produced a clickable, expandable downline tree.
- She kept the human touch: an iPhone Shortcut (designed by Claude) turns a new signup into a group text with Kristy, Gogo and the new agent.

### Agents' AI builds (folds into [[Co-Sponsorship and Agent Attraction System]] / [[Lead Generation]])
- **Agent-attraction pipeline (a team agent):** a Gmail filter forwards a weekly mastermind email to an AI agent, which rebuilds a Canva template, swaps the CTA and schedules the post in Buffer; the link in bio goes to a Halo landing page; each lead is emailed the Zoom link and added to the CRM for a recruiting drip. Tip: use an AI that wakes on forwarded email rather than one constantly watching your inbox.
- **Lead re-engagement (a team agent):** every Tuesday at 7 AM Claude pulls 15 unattended CRM leads and drafts reintroduction emails; the agent approves and sends, Claude marks them complete and flags replies. Correction from Kristy: send from inside the CRM, not Gmail, so each contact's full timeline stays in one place.
- A referral alert through the MCP and Hub flags new referrals in your areas, replacing the old practice of paying a VA to watch referral groups.
- **Oct 28 is the last Open to the Public / Why Not eXp of 2026** (November and December fall on holidays). Get prospects there or they wait until 2027.

### Halo AI websites (folds into [[Personal Branding]])
- [[Halo]] builds and edits the whole site by text, checks Cloudflare's guidance every Friday, and on request blogs 3×/week by finding what locals ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity.
- [[Nick Krem]]: getting AI-recommended takes time (~2 months); sites AI can't find "will never be recommended." Its coding agent builds tools such as CRM-linked surveys, mortgage and cost-of-waiting calculators.
- Free start through Gogo, then $99/month (per Nick, for 2 sites with unlimited edits and blogs).

### New eXp programs from eXpCon (folds into [[Negotiation]]) (as reported on the call; confirm details with eXp)
- **eXp Ready:** pay-at-close home prep financed by Notable. Sellers get a credit line (up to ~$50,000) for pre-listing repairs, staging, paint and landscaping, repaid from closing proceeds. Agents complete a short certification first. An alternative to Curbio, which doesn't serve rural areas.
- **ShowGo:** a free app in the eXp Hub App Store. Post open houses (free; the host keeps the leads) or showings (paid; the posting agent sets the fee) for other agents to claim.
- **Mortgage:** eXp's mortgage joint venture is moving to a new lending partner; details to come.

### Mortgage minute (folds into [[Wealth Building]])
- [[Jason Dupree]] on a draft (unsigned) executive order on housing costs that would cut some loan-level price adjustments, extend appraisal waivers and add refinance title waivers (~$100–200 saved per loan), and on VantageScore acceptance for FHA from Jan 1.
