# The AI System for One Listing Appointment a Day (Kirby Chan)

> A #teamgogo / [[GoGet'Em Community]] guest training (Aug 26, 2026; open-to-public last-Wednesday session) — [[Kirby Chan]]'s **second** appearance. Where his [[AI SEO and Google Ranking for Listings (Kirby Chan)|first training]] laid out the AI-SEO/GEO thesis, this one is the **execution playbook**: reverse prospecting, the one-niche-at-a-time build, the Claude "sister-city" article cheat, live Google Business Profile audits, and how ranking becomes an agent-attraction engine. Opens with a [[Jason Dupree]] mortgage minute. Raw transcript in Clippings.

## Who / results
- Toronto-area [[eXp Realty]] agent (also Fast Forward Movement); **#1 individual producer in Ontario (July 2024), top-3 in Richmond Hill, Toronto Star Platinum Award**; **3× ICON** (3 years at eXp); does ~**50 deals/year** on ~**3 hours/day**; team of **~40**; **595% increase** in his eXp revenue-share group last year.

## Key Takeaways

### Reverse prospecting — get sellers to call you
- Kirby gave up on SEO ~15 years ago (couldn't beat Remax/Zillow/Realtor.com) → Facebook ads (so many leads he had to hire an ISA, and still door-knocked/cold-called) → rebuilt everything around one idea: **"get people who already want to sell to call me."** He has 3 kids and wanted his time back. Result: **one listing appointment a day**, inbound. "Nobody goes to AI to search for an agent; they go to AI to assist them with what they're going through in life" — so become the content the AI teaches from, then recommends.

### Brokerage sites are invisible to AI — build the ranking layer yourself
- ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini (and sellers) don't know your **templated brokerage website** exists — Google won't rank an identical template it hands every agent. Options: build your own site (own `yourname.com`), or on an **eXp Lofty** site pay ~**$39.50/mo** to unlock the **blogs/SEO feature** so it can rank (skip the ~$499 one-time setup upsell). He also demoed an **AI text-to-website service** (~$99/mo, hosting included, unlimited sites/edits, "text your AI web developer" via a phone number) that builds **AI-searchable sites in ~2.5 minutes** — sites "built by AI for AI" are SEO-optimized from creation. He used to spend **$30–50K/year** on websites.

### The Claude "sister-city" competitor-cloning cheat
- Find a **sister city** similar to yours (e.g., Seattle) and Google "[sister city] downsizing." Take the **top 1–3 ranking agent pages**, paste them into **[[Claude]]/ChatGPT** with: *"Analyze these websites, copy the good, improve the bad SEO, then write me an article for [my city] using my standards."* You get a ranked-competitor-grounded article in minutes. (Run it inside a saved **project** with your standards.)

### One niche at a time; one asset a day
- **Dominate one niche before moving to the next:** downsizing → upsizing → first-time buyers → probate → divorce, getting each to page 1 before starting the next. Pick your **#1 niche**, put it at the top, master it with blogs.
- **Create one asset a day** (blog / article / press release / video) — ~1 hour a day. AI weights those four asset types; the more you have on a topic, the more it recommends you. "Nobody puts as much [Richmond Hill downsizing] content as me."
- **Service pages vs. blogs:** each niche gets an **evergreen service page** *plus* **supporting blog articles** (e.g., "selling an inherited home in Ontario — probate & taxes") that make you the **topical authority**. Mix AI-written + human edit (add photos, client stories, testimonials so it's unique).

### GEO — be the name the AI cites
- The real goal isn't #1 on Google, it's being **in the AI Overview / cited by ChatGPT-Gemini-Claude.** A first-time buyer asks AI "what do I do?" → it teaches → then "find me a good agent/mortgage broker" → it names whoever put the most first-time-buyer content out. Test it: ask ChatGPT to "find an agent in [city] to refer for downsizing" — **win 50% of the time and you book appointments.**
- **Foreign-language SEO:** if you speak another language, own it — "Polish real estate agent in Nashville," "Chinese-speaking [city] agent." Near-zero competition, rank #1 fast, better leads.

### Google Business Profile tactics (from the live audits)
- **Name field = keywords.** Put **city + "Realtor"** in the name (e.g., "[Name] Realtor, Winter Park"), not a slogan ("Sell with Norma" — nobody searches that). Match what people actually type ("Winter Park realtor").
- **Target a smaller, winnable city** (Richmond Hill not Toronto; Winter Park not Orlando) — then write a blog ("most of my sales are Winter Park, but I also cover Orlando") to also rank for the bigger metro.
- **Reviews: keywords beat count.** Have clients put the keyword in the review ("first-time home buyer," "senior," "downsizing"). A new agent with 16 reviews outranks a 30-year vet with 1,200 because of keywords + AI. **Reply to every review.**
- **Set hours to 24 hours** (VAs/voicemail cover it); **fill every field** — Instagram/YouTube/LinkedIn (not just Facebook), regular updates, and the **description/bio** (rewrite it with AI to pack your target keywords, service areas, accolades, and niches).
- **Address:** don't edit it (triggers re-verification). Use your **home address but toggle "show business address" OFF** so it's hidden — it still tells Google you're a real person. Keep the address **identical everywhere** (website, realtor profile, GBP) so it verifies instantly.
- **The "known as" workaround:** a **June/July 2026 US Google update** bans stuffing multiple cities in the name field (must match your license: "Name - Brokerage"). Workaround: set your business corp's **"also known as"/DBA** to the keyword-rich name, and those city words also work as keywords in your description. (Multiple GBPs per city via multiple DBAs is possible but "a bad strategy" — confusing.)
- **Domain-as-keyword:** an agent whose brand/domain is "Ranch Properties / ranchesforsale" ranks high on little text because the keyword is in the name. Go all-in on a term via your domain, *or* (Kirby's choice) use your name + city ("Kirby Chan & Co, Richmond Hill real estate").

### One website, many money-pages
- You don't need many websites — you need **many pages** (referral, seller, buyer, lakefront, open-houses). Each **money maker gets its own page/link** so you send the right traffic to the right place (post the *open-house* link on an open-house Facebook post, never your referral link). Multiple sites are fine only for genuinely different audiences (e.g., a "luxury Winter Park" site separate from a "Florida living / relocation" site — and give the second, better-ranked site its **own** GBP so you don't waste a #1 ranking).

### Ranking = the easiest agent-attraction engine
- When you rank #1, **every agent ranking #2–#7 below you says yes** to "want to learn how I do this?" Every listing you win is an agent-attraction moment. His downline: **10 (May 2024) → 21 (June 2025) → then stopped prospecting, switched to attraction → 595% rev-share increase.** Teach your downline to rank: **they win commissions, you win rev share — either way you win** (even when teammates rank #2–4 under you: "there's enough business to go around"). His "**Co with Kirby**" mirrors Gogo's co-sponsorship.
- **Homework:** Google "[your city] realtor" now; not on page 1 = a fixable problem → fix your GBP bio/description keywords, then add blogs covering your service areas. Don't search your *own name* (people who do already know you exist).

## Jason Dupree — Mortgage / Market Minute (Aug 26, 2026)
- **Active inventory up 3.6% YoY — highest since Nov 2019**; a **buyers' market developing** (heaviest in the Southeast — FL, TX); avg listing price **down 1.3% YoY to ~$424,500**; mortgage credit **fundamentally healthy** (low defaults). Parallels the late-2019 buyer market that COVID + plunging rates flipped back to a sellers' market — "be ready."
- **Stop cutting price, negotiate seller concessions / temporary rate buydowns.** A $25K price cut saves a buyer only ~**$150/mo**; a **$14K seller concession → 2-1 buydown saves ~$771/mo year 1, ~$395 year 2**; a **1-0 buydown (~$4,740) saves ~$395/mo** year 1 (year one is the most expensive year of ownership). Temporary buydown = "the bridge over the rainbow" until they refi (everyone refis in a couple years, so a *permanent* buydown is wasteful). REMLO recap: ~0.5% of the loan (~$1,800–$2,200/closing), 6 buy-side transactions to qualify, full-service agent, Texana, all 50 states.
