# The Circle Coaching — Handling Criticism at the Top, Agent Attraction Targeting, Three-Tier Product Ladders and Burnout-Proof Scheduling (de-identified)

> Private [[The Circle|Circle]] coaching call (~1 hr). **NDA: raw transcript not retained; all member PII removed.** Only [[Gogo Bethke]]'s generalizable teachings are captured.

## Key Takeaways

### Stress, criticism and thick skin at the top (folds into [[Mindset]])
- **Most people never reach the top because they can't handle the stress of that level**: hearing no, firing people, telling the truth, carrying the financial consequences. They want only the good part. "Two sides to every dollar": no good without bad.
- **It's lonely at the top.** As stakes grow you run out of people who understand your problems (a lost $40K opportunity is more than some friends earn in a year). That is why a peer room matters: people who've been there can advise, and others learn before they hit the same wall.
- Leaders are human too. On a rough day, tell the room up front you may be blunter than usual, so nobody takes it personally.
- **Criticism means you're relevant.** Her early broker's advice: worry when people *stop* talking about you. Give critics no time or energy; put yourself out there more. Critics usually haven't achieved anything worth talking about.
- **Meet hostility with warmth:** smile and invite the critic to your event. (A peer who confronted her about her co-sponsorship training later attended it and asked questions.)
- Teaching inside the communities you belong to makes you the expert, and the expert is who people partner with. Results come from showing up, the right room, learning, and above all implementing.

### The fishing framework and the ideal agent avatar (folds into [[Co-Sponsorship and Agent Attraction System]])
- **To catch a whale, fish in the ocean, not a backyard pond.** Define (1) the fish (ideal agent avatar), (2) the bait (content), (3) where and when you use the bait, (4) how often you fish.
- **Avatar questions:** gender, age, new or near retirement, team builder or not, monolingual or bilingual, location, buy-side / listing-side / investor, transactions only or open to agent attraction as a second income stream, or aiming to be a top solo agent.
- **Gogo's avatar is the $2M–$7M producer.** At $2M she can add ~2 transactions through referrals, trainings, events and emails; at $7M, two more makes them ICON. She no longer takes brand-new agents (no time to carry them); she matches them with a local team to get to $2M and takes them from there. Partners must already know the basics (addenda, broker contact, state meetings, running their own open house, PITI).
- **Match content to the producer level:**
  - ~$2M (~$60K at 3%) wants to break six figures: "six figures in under eight months as a newly licensed agent," "cap in under three months when you partner with me."
  - ~$7M (~$250K) needs leverage: first VA, a TC from day one (not just contract to close), a local support team (buyer's agent, TC, VA), time back with family, not paying ~40% in taxes.
- **Balance production with recruiting.** While you still do buy-side or listing-side business, don't go all-in on attraction or the pipeline dries up. Once you've left transactions, go all-in.
- **Cadence (talking only to agents):** educational content 4 days a week, "fishing" (asking agents to partner) 3 days a week. Teach, ask. Prove you're the expert, then invite.
- **Co-sponsorship is a major entry point** for agents outside her communities; many recent recruits came that way.

### Posting-time analytics and comment triggers (folds into [[Social Media and Email Marketing]])
- **Post when your audience is active.** An 11 AM post can be buried by the time your "big fish" scroll at 8–11 PM.
- **Instagram steps:** Profile → Your dashboard → Audience tab → scroll about halfway (past gender, age range, top locations) → **Most active times** by day and hour.
- Re-check periodically; it shifts with seasons and agent busyness. Gogo's best window moved from Tue/Wed/Thu noon–3 PM to Mon/Wed/Thu 3–6 PM; only ~1% of her followers visit on Sundays (her profile reads as business). Put important agent-attraction posts in the peak window.
- Followers tend to mirror your own gender and age. A ~1% follower drop is usually Instagram purging bots.
- Use email stats (e.g. Mailchimp open and click times) to time newsletter sends.
- **One trigger word per post:** "Comment PARTNER" → ManyChat / GoHighLevel auto-DMs a link to your agent-attraction website.

### Lend your VA to the tech call (folds into [[Productivity]])
- Bring your VA into anything from coaching. A VA should never say "I don't know how": they have chat access and a weekly tech call with Gogo's team.
- **Flow:** topic comes up at the Tuesday-noon coaching call → VA attends the 3 PM tech call → it's set up by Friday. Gogo doesn't attend or track the questions; she "lends her team" for an hour.
- "I tell them what to do and they do it. I cut the check."

### The three-tier product ladder (folds into [[Wealth Building]])
- **Always offer three products; buyers almost always pick the middle**, so make the middle the one you most want to sell.
  - **Low:** one-time DIY digital course (e.g. $97). One-time income.
  - **Middle:** monthly continuity (e.g. ~$97/month): the course plus done-with-you help via a weekly tech-support call run by your VA, not you (~$1,164/yr recurring that costs none of your time).
  - **High:** full coaching with direct access. "Life-changing money."
- **Upsell at checkout:** before someone buys the DIY course, explain it's do-it-yourself and offer the monthly option with setup help. ~90% of buyers never implement unless someone helps them set it up.
- **Low-cost access tiers:** a "brain-dump" broadcast chat (e.g. $49/month) where followers get insights but can't message you, or a capped chat (one question a day). Direct access only at the top tier.
- **Renewals:** ~10% off to stay on; a pay-upfront discount (e.g. 3 months for $2,700 vs. $1,000/month) gives a reason to prepay. Top-tier perks (extra event tickets, first notice of opportunities) go to the inner group first.
- **Teach what excites you right now.** It will change: her first product (a 2018 social-media boot camp) became something she no longer wanted to teach. Repeat it until you're done, then move to your next phase.
- Show Gogo the finished product (live site, automations, ManyChat) so she can review it as a consumer, not in theory.

### "There are no busy seasons" (folds into [[Productivity]])
- **Responsiveness test:** in the room, nobody had gone more than an hour without a text reply from Gogo. Not answering messages for weeks means missed opportunities.
- **Diagnostic:** do you secretly enjoy being busy because it makes you feel important?
- **If you have busy and slow seasons, you don't have enough team** to carry the busy ones. Gogo runs the same "boring" Monday meetings every week, February or August.
- **Ideal-day exercise:** walk through your ideal day once every goal is met, nothing sacrificed (wake time, bedtime, morning routine, house help, workdays, days off). Then schedule it and live by your calendar.
- **Block rest like workouts.** E.g. block Wednesday and Friday noon–8 PM (not just to 5, so no evening listing appointments sneak in): no showings, sellers, listing appointments or email.
- **Your assistant runs your calendar and is "your boss."**
- **Hire a house manager** (laundry, groceries, doctor and vet appointments, dry cleaning). Doing laundry and email yourself isn't your best use of time; using help doesn't make you less of a mom. **How:** post a very detailed listing in local neighborhood or school Facebook groups, candidates DM you, your assistant runs ~5 back-to-back hourly interviews in one morning, pick one within a 15–20 minute drive.
- Consistency prevents burnout: same things, same times, whether you feel like it or not. "Life is not about the chase. We hustled enough."
