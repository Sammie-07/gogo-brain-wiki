# How To Invest for Beginners — The Ultimate Guide (Toby Mathis)

> A standalone Infinity Investing educational video by [[Toby Mathis]] (tax attorney; the auto-caption "Tony Matthysse" is a mis-transcription). A beginner's case for consistent, hands-off index investing — "set it and forget it," time in the market, and compounding. Sits alongside Gogo's [[Wealth Building]] canon.

## Key Takeaways

- **Fear is the real blocker.** The stock market feels like a big scary dog but is "just a puppy" — a little knowledge breaks the fear. Free training at infinityinvesting.com.
- **Who actually builds wealth:** a millionaire study found the top three millionaire-making professions were **accountants, engineers, and teachers** — not through inheritance, but because they follow a plan, invest consistently, and don't mess with it ("let it bake like a cake").
- **Crash history rewards the patient.** Black Monday 1987 (Dow −22% in a day), 2002 dot-com, 2008 (−38%), 2020 pandemic — every crash was followed by a big runup. The people who panicked and sold missed the recovery every time.
- **Cash is trash.** $1,000 left invested from 1980 ≈ **$45,000** today; in cash it's worth ~**$250** (the dollar is down ~75%). Inflation melts cash "like ice" — keep it in the freezer and grow it.
- **The S&P averages >10%/year over its life** — not a straight line, so don't panic on pullbacks.
- **Time in the market, not timing the market.** A Schwab study (76 twenty-year stretches) showed even a perfect bottom-picker barely beat someone who just invested consistently — timing is nearly impossible and not worth it.
- **Compounding math (start at 20, retire at 65 = 45 yrs, avg S&P):** $1,000 once → ~$72K; **$5/day (~$1,825/yr) → $1.3M**; $3,000/yr ($250/mo) → $2.1M; $5,000/yr → ~$3.6M. It's just a small % of income, consistently, over a long horizon.
- **Get the kids in early** via a custodial account; **max a Roth IRA** so they never pay tax on the growth. "He who understands compound interest earns it; he who does not pays it."
- **The 5 steps:** (1) **know your numbers** (facts don't care about feelings); (2) **open a brokerage account** (Robinhood, Fidelity, Schwab, TD Ameritrade, E*Trade, Webull, M1 — ~30 min); (3) **automate both funding and investing** into an ETF (SPY, VOO, or Kevin Simpson's covered-call ETF DVO) — pay it like a bill so you don't see it; (4) **be patient** ("bake the bread — let it rise; yank it early and it falls"); (5) over a long enough horizon, **you are a millionaire**.
- **If trained/confident, pick individual stocks; if not, SPY/VOO** — "you will not be disappointed."
