## Key Takeaways

### The thesis
- **"We sell homes, not views and subscribers."** Optimize for the **viewer** (average view duration), not view count. A 1,000-subscriber small-market channel can out-earn a 50,000-subscriber big-market one.
- **YouTube's scale:** #1 streaming platform (doubles Netflix), #1 podcasting platform, 2B+ monthly users; **51% of buyers use YouTube as their primary "where to live" resource.**
- **Most agent YouTube fails** because agents post listings, open houses, "day in the life," and "look at me" videos — those target *other agents* and people already in the process. Make content for the **highest-intent viewer.**

### Avatar = relocation buyers
The most scalable YouTube audience. One relo client ≈ **4 transactions** (referral to sell their current home + the buy where they move + relo buyers transact **~every 3 years** vs. 7–10). Nurture them: 90% of consumers say they'd reuse their agent, only 10% do — a post-close automation failure; a simple nurture system should convert **9/10** to repeat.

### The 3-stage content funnel
1. **Top (search/evergreen):** "cost of living in [city]" + "pros and cons of living in [city]" — the first two videos every new client makes. Re-record quarterly; cite data points (niche.com, realtor.com, Redfin) to avoid steering.
2. **Middle (consideration):** "where to live in [city] — full map tour," "best 5 suburbs/master-plan communities near [city]." **Title with the biggest, most-searched "driver city," never a micro-suburb** (even when the content is about the suburb).
3. **Bottom (decision = the algorithm):** **18–28-min home tours** and **vlog tours** → trigger **browse & suggested traffic** ("YouTube recommended traffic"), which *is* the algorithm — **not** search/SEO/keywords (that was the 2018–2021 game). Pushed to home screens and especially **TV** (TV viewers watch ~2 min longer, highest intent).

### Home-tour video structure (works for every video type)
- **Call out the avatar broadly** ("if you're thinking about moving to Boise…") using the **driver city**, not the actual town; don't over-narrow.
- **Hook in ~20 sec** teasing features (shops, RV bays, land, single-level) — **never price.**
- **Never reveal price until the end** ("the reveal"); never "what you can get for $500K" (kills all other bands) — say "a very affordable price."
- **Rehooks** + **smooth transitions** + **pacing** (info, not padding).
- **"Undercover" CTA** woven into the content, not a cheesy pitch.
- **Big reveal → playlist hand-off:** recap features → big rehook + CTA → reveal price → "want more homes like this? click here."
- **Proof:** a Rachel home tour drove one lead closing ~$10M; 1,900 watch-hours (≈ a reel with 2.2M views), 15,000 viewers @ 8:25 avg, **25% watched 100%** of a 25-min video. Long form = education; short form = dopamine ("buffet vs. slot machine").

### Activating the algorithm (checklist)
1. **One video a week** — "if you can't commit, don't even do it." Entrepreneurship is boring; do more and better of what already works.
2. **7-min average view duration** → **18–28-min videos** (people watch ~28–30%).
3. **50%+ views from TV** (top agents ~70%).
4. **70%+ browse & suggested traffic.**
5. **No ads** (they pull videos out of organic ranking — Jesse's own hard lesson).
6. **No shorts** (they dilute — exception: a nationwide strategy).
- "Favored by the algorithm" = **15 videos with 50+ views in the last 7 days.** Best post days: **Saturday (#1), Wednesday (#2).**

### The AI guardrail (important counter-note)
**AI scripts / AI voice / AI avatars get channels throttled.** YouTube measures AI visual + language, throttles/demonetizes high-AI content, and has removed tens of thousands of AI accounts. "Everyone can smell AI on the scroll." Do the activities closest to the money — be the human on camera; use AI only for recon from your own perspective. (Tiffany Cabrero, ~$30M/yr, hit "the plague" after switching to AI scripts/voice.) Virality ≠ revenue: **Alex Hormozi** 2024 virality → +3× brand, −30% revenue; 2025 core-avatar refocus → −30% views, +6× revenue.

### The refer-off / out-of-production model
YouTube is the only platform where the agent needn't close the deal. Many clients **refer every lead** at a **50/50 split** ("if Zillow can charge 30–40%, I can charge 50"), living abroad / part-time. $200–300K GCI referral-only is "very doable"; a consistent LA agent ~$500K. Speak as **"my team and I"** so callers expect a hand-off. Cross-state referrals ride the **eXp** platform. "A YouTube lead is better than a referral."
