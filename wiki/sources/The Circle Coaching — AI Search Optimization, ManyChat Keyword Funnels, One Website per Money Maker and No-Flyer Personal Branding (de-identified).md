# The Circle Coaching — AI Search Optimization, ManyChat Keyword Funnels, One Website per Money Maker and No-Flyer Personal Branding (de-identified)

> Private [[The Circle|Circle]] coaching call. **NDA: raw transcript not retained; all member PII removed.** Only [[Gogo Bethke]]'s generalizable teachings are captured.

## Key Takeaways

### AI search optimization and website architecture (folds into [[Personal Branding]])
- **SEO is no longer enough.** Sites also need AEO (answer-engine) and GEO optimization. Gogo cites research that ~41% of people now search in ChatGPT or Claude instead of Google (unsourced, approximate). Not AI-optimized = "you don't exist. You're not an option."
- Gogo's own site scored **A for SEO and F for AI optimization**; expect most sites to do the same unless built recently with AI optimization.
- **A fix takes about a month:** change the pages, then wait for AI crawlers and Google to re-crawl and re-index on their own schedule.
- **Homework prompt for Claude:** "Research [direct link to your site] and check whether it is AEO, SEO and GEO optimized. Report what's working, what isn't, what needs to change and how."
- **Don't audit brokerage-provided agent sites** (e.g. eXp agent sites): they aren't AI-optimized, you can't change them, and they won't show up. Audit the sites you own.
- **Every money maker gets its own landing page.** Agent-attraction traffic ("comment partner") must never land on a buy-a-home or sell-with-me page.
- **Separate domain or slash path?** "If you can sell it one day, build it separate." GoGet'Em Community has its own .com and Instagram so it can be sold as a standalone business; personal brands (downline, coaching) live as /partner or /thecircle on her personal site.
- **Re-test links after every site update.** During AI-optimization work the link in her "partner" ManyChat button broke; the page loaded fine, so nobody noticed until she tested the funnel live. After any site change, the team re-tests every keyword as a prospect would: comment on Instagram and follow the whole flow.

### ManyChat keyword funnels (folds into [[Co-Sponsorship and Agent Attraction System]] / [[Lead Generation]])
- Someone comments a keyword and a DM with a link goes out automatically. Keywords feel less salesy than "DM me," so they get more engagement.
- **"And," not "or":** as many keywords to as many pages as you want (e.g. one to a co-sponsorship page with Gogo, one to the agent's own partner page).
- **Pick keywords for life.** Gogo's is "partner" (it's a partnership), used ~4 years.
- **Evergreen vs. temporary:** some keywords serve for years; others are for one listing or this weekend's open house, retired or relinked once it sells.
- **Homework:** list your top 5 money makers (sellers, buyers, referrals, agents, open houses, investors, your niche) and set up a keyword, message and destination link for each.
- **Seller keyword:** post a market stat ("our market went up X%; want to know what your home is worth?"), ask for "seller," and DM a link to a self-serve CMA generator that collects name, email and phone. You wake up to a list of people thinking about selling.
- **Write the DM as if texting by hand,** and don't assume they've decided. Weak: "So glad you want to join my downline." Better: "So excited you're thinking about joining eXp. Can't wait to see you in my calendar."
- **Make it about them:** "here's a link to learn more about me" fails.
- **Cut the steps.** Target two clicks: button, then calendar. Gogo's flow: "So excited you're interested in changing brokerages" → "I'm in" button → watch the video on the partner page → calendar.
- **A partner-with-me page needs:** who you are, why partner with you, ICON status, location, what you provide (weekly trainings, direct line, group chat, Facebook group), a video of you, and a direct calendar link.
- **Test every funnel from a real account** before trusting it.

### Instagram: no flyers, put yourself in the post (folds into [[Social Media and Email Marketing]])
- **"Flyers don't work."** Gogo's rule of thumb: more than ~20% text and Instagram treats the image as an ad and won't show it to your own followers (her heuristic, not a documented rule).
- Example from the call: a flyer had zero engagement after 19 hours; a Reel with the agent in it got ~1,000 views from ~1,200 followers.
- **Flyers drag the whole account down:** repeated zero-engagement posts teach the algorithm your content isn't important.
- **Be in 95% of your posts.** Listings and open houses are fine with you in front of the house or in the kitchen. Use AI graphics sparingly. Big personal brands (Ryan Serhant, Grant Cardone) appear in almost every post and show almost no property.
- **Lock the .com and the Instagram handle first** (the only places a name belongs to one person), then use the same name everywhere. YouTube can run as channels under your main profile.

### AI tools and the eXp MCP (folds into [[AI for Real Estate]])
- **Learn Claude's browser extension** (Claude in Chrome): take every idea to Claude first. Setup help at the GGTC tech call.
- **Mine your own history:** Gogo's team produced a report of all her 2018–2020 agent-attraction posts (dates, descriptions, post type) to rebuild as a "2026 version." Old announcements become anniversary recaps (e.g. a seven-year recap of retiring Duane with revenue share).
- **eXp MCP for offboarding cleanup:** pull the offboarded-agent report, remove those agents from the team Facebook group, and retag them "offboarded" in HighLevel so team emails stop. Safety: ~10 removals a day so the platform doesn't flag a bot; act only when 100% sure it's the same person; anything uncertain goes to a spreadsheet for manual review.
- **Live lookups:** ask the MCP whether an agent is in your downline; it returns join date, sponsor, upline, level and production.
- Circle members get an extra hour with Gogo's full team every month (website, Facebook group, systems). Use it.

### Speed of execution (folds into [[Productivity]])
- **Impatience is a success trait.** Gogo's team turns a new ManyChat keyword around in ~5 minutes; ManyChat setup takes "moments," so two weeks of waiting is two weeks behind.
- Coaching is for working on the business: bring links, websites and products to pick apart live.
- Put assistants on a dedicated work phone and add them to the coaching group chat.

### Brokerage tools (folds into [[Co-Sponsorship and Agent Attraction System]])
- Per Gogo: eXp's new bonus pays the attracting agent $8,000 (instead of $4,000) if a new agent caps in their first year (unverified).
- eXp includes a CRM in the monthly fee, changeable each year during the annual window. A separate CRM is mainly worth it to keep your database independent of the brokerage.
