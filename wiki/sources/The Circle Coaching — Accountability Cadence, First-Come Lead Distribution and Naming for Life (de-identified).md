# The Circle Coaching — Accountability Cadence, First-Come Lead Distribution and Naming for Life (de-identified)

> A private [[The Circle|Circle]] coaching call. **NDA — raw transcript NOT retained** (no Clippings copy; `original:` = "NDA; transcript not retained"); all member PII, team names, and vendor names stripped. Only [[Gogo Bethke]]'s generalizable teachings are captured. New members onboarding; heavy on accountability, revenue-share retirement math, brand-naming, lead distribution, and CRM setup.

## Key Takeaways

- **"You hired me to tell you what you need to hear, not what you want to hear."** The come-to-Jesus: if you finish 12 months in the same situation you started, "I will fire you — that will not be tied to my reputation." Pay $30K, get it back **10-fold** or she fires you and keeps the money. New results require new habits; you can't be the same person.
- **Homework is due Fridays — and it's for you, not the coach.** It (1) forces completion, (2) builds the tracking habit, and (3) compounds into a monthly → quarterly → yearly report of "holy [—] look how much I got done." "I don't log into your dashboard — I'm here to help you grow, not to babysit."
- **Homework completion is a leadership X-ray.** Half your homework can be done by your team. If your dashboard (and your team's) hasn't been logged into in weeks, "guess who's not moving the needle" — you don't hold your team accountable. "Whatever happens, whose fault is it? Mine — because I allowed it."
- **Meeting cadence: Monday money-maker meetings → Wednesday catch-up → Friday delivery.** Monday, meet each "money maker" team and assign; **Wednesday catch-up call** is where the team surfaces problems/upgrades ("that feature needs a $60/mo upgrade — okay?") so nothing blindsides you Friday. "Don't come to me Friday to tell me we need to upgrade — that tells me for 5 days you did nothing."
- **Track everything that makes money — "my trackers have trackers."** Example: a 10-minute Monday email review — emails sent, open rate, click-through. "If I sent a million emails and 100,000 opened, that's 100,000 opportunities." Reverse-engineer how many sends you need to hit the income goal.
- **Rev-share retirement math (reinforced):** you need **30 FLQAs** (frontline agents who've each collected ≥$5,000 commission in a 6-month window) to open all Level 7 and retire off revenue share. While **ICON**, all Level 7 is open regardless — but you fall back to your FLQA count the moment you drop ICON, so **build for the future, not the present.** ~50% of frontline qualify, so "if you need 30, go get 60."
- **First-come-first-serve leads, NOT round robin.** Round robin breeds complacency ("agents sit like little birds, mouths open — next one's mine"). Instead, dump every lead into a team chat (CRM → Zapier → an assistant posts it, since Zapier can't post into a group chat) and it's **first come, first serve — claim it by your initials.** "Next time someone's complaining they don't have enough leads: wake up." Ownership dispute? **Make them prove it**; if proven, it's theirs — otherwise whoever gets it, gets it.
- **Name your org for life, and don't tie it to location.** You'll pitch this name "for the rest of your career," so make sure it rolls off the tongue and you'll never repackage shop. eXp is global — even if you produce locally, you attract agents worldwide, so **nothing in the brand should be tied to a location.** Own it first (GoDaddy `.com` + the Instagram handle) before building. Gogo's own regret: she'd **not** put "team" in the name today (it implies she's a team leader taking a split — she takes no cut).
- **Don't platform-hop your community — you lose ~30% every migration.** Pick a container that scales past thousands *up front* (a WhatsApp community holds ~5,000+), because moving thousands of members later "is such a pain, you lose about 30% every time you switch."

## Gogo's Generalizable Teachings

### Accountability is the product

- **New habits, new results; old habits, old results.** Homework forces the break from old patterns. "If you keep doing the same things, you'll get the same results — and I won't be tied to that."
- **The compounding report as motivator.** Small check-marks feel like nothing in a week, but the monthly/quarterly/yearly report reveals the volume — "how much you removed from your plate, how many new things you started." The year-end goal: "this long of a report of all the [—] we got done, then meet up and celebrate." If you don't log in, the year-end report "looks like you didn't do your job."
- **Put it in the calendar until it's a habit.** "If you can't hold yourself accountable without an appointment, put it in your calendar" — Fridays at 9am with your coffee, until Friday-morning homework is automatic.
- **Leadership = holding others accountable.** "If someone's helping you with it, it's their responsibility — but as the leader it's *your* responsibility to hold them accountable." On her own team, a due item is *never* optional.

### Revenue-share build (reinforcement of the [[Real Estate Teams#Mechanics: FLA, FLQA & Level Unlocks (detailed — June 2026 Roundtable)|FLQA mechanics]])

- Pull your **actual** frontline qualifying count (an ICON's dashboard just shows "30 QA" regardless), so you know the real gap to 30.
- Build over 2–3–10 years toward a rev-share retirement, not for the present month.
- **Co-sponsorship** is how an agent *outside* your downline plugs into the team benefits (they name you Primary, Gogo Co-Sponsor); agents already in your downline inherit it automatically.

### Agent-attraction sites: platform vs. multiplier

- Co-sponsorship + agent-attraction websites are built for members (fill out a form to personalize). Framing on the page: **eXp is the platform** (everyone company-wide gets it); **you + the team are the multiplier** (the extra benefits). Pull your team production numbers into the site to boost the headline stats.
- **"You are not lazy — you are just unsupported."** The attraction pitch reframes an under-producing agent's problem as missing support, not effort.
- **Three steps, no gatekeeping:** watch the video → book a call → make a decision. A recurring monthly "why-not-eXp" presentation (last Wednesday of the month) is built into the site so members can invite prospects and Gogo closes.

### Brand naming & rebrand execution

- Choose a name you can **relate to and pitch for the rest of your career**; "I'd rather make a mistake and move than not move at all," but if it doesn't roll off the tongue, **pause a week** and get it right before building.
- **Own the brand first:** GoDaddy `.com` + Instagram handle for the *downline group*, not just your personal brand.
- **Eat the elephant one bite at a time:** break a rebrand into concrete weekly pieces — Facebook group, Facebook community chat, Instagram account, `.com`, then the bios for each.
- **AI caveat:** Claude/AI defaults to a *local* real-estate framing and will keep tying your brand to a location — you must **explicitly tell it** you operate in every US state + 27 countries and attract agents globally, or it won't listen. (Ties to the [[AI for Real Estate|prompt-context discipline]].)
- Emergent-tagline example (kept as craft, not member-identifying): a downline brand landed on *"independent by design, connected by choice"* — the whole point of the model in one line.

### Lead distribution & CRM

- **First come, first serve** beats round robin (which breeds "next one's mine" complacency). Route leads into one team chat; claim by initials. Disputes → prove it.
- **CRM = Bold Trail (the renamed KV Core)** — same dashboard, comes free with eXp (`firstname-lastname.exprealty.com` site + built-in CRM); the paid "team" version isn't required — Gogo ran a team on one KV Core with everyone claiming leads by initials.
- **Setup order:** upload your whole database → **tag everyone into "buckets"** (buyer, seller, past client, sphere, referral partner, open house) → set up market updates → set up automated emails — then **rewrite the canned emails in your own voice** (the defaults are "boring and politically correct," "nothing like how I talk to my clients").
- **Downline email cadence:** don't over-email cloud-flooded agents (they already get upline comms); a predictable weekly touch (e.g., Monday morning) plus a **monthly own-training** beyond what the upline offers, with email + community-chat reminders.
