# The Relationship Beneath Results — Deeply Connected Method (Ludmila Woodruff)

> A #teamgogo / [[GoGet'Em Community]] guest webinar (2nd appearance) by somatic/nervous-system coach [[Ludmila Woodruff]], hosted with [[Gogo Bethke]] (who does a live exercise herself). A body-first, IFS + Polyvagal lens on why the way you relate to *one* thing shapes your business results — "the relationship beneath results."

## Key Takeaways

- **Core thesis:** *"The way you relate to one thing is the way you relate to almost everything."* Revenue, leadership, delegation, marriage, and parenting all trace back to one internal pattern.
- **We don't relate to reality — we relate to the meaning we made about it.** Humans are "meaning-making machines" and "walking patterns" formed in childhood and reinforced through life.
- **The meaning-making chain:** experience → the meaning given (about self/others/world) → an emotional imprint stored in the body → predictions → a protection/response behavior → your business results.
- **Neuroception** (Porges/Polyvagal): the nervous system automatically reads cues as safety, danger, or life-threat. You can't switch it off, but healing your patterns shifts it to read more safety than danger. *"Unless I feel safe inside myself, I will always feel unsafe in the world — and that means business too."*
- **Triggers are the map.** "The more you get triggered, the more you understand there's something inside you that still doesn't feel safe." A trigger in sales/marketing/client work points to an unhealed part that feels disrespected, rejected, mocked, or "not good/smart enough."
- **Journaling framework:** write your triggers → the childhood origin → the belief/meaning ("I'm not smart enough," "I'll be mocked") → the protection behavior (avoidance, procrastination, over-analysis, micromanaging, control).
- **Body first, not mindset first.** Mindset is only ~10%; most drivers are subconscious and stored in the body — regulate the body first ("top-down *and* bottom-up"). Sessions open with **breathwork** (slow nasal inhale, hold, exhale through a moving jaw) and **vagus-nerve/neck** movements.
- **Embodiment exercise:** eyes closed, drop a statement into the body — "My business means ___" / "Money means ___" — and answer with the first word/sensation. The subconscious answer (e.g., "validation," felt in the gut) often contradicts the conscious wish.
- **"Parts" work (IFS):** unhealed younger parts keep running old protection programs; you "hold space" and speak to the part ("I can feel you in my gut; I know you're here for a reason") rather than fighting it. The gut is framed as the seat of the authentic self; unhealed, it runs the "shadow" version (worth = performance/validation).
- **Money mindset:** childhood exposure to parents' money fights or "financial abuse" imprints "money = safety/survival," shaping adult earning and risk behavior.
- **Relationship → business bleed:** an unpredictable/unsafe father can imprint "men are unsafe" → hyper-independence, inability to delegate or trust (especially men), control/micromanagement — straight into how you run a team.
- **"Self energy":** everyone is born with an authentic self (courage, curiosity, presence, regulation). You weren't "weaker" before — you adapted to survive. The work is transforming what's in the way, not fixing a broken person.
- **Reading references:** *No Bad Parts* (Richard Schwartz, IFS) and *The Body Keeps the Score* (Bessel van der Kolk).
