# How to Convert More Clients With Email (Eben Pagan)

> [[GoGet'Em Community]] guest training (part of GGTC's September "email marketing" month), hosted by [[Gogo Bethke]]. Veteran direct-response marketer [[Eben Pagan]] teaches using AI to write client-converting email newsletters. Three de-facto segments: the case for follow-up → the Fears/Frustrations vs Wants/Aspirations avatar sprint + a live ChatGPT build → Gogo's "find six Matts" list-segmentation close (with the ~$24 [[Niche Finder]] upsell).

## Key Takeaways

### The follow-up thesis (Eben)
- **"Most things don't work for most people" in marketing — but something works for each of us; find your one channel** (echoes Peter Thiel's "one good distribution channel").
- **Buyer-readiness math (from Dean Jackson):** only ~**1–3%** of leads are ready to buy *now*; follow them for a year and ~**50%** buy something to solve their problem. **"Nobody follows up"** — that gap is the entire edge.
- **Email is the channel:** cheapest, text-only, still "the language of business," and it builds a **database you own** (vs. algorithm-dependent social). Eben estimates he'd have done only 5–10% of his sales without email (a "10–20x return").
- **Repurpose across formats:** newsletter → AI → 3 video scripts; or a live-video transcript → AI → newsletter. Do one thing weekly, reuse it 2–3 ways.
- **Content rule:** write what a $1M buyer/seller actually wants (how to save/make $100K, sell fast, best school district, best rate) — not "lame" cat/kid filler (a little personality is fine).

### The FF/WA avatar sprint (Eben)
- Draw a line down a page: left = **Fears & Frustrations**, right = **Wants & Aspirations**. Sprint ~3 minutes each for your **ideal** client (not any client).
- Be **specific and concrete** ("buys houses with foundation problems"), not abstract ("doesn't want to lose money"). Anchor to time, money, hassle, risk, convenience.
- **Loss aversion (Kahneman & Tversky):** people will do ~**2×** as much to avoid a loss as to achieve a gain — so deliberately prompt AI for **both "toward" and "away-from" motivations**, because marketers unconsciously over-index on the positive.

### The step-by-step AI newsletter workflow (the core method)
1. Start in a **blank ChatGPT/Claude** (no templates/memory): "I'm a real estate agent and I work with [avatar]…"
2. **Dump everything** about the avatar — demographics, psychographics, behaviors, quirks. Every detail matters ("AI is spectacular at reading into psychology").
3. Ask for a **deeper psychological/motivational profile** including **toward and away-from motivations** (surfaced autonomy, control, certainty, status, "avoiding submission can outrank profit").
4. Use AI to **reframe how you say things to a type** (e.g., give a control-driven client **three options** so they feel in control).
5. **Ask for TOPICS first** ("list topics, don't write the newsletter") → picked ~10–15 favorites from ~80 → "write more like my favorites."
6. Go **step by step / "checkpoints" / "waypoints"** — don't jump to "write it." Define the **single desired reader action** (a phone call) and script exactly what you want them to say (Dean Jackson's "magic wand": the more specific the desired action, the better AI performs).
7. Feed AI **your own framework** so output reflects how you think; ask for the **outline** → review → then "write it, 3 pages max."
8. **Edit for voice:** have AI rewrite in your style from samples of your past writing, then **line-edit by hand.** Never copy-paste-send raw. (Gogo add-on: also turn each topic into an **Instagram caption.**)
- Leverage claim: a profile that would take 1–2 VIP days took ~10 minutes of prompting (~100×).

### Gogo's list-value / segmentation close
- Reframe your list: each lead can be worth ~$1M in lifetime commissions; write to the specific few, not "at the list."
- **"Find six Matts":** email a simple **questionnaire** to the database ("how many properties do you own? 10+/20+/40+/50+/100+"); people answer out of pride. Then **tag/segment** into tiers (starter / medium / "Matt") and email each tier at their caliber.
- Slow down and truly define the ideal client — *"we're fishing in a pond for a shark"*; getting annoyed describing them means you never defined them.

## Numbers / facts
- Buyer readiness: ~1–3% now, ~50% within a year; loss aversion ≈ 2×.
- Demo outputs: 80 newsletter topics generated; newsletter capped at 3 pages; ~10 min vs. 1–2 VIP days (~100×).
- Investor-client economics (demo): ~10 transactions/yr × ~$1.2M avg × 2.4% buy-side ≈ **$288K/yr** from one client; 6–7 such clients ≈ **$1.7–2M**.
- [[Niche Finder]]: ~$24, one-time, re-runnable per client type.
- Gogo: sends "an email a day" ≈ ~7M emails/year across her list.
