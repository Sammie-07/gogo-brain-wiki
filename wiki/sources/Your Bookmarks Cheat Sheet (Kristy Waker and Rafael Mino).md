# Your Bookmarks Cheat Sheet (Kristy Waker & Rafael Mino)

> A #teamgogo Wednesday training (Aug 5, 2026) by [[Kristy Waker]] & [[Rafael Mino]]: the definitive "where's that link again?" reference — every link a #teamgogo/eXp agent should bookmark, in three folders. Opens with a [[Jason Dupree]] mortgage/REMLO minute. Follow-along slide deck lives in the Team GoGo Google Drive.

## The 3-Folder Bookmark System

Create three bookmark-bar folders in this order — **#teamgogo → eXp → Real Estate** — via the address-bar star (Chrome; Safari = "Favorites").

**📁 #teamgogo** (upline/benefit resources; the membership area is the hub everything else reaches):
- Team GoGo membership area (login-gated recordings/onboarding hub) · **First 90 Days of Real Estate** checklist (updated ~weekly) · **Team GoGo map** (agent referral map; pin from My eXp primary license; multi-location via Rafael's form) · #teamgogo Facebook group (features tab → membership area, map, Messenger chat) · **Honey Badger Nation** (+ weekly-coaching calendar; NAEA course free to downline) · **Fast Forward Movement** (Zoom passwords in the Monday newsletter) · **Gogo's Boot Camp** (agent attraction)

**📁 eXp:**
- New Agent Orientation (an eXp University link) · **Passport / Okta** (eXp SSO — migrating into the Hub) · **The Hub** · eXp events calendar (`eventscal.exprealty.com`) · **My eXp** · eXp Toolkits · **eXp University**

**📁 Real Estate** (client/transaction tools — swap in your own):
- Your CRM (Kristy uses BoldTrail; others Lofty/HubSpot/Lion Desk) · Your MLS · **SkySlope + SkySlope Forms** · Your eXp state/agent website · the **Transaction/Commission Quick Reference** (US splits, caps, TC fee min/max, doc upload/split/assign) · Buyer & Seller toolkits

## eXp Platform Map (what lives where)
- **The Hub** — eXp's new workplace (replaces Slack/Workplace; "join the community" + email code to enter): feed/chat/DMs, groups (one per state, eXp University, announcements, referrals, cons), and an **App Store of 43 apps** consolidating every eXp tool. Desktop + "eXp Hub" mobile app; eXp Care in the left menu.
- **My eXp** — capping status, uplines, influencer/ICON qualification, revenue share, dashboard, document portal (1099s, the ICA, W9, address), events calendar, mentor status, knowledge base, Contact eXp Care. (My eXp + Hub together = "everything.")
- **eXp University** — training paths (Emerging, Capping & ICON, Teams, Attractors, New to eXp, Canadian, International); live trainings (ICON Seller, Fast Cap, Fast Attract, Team Mastery, etc.); Course Overview pages carry downloadable **playbooks** (scripts). Training calendar bookmarked separately.
- **SkySlope** — mandatory: transaction-doc submission + broker-approval checklists. **SkySlope Forms** (Apps → Forms & Digisign): broker-preferred contract platform with state templates + eXp docs pre-loaded (optional; e-sign via **DigiSign** ~$60–75/yr or **DocuSign**). A file built in Forms auto-connects to SkySlope for broker approval; eXp accepts any signed contracts on the checklist (even hand-filled + uploaded).

## Jason Dupree — Mortgage / REMLO Minute (July numbers)
- **Market:** "every day is the best day in the market" — extreme uncertainty; cautious optimism on a possible 60-day Middle East ceasefire; **oil down to ~$75/bbl from ~$100** two weeks prior; a **weak jobs report** (growth mostly seasonal education/health). Coaching tip: reinforce the buyer's *payment comfort* early — deals fall through when buyers get scared late.
- **REMLO — July:** **$23,791,000** in loan volume nationwide and **~$118,955** in total commission earned by agent-REMLOs — "in one of the worst months," with rates back near last year's levels. REMLO = agents licensed as loan officers earning on the loan too (refis of past clients, new clients, cross-agent deals; all 50 states). "What is REMLO" call 3 PM ET; onboarding ≈ a 45-min session with Jason after HR paperwork.
