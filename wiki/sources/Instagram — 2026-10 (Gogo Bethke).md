# Instagram — 2026-10 (Gogo Bethke)

> Meaningful captions and stories from @gogosrealestate, ingested daily. Raw: [[Clippings/instagram/2026-10.md]] and [[Clippings/instagram/stories/2026-10.md]]. Covers 2026-10-08 through 2026-10-16 so far.

## Key Takeaways

### Oct 8 — "Amy and I met on Instagram" (caption)
- Amy Gregory reached out to Gogo on Instagram about eXp **6 years ago**; Gogo told her why she switched and Amy switched too. They became business partners and good friends, and "each retired our hubbies" (both husbands now work with them instead of in corporate America).
- Both are top producers and top agent attractors **through social media marketing alone**: "Neither one of us ever bought a lead or cold called an agent."
- Amy was speaking on the big stage at eXpCon on how to run your business like a CEO; Gogo called it a "proud mom moment" in her stories.
- Takeaway she pushes to the next generation: build your brand and run it for free on socials.

### Oct 8 — eXpCon Salt Lake City (stories)
- Gogo and Duane attended eXpCon in Salt Lake City; Gogo marked "I can't believe I've been here for 8 years already" (consistent with joining eXp in 2018).
- She played in the **Agent Games** on the general-session stage, "OGs vs. the New Kids on the Block," to raise money for **Extend a Hand**, with a stated goal of **$50,000**. A teammate noted the OGs "raised double" in the first round.
- Her day: three general sessions, two podcasts, three parties, plus a "wheel of social media" stage session with Keegan (rapid-fire questions, unscripted). She framed it as "a true day of a real estate rockstar."
- Her eXpCon refrain to the crowd: "Let's make work FUN." Another story card read: "You have the power to shape your own destiny."
- Lunch with eXp friends and business partners; a teammate-turned-brokerage-owner and a couple who "changed careers" rather than retired (husband started a mortgage business).

### Oct 8 — "This is what a good sponsor does" (stories)
- At eXpCon, three #teamgogo agents taught concurrent breakout sessions, and Gogo moved room to room, about 20 minutes in each, to support all of them, including a class on working across Middle Eastern cultures. Her reason: "because this is what a good sponsor does."
- She introduced a high-level teaching session by asking attendees to write down questions and leaving 20 to 30 minutes at the end for Q&A.
- She celebrated her Canadian business partner [[Carrie Soave]] ("Agent 3.0 – Powered by AI") speaking at the conference.
- Her team story card: "We have so many different cultures in @teamgogofamily. This is exactly why we call ourselves the #teamgogo International Family of Agents."

### Oct 9 — "Turn one idea into 20 pieces of content" (caption, Reel, stories)
- Last day of eXpCon: Gogo and @learnwithrene (Rene Rodriguez) shared the stage for **"How to turn one idea into 20 pieces of content"** (Friday 11am, Room 151 A-G). Promoted in a caption ([permalink](https://www.instagram.com/p/DeRp8aCzfVG/)), a Reel ([permalink](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DeRz6INBScu/)) and stories.
- An attendee's reshare of her stage talk quoted her: "Instagram is my full time job," read as the mindset of building a brand that makes people chase you instead of chasing leads.
- Wrap-up story: "So many agents, so many takeaways, so many people I got to talk to, so many lives changed." Agent Games stories joked that the "NKOTB" (New Kids on the Block) "are going down."

### Oct 9–10 — Gary Vee's keynote and the posting cadence (caption)
- Gary Vaynerchuk keynoted eXpCon 2026. Gogo called it "a bit more personal to me": "Through the years I got so much s.it for how much I post. But I always knew this is what I suppose to be doing. And Gary confirmed you can't post ENOUGH!" She noted he posts 900+ times a day and said she has "some catching upping to do."
- Her cadence for agents, as taught in [[GoGet'Em Community]]: **1-3x a day into the feed** (cross-posted to every platform); **10-30x a day into stories** (let people get to know you, and share links to your website, calendar or an invite); then **keyword automations** and **email follow-ups** (she says she'll send over 7 million emails this year).
- Why: "Content reaches people - people buy houses, licensed people join brokerages... the more u post the higher your chances they will chose YOU! Simple." ([permalink](https://www.instagram.com/p/DeUpZRWG3nP/))
- Her wheel-of-real-estate stage game on social media marketing ([permalink](https://www.instagram.com/p/DeSnlZbGwRl/)): "Social media marketing is super simple guys! Just take action! Grab your phone and post."

### Oct 9 — Agent Games result (caption)
- "Technically the #exp OGs lost to the NKOTB based on points. But if you ask me, we WON! We raised way more money for Extend a Hand fundraiser!!!" ([permalink](https://www.instagram.com/p/DeSuqcBm6HZ/))

### Oct 10 — Home from eXpCon (story)
- Gogo and Duane worried about the hurricane news while in Salt Lake City and flew home a day early on a red-eye "kind of around the storm," because "we can't have a child home by himself through a storm." Safe at home: "EXPCon was amazing. I miss you all."
- A story on sitting in on other leaders' sessions: you "learn things you would've never learned elsewhere" and she loves "having people who have accomplished huge things share their expertise... so I can become better myself."

### Oct 10 — Why eXp over a localized brokerage (Reel)
- Her pitch to agents not at eXp ([permalink](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DeUqR9TTSSc/)): a localized brokerage means no referrals from agents out of your area, trainings from one more local agent telling you what they do, and no financially beneficial community. In #teamgogo "we train nationally by the best," with thousands training together multiple times a week plus in-person events.
- On costs: paying "north of $20K+ yearly" is "a LIE that you have been buying for years! You can earn your cap back at eXp and have a retirement plan, like no other brokerage."

### Oct 10 — The oil painting: legacy and work family (Reel)
- Gogo taught at a Christian women's real estate event on building wealth, providing for family and leaving something behind, anchored in **1 Timothy 5:8**. Tamra Minch of #teamgogo, who was visiting while deciding whether to join eXp, captured the moment and had it made into an oil painting, shipped to Gogo's door a couple of weeks later ([permalink](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DeU0QmsTD6b/)).
- Her reason for the work: "building wealth has a purpose. I want the work I do today to make a difference for my children and the generations after them." The painting goes in her office "where I'll get to see it every day and remember who and why I work so hard for."
- On #teamgogo culture: beyond coaching and training, "we also pray for one another. We show up for each other. We celebrate each other's wins and stick together through the hard stuff. Our shared Christian faith is such a meaningful part of those relationships."
- Team detail: she sent new ICON agents a congratulations gift and note: "only 6% of the agents at eXp are ICON," and asked them to post and tag her and the team.

### Oct 11 — Comparison and 413 agents (stories)
- Proud of attracting 413 front-line agents in eight years at eXp, she acted out a Grant Cardone-style retort ("95,000 followers... only 413") and said "I need to 10X this." Her lesson: "our excitement only works until we decide who we compare ourselves to." About 84% of eXp agents have never brought in an agent (16% have); against them she is a baller, against Cardone she has work to do.

### Oct 11 — Faith, family and work ethic (stories)
- Sunday at The Chapel: a story on 1 Thessalonians 5:16-18 ("Rejoice always, pray without ceasing, give thanks in all circumstances"), and from the sermon, praying daily keeps communication open to hear God's answers.
- Home from eXpCon in time for son Duke's homecoming, with Duane noting "Grandpa was present." A happy-birthday story for a #teamgogo agent.
- Prepping the Airbnb for a balcony crew: "Don't ever be too good to work hard. Respect money, so it respects you back."
- On follower DMs: "Messages like this keep me going... I believe they are messengers."

### Oct 11 — The HVAC shutoff (story)
- A comic "Dumb and Dumber" report with Duane: she poured vinegar into the Airbnb's HVAC condensation line (do it monthly, or every other month, to keep it clear), but the line's shutoff switch sat at an angle, backfilled and cut power to the thermostat and everything else. Her silver lining: three episodes of a show and a load of laundry. Real-life proof she and Duane maintain their own rental.

### Oct 12 — "You're using ChatGPT wrong" (caption)
- Most agents use ChatGPT like a search bar ("like buying a Ferrari to drive to the mailbox"). The bigger use is delegating the small tasks that eat half the day: filling out online forms, updating the CRM, building follow-up plans, logging activities, organizing contacts, posting on social. [[GoGet'Em Community]] brought in an AI trainer for a live session to show it ([permalink](https://www.instagram.com/p/DeZgvE_z2_T/)).
- Her sequence for getting work off her plate: "First I hired people, then we built systems, then we automated as much as possible. And now AI is opening up a whole new level of what's possible." Her fear: agents "doing everything manually while other agents are figuring out how to get the same work done in a fraction of the time."

### Oct 12 — "You got into real estate to buy your way out of it" (caption)
- Closings are capital; "what you build with the selling years is the whole point." The goal is to get out of "the part where every month starts at zero and the only thing keeping the lights on is you showing up again."
- Example from a coached agent: a digital product that earns off the calendar, agents she leads whose production grows her income past her own hours, and a coaching program that pays for the path she walked. "Her ceiling stopped being tied to her calendar."
- "The wheel doesn't stop on its own. You have to build something that runs without you on it." ([permalink](https://www.instagram.com/p/DeaHeNRkm8l/))
- **Oct 13 – "I love hard."** On a Reel: "It's hard I know. But I love hard. I love winning. I love proving someone wrong... I love if it never was done before. I'll be the 1st to do it then." ([permalink](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DebzlPITxjb/))
- **Oct 13 – Get in the room.** Two #teamgogo agents topped September's agent-attraction leaderboard; both are Circle members, both came to her Private Room mastermind, both flew to eXpCon. Her read: invest in yourself, show up, be around people who think bigger, then go home and *do* something with it. "It's pretty hard to build a bigger business while constantly talking yourself out of the opportunities that could help you grow one." ([permalink](https://www.instagram.com/p/DecE8jvG2M1/))
- **Oct 13 – No-shows get blacklisted.** She permanently blacklists no-shows with no text or call: people wait weeks for her calendar, and a hold-up of four seconds to cancel is too much to ask. She used to reschedule and make exceptions, "teaching them that my time was negotiable." Her team now handles no-shows so she never has the uncomfortable conversation; one exception ever, for a woman who chased her across a room of thousands to own the miss. Systems and people that hold boundaries for you are part of what she builds with members in [[The Circle]]. ([permalink](https://www.instagram.com/p/DecwHRekmX4/))
- **Oct 13 – Bringing in the best.** #teamgogo brings top industry trainers to its agents (a guest training from eXp's #1 producing solo agent on Oct 14). Her line: "Show up. Take notes. Steal everything." ([permalink](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DecORVoTzR7/))
- **Oct 13 – Pink Pen.** She launched "Pink Pen," a long-form LinkedIn newsletter: "my thoughts on different subjects in a looooooooooong format, cause I have a LOT to say." Early editions include the no-show blacklist piece (story).

### Oct 14 — Pray over your business (Reel)
- "Pray over your business the same way you pray over everything and anyone else. Let this be your reminder today!" ([permalink](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DeeZ7W1zuak/))

### Oct 14 — "How lucky are we that we get to stress over millions of dollars?" (Reel)
- After a Circle call on the pressure of owning a business: stress doesn't vanish with success, because "the decisions get bigger. More money. More people counting on you." People stop growing "because they've reached a level of pressure they don't know how to handle yet."
- Gratitude and overwhelm coexist: "I remember wanting what I'm responsible for today. I prayed for the opportunities that now come with decisions that keep me up at night... I can be overwhelmed AND grateful."
- You need a room where you can say "this is hard" without being judged for making good money: "Your best friend can love you and still have absolutely no idea what you're carrying." She describes [[The Circle]] as feeling like family, with an average member income of $700,000 (her figure), "women who understand your ambition, love you through the hard parts, and can celebrate your success without you shrinking it first." ([permalink](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DeebWOXzOD0/))

### Oct 14 — Commissions fund your life or your freedom (caption)
- "Every commission either funds your life or funds your freedom. It doesn't do both at the same time, you choose which one it becomes the second it hits your account." Treat the next deal as fuel, not the destination: "You don't park at the gas station."
- Her own income sources: rev share, stocks, real estate investments, digital products, affiliate income, coaching and speaking, "that I no longer have to sell." On "you don't even sell anymore": "That's supposed to sting. It doesn't. It means it worked." ([permalink](https://www.instagram.com/p/Dee9A0NGhHV/))

### Oct 14 — The 21-year-old immigrant girl (Reel)
- "In 2003, I left Csíkszereda, Transylvania, and came to America with $200 in my pocket. Hungarian was my first language, Romanian my second, and English? Well, we were going to have to figure that one out." No contacts, no money, "certainly... [no] big master plan," but "work ethic, a dream, and enough stubbornness."
- Today: 9 companies, multiple Two Comma Club awards, and #teamgogo at over 1,700 agents. "You don't need to come from money to make money... But you DO need to show up. Over and over and OVER again, even when nobody is watching, nobody is clapping." Then surround yourself with people who believe in where you're going, "not just where you've been." ([permalink](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DefhxVlPUFq/))

### Oct 14 — Live Zoom swap (stories)
- She cancelled a Michigan in-person presentation because she wasn't feeling well enough to travel, offered a live Zoom instead, and delivered it Oct 14 for a Ditch the Heels virtual session ("Bet On You," with Kim Agemy): "I showed #realtors today how to make stupid money and live life by design." Her take: "I think I blew some minds."

### Oct 15 — Highest tax bracket, new problems (Reel)
Joking that a "dude" fixes problems by slowing down and making less money, she drops the punchline: look up the 2026 tax brackets, "we fall into the highest one." When you'd owe 37% to Uncle Sam you buy houses too, and five years in "you created yourself ANOTHER problem. So much for #financialfreedom." Her line: "Different levels, different devils." ([permalink](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DefpsuBTU3s/))

### Oct 15 — Why she stopped selling houses (caption)
She loved selling and was "really good at it," but asked whether she'd still be "showing houses on weekends" and "answering calls during family dinners" at 60, and "starting every January wondering where my next commission check is coming from." Today she helps agents grow businesses, build brands, attract agents and add income streams, "and eventually have the CHOICE to step away from production." Among the 1,700+ agents in #teamgogo, "the goal was never to build 1,700 little versions of me": some want to be top producers, some huge organizations, some just to "pick their kids up from school without checking their phones every five minutes." ([permalink](https://www.instagram.com/p/Deh5xQvEsuQ/))

### Oct 15 — Credit score is part of adulting (story)
She keeps her credit score above 800 and checks her numbers monthly: "What you don't track, you don't manage." Her rule: "No bank or lending institution would say no to you with a healthy credit. And when you build wealth you NEVER use your own money."

### Oct 16 — Why she started @teamgogofamily (Reel, story)
Prompted by a #teamgogo agent's public post that he had just closed his **4th #teamgogo referral deal this year**, Gogo told the origin story: "When I got licensed in #realestate there was no community... Us agents, were in each other's back yards. We were competition to each other. The top producer had no interest in helping me." Joining #exp changed that: agents from every state and country "come together and learn from each other and support each other by sending their nationwide referrals to each other." "Nothing makes me happier than when I see our community truly caring for each other." She offers the teamgogo referral map to any agent who asks, and invites unhappy agents to DM her. She reshared the agent's thank-you in her story, calling @teamgogofamily "the BEST real estate work family in the industry." ([permalink](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Deie-l3TXu_/))

### Oct 16 — From terrified of flying to faith (Reel)
She used to cross the plane door and pray with her eyes closed until 10,000 feet. A retired teacher beside her asked, "Do you believe in God?... Then why do you only believe in Him on the ground?" Gogo: "You only have FEAR, when you have no FAITH." She still crosses the plane door, "except now it's a blessing, not a panic attack." Now "when God gives me an idea, I don't question it. I GO. Fully packed semi truck, straight into the brick wall." Closing challenge: "Do you have the balls to act on it?" ([permalink](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DekBK4izPQl/))

### Oct 16 — Let the scoreboard fire the fifth agent (caption)
A team-leader system: five agents compete for one seat on the team, transactions pick the winner, and the other four move to solo positions. Everyone knows the rules on day one ("Same leads, same support, same real shot. Whoever closes the most keeps the seat."). "You're not the judge anymore. You're just the one who set up the game." She offers to help coaching clients build a local team and a downline or support team, working "TOGETHER for a year." ([permalink](https://www.instagram.com/p/DekYV0IEkcY/))

### Oct 16 — Gma's cabin and a Gilded Age daydream (captions, stories)
- Gogo and Duane bought a dated Michigan lake cabin, "Gma's cabin," as a long-term rental, fell in love on the visit and decided to keep it as a **short-term rental** they can also use, to escape Florida's hot summers. She asked followers to help pick cozy finishes ("Warm! Fireplace! Crockpot dinner. Candles. Heavy curtains. Blankets"), polling upper cabinet colors (Retreat Green vs Cloud Gray) and dark vs light countertops in stories, plus before/after bathroom shots. ([permalink](https://www.instagram.com/p/DeiNWUBGyIN/))
- She's watching *The Gilded Age* and had Muse make images of herself in the era: "I must have lived a very good life in that era, cause I miss it. My soul loves everything about it." ([permalink](https://www.instagram.com/p/Dejjy0im1Nf/))
