# Instagram — 2026-10 (Gogo Bethke)

> Meaningful captions and stories from @gogosrealestate, ingested daily. Raw: [[Clippings/instagram/2026-10.md]] and [[Clippings/instagram/stories/2026-10.md]]. Covers 2026-10-08 through 2026-10-10 so far.

## Key Takeaways

### Oct 8 — "Amy and I met on Instagram" (caption)
- Amy Gregory reached out to Gogo on Instagram about eXp **6 years ago**; Gogo told her why she switched and Amy switched too. They became business partners and good friends, and "each retired our hubbies" (both husbands now work with them instead of in corporate America).
- Both are top producers and top agent attractors **through social media marketing alone**: "Neither one of us ever bought a lead or cold called an agent."
- Amy was speaking on the big stage at eXpCon on how to run your business like a CEO; Gogo called it a "proud mom moment" in her stories.
- Takeaway she pushes to the next generation: build your brand and run it for free on socials.

### Oct 8 — eXpCon Salt Lake City (stories)
- Gogo and Duane attended eXpCon in Salt Lake City; Gogo marked "I can't believe I've been here for 8 years already" (consistent with joining eXp in 2018).
- She played in the **Agent Games** on the general-session stage, "OGs vs. the New Kids on the Block," to raise money for **Extend a Hand**, with a stated goal of **$50,000**. A teammate noted the OGs "raised double" in the first round.
- Her day: three general sessions, two podcasts, three parties, plus a "wheel of social media" stage session with Keegan (rapid-fire questions, unscripted). She framed it as "a true day of a real estate rockstar."
- Her eXpCon refrain to the crowd: "Let's make work FUN." Another story card read: "You have the power to shape your own destiny."
- Lunch with eXp friends and business partners; a teammate-turned-brokerage-owner and a couple who "changed careers" rather than retired (husband started a mortgage business).

### Oct 8 — "This is what a good sponsor does" (stories)
- At eXpCon, three #teamgogo agents taught concurrent breakout sessions, and Gogo moved room to room, about 20 minutes in each, to support all of them, including a class on working across Middle Eastern cultures. Her reason: "because this is what a good sponsor does."
- She introduced a high-level teaching session by asking attendees to write down questions and leaving 20 to 30 minutes at the end for Q&A.
- She celebrated her Canadian business partner [[Carrie Soave]] ("Agent 3.0 – Powered by AI") speaking at the conference.
- Her team story card: "We have so many different cultures in @teamgogofamily. This is exactly why we call ourselves the #teamgogo International Family of Agents."

### Oct 9 — "Turn one idea into 20 pieces of content" (caption, Reel, stories)
- Last day of eXpCon: Gogo and @learnwithrene (Rene Rodriguez) shared the stage for **"How to turn one idea into 20 pieces of content"** (Friday 11am, Room 151 A-G). Promoted in a caption ([permalink](https://www.instagram.com/p/DeRp8aCzfVG/)), a Reel ([permalink](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DeRz6INBScu/)) and stories.
- An attendee's reshare of her stage talk quoted her: "Instagram is my full time job," read as the mindset of building a brand that makes people chase you instead of chasing leads.
- Wrap-up story: "So many agents, so many takeaways, so many people I got to talk to, so many lives changed." Agent Games stories joked that the "NKOTB" (New Kids on the Block) "are going down."

### Oct 9–10 — Gary Vee's keynote and the posting cadence (caption)
- Gary Vaynerchuk keynoted eXpCon 2026. Gogo called it "a bit more personal to me": "Through the years I got so much s.it for how much I post. But I always knew this is what I suppose to be doing. And Gary confirmed you can't post ENOUGH!" She noted he posts 900+ times a day and said she has "some catching upping to do."
- Her cadence for agents, as taught in [[GoGet'Em Community]]: **1-3x a day into the feed** (cross-posted to every platform); **10-30x a day into stories** (let people get to know you, and share links to your website, calendar or an invite); then **keyword automations** and **email follow-ups** (she says she'll send over 7 million emails this year).
- Why: "Content reaches people - people buy houses, licensed people join brokerages... the more u post the higher your chances they will chose YOU! Simple." ([permalink](https://www.instagram.com/p/DeUpZRWG3nP/))
- Her wheel-of-real-estate stage game on social media marketing ([permalink](https://www.instagram.com/p/DeSnlZbGwRl/)): "Social media marketing is super simple guys! Just take action! Grab your phone and post."

### Oct 9 — Agent Games result (caption)
- "Technically the #exp OGs lost to the NKOTB based on points. But if you ask me, we WON! We raised way more money for Extend a Hand fundraiser!!!" ([permalink](https://www.instagram.com/p/DeSuqcBm6HZ/))

### Oct 10 — Home from eXpCon (story)
- Gogo and Duane worried about the hurricane news while in Salt Lake City and flew home a day early on a red-eye "kind of around the storm," because "we can't have a child home by himself through a storm." Safe at home: "EXPCon was amazing. I miss you all."
- A story on sitting in on other leaders' sessions: you "learn things you would've never learned elsewhere" and she loves "having people who have accomplished huge things share their expertise... so I can become better myself."

### Oct 10 — Why eXp over a localized brokerage (Reel)
- Her pitch to agents not at eXp ([permalink](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DeUqR9TTSSc/)): a localized brokerage means no referrals from agents out of your area, trainings from one more local agent telling you what they do, and no financially beneficial community. In #teamgogo "we train nationally by the best," with thousands training together multiple times a week plus in-person events.
- On costs: paying "north of $20K+ yearly" is "a LIE that you have been buying for years! You can earn your cap back at eXp and have a retirement plan, like no other brokerage."

### Oct 10 — The oil painting: legacy and work family (Reel)
- Gogo taught at a Christian women's real estate event on building wealth, providing for family and leaving something behind, anchored in **1 Timothy 5:8**. Tamra Minch of #teamgogo, who was visiting while deciding whether to join eXp, captured the moment and had it made into an oil painting, shipped to Gogo's door a couple of weeks later ([permalink](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DeU0QmsTD6b/)).
- Her reason for the work: "building wealth has a purpose. I want the work I do today to make a difference for my children and the generations after them." The painting goes in her office "where I'll get to see it every day and remember who and why I work so hard for."
- On #teamgogo culture: beyond coaching and training, "we also pray for one another. We show up for each other. We celebrate each other's wins and stick together through the hard stuff. Our shared Christian faith is such a meaningful part of those relationships."
- Team detail: she sent new ICON agents a congratulations gift and note: "only 6% of the agents at eXp are ICON," and asked them to post and tag her and the team.
