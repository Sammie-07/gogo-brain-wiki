# Kirby Chan

Toronto-area ([[eXp Realty]]) listing agent and guest trainer in [[GoGet'Em Community]]; teaches ranking on Google for listing niches and getting **cited by AI** (ChatGPT / Gemini / Claude). Runs the "AI SEO Boot Camp," which doubles as a revenue-share recruiting funnel.

## Overview

Kirby Chan built a listing-first business by dominating a **smaller, winnable geography** (Richmond Hill, ~10k agents) instead of the saturated metro (Toronto, ~100k), and by engineering content so both Google and AI assistants recommend him. He credits the system for reaching eXp ICON faster and for scaling a co-sponsored downline.

## Key Facts

- **Listing-first, ~3 hrs/day, "book one listing appointment a day."** Reached eXp **ICON** in ~6 months (vs. 9 the prior year).
- Businesses: Kirby Chan & Co. Real Estate Team; **Team Forward** (his revenue-share org); **Kirby Chan Digital**.
- **AI SEO / GEO thesis:** win one niche + geography keyword cluster; Google's "Helpful Content" update lets a thin, genuinely-helpful site outrank Zillow/Redfin. Then use **"parasite SEO"** (daily "best agent in [city]" posts on LinkedIn / Rate My Agent) so ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude **cite you** when asked for the best agent.
- **AI content pipeline:** Claude writes scripts → HeyGen AI twin (or Captions.ai) voices them → AI editor adds B-roll (~5 min/video), run on separate **niche Instagram** accounts so the main brand stays real. Also teaches **foreign-language SEO** (write/voice content in a second language for near-zero competition).
- **Reviews:** drip ~1/week (never bulk — Google flags spikes), ask everyone including vendors, photo reviews rank stronger.
- **Attraction engine:** the "$15 AI SEO Boot Camp" recruits the #4/#5-ranked agent in *sister* cities → co-sponsor into eXp. Cited growth: 10 (2024) → 21 → 146 agents.

## Second training — "One Listing Appointment a Day" (Aug 2026)

*Source: [[The AI System for One Listing Appointment a Day (Kirby Chan)]] — execution playbook.*

- **Results stated:** #1 individual producer in Ontario (July 2024), top-3 Richmond Hill, Toronto Star Platinum; **3× ICON**, ~**50 deals/year on ~3 hrs/day**, team of **~40**, **595% rev-share increase** last year (downline 10 → 21 → then attraction-only).
- **Reverse prospecting** — the whole system is "get people who already want to sell to *call me*" (gave up SEO 15 yrs ago vs. Remax/Zillow → FB ads + ISA + door-knocking → rebuilt on AI-ranking to win his time back; 3 kids).
- **Build order:** one niche at a time (downsizing → upsizing → first-time → probate → divorce), **one asset a day** (blog/article/press release/video, ~1 hr), each niche = an evergreen **service page** + supporting **blogs**. Sites: own `yourname.com`, or **eXp Lofty + ~$39.50/mo** blogs/SEO feature, or an **AI text-to-website builder** (~$99/mo, hosting incl., ~2.5-min AI-searchable sites).
- **The Claude sister-city cheat:** paste a sister city's top-1–3 ranking agent pages into [[Claude]] → "copy the good, fix the SEO, write my city's article to my standards."
- **GBP tactics:** put **city + Realtor in the name field** (not a slogan); target a **winnable smaller city**; **reviews with keywords** beat review count; set **hours to 24h**; fill every field + AI-rewrite the **description** with target keywords; **hide the home address** (toggle off) rather than edit it; the **"known as"/DBA workaround** for the June/July 2026 US Google name-field rule.
- **One website, many money-pages** (referral/seller/buyer/niche/open-house), each with its own link. **Ranking = agent attraction** — recruit the agents ranking #2–7 below you; teach the downline to rank so they earn commissions and you earn rev share ("Co with Kirby," mirroring Gogo's co-sponsorship).
