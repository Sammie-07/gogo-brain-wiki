# Nathan (Synthetic Echo podcast)

Former Toronto-area real estate broker turned full-time AI educator; GoGet'Em Community guest presenter on browser-controlling AI agents. (Surname not given in the source.)

## Overview
Licensed 16 years northeast of Toronto; managed an office and ran a team. Now "fully AI": hosts the *Synthetic Echo* podcast and teaches a 4-week cohort course on building Grok-based bot teams, offered to [[GoGet'Em Community]] members through Gogo's affiliate link. Met [[Gogo Bethke]] and [[Kristy Waker]] at Project 42 in Canada.

## Key Facts
- Runs ~26–28 single-job bots (chief of staff, email triage, social media, research, real estate, podcast booking).
- Teaches: treat AI like a new hire, build one block at a time, turn working workflows into portable skills, approve output while the bot learns.
