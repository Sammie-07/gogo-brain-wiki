# Leila Modetz

Michigan real estate agent and GoGet'Em Community guest presenter on negotiation objections; known for value-stacking and heavy client communication.

## Overview
Panelist on the Oct 2026 [[GoGet'Em Community]] objection-handling game show alongside [[Curt Shewell]] and [[Austin Rankin]]. Charges 3% on both buyer and listing sides and handles any buyer-commission gap up front.

## Key Facts
- Marketing stack she sells against discount brokerages: a paid local videographer and photographer, Zillow Showcase, luxury marketing, Homes.com 3D scans, open houses and door knocking.
- Sends sellers weekly written updates with comps and showing feedback, so sellers usually propose price reductions themselves.
- Matches buyer-agreement terms to the buyer's real time frame (3 months while browsing, 6 months when a lease ends).
