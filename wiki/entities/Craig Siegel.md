# Craig Siegel

WSJ and USA Today bestselling author, performance coach, and host of *The CLS Experience* podcast. Speaker on identity transformation and energetic expansion.

## Overview

Craig Siegel is a former Wall Street trader turned mindset and performance coach. He is the author of *The Reinvention Formula* and hosts *The CLS Experience*, a top-ranked podcast. He has run 9 marathons. He met Gogo Bethke at a Dallas event, and she brought him into the GoGet'Em community.

His core teaching: identity — not effort, tactics, or market conditions — determines your ceiling. Until you change who you are at the identity level, behavior changes are temporary. He integrates Kabbalah (specifically the concept of expanding the vessel to receive more light) into practical business coaching.

## Key Concepts

- **Energetic set point:** Each person has an invisible ceiling that caps their results. Effort without raising this set point produces diminishing returns.
- **Expanding the vessel (Kabbalah):** You must increase your capacity to receive before more can arrive. Cannot pour more water into a vessel that isn't larger.
- **Quantum leaps:** Transformation is not linear. When identity shifts, results can jump dramatically without proportional increase in effort.
- **Identity over strategy:** The strategy is rarely the problem. The person executing it is.

## Self-Image Framework (2nd training)

His "Expand Your Self-Image, Expand Your Life" session made the ceiling concept concrete:

- **Self-image sets the boundaries for individual accomplishment.** "You might not have a business problem — you might have a self-image problem."
- **The loop:** self-image → interpretation → action → result → **evidence → back to self-image.** We respond to our interpretation of reality, then use the result as proof the belief was true. "That's not who you are, that's who you've *rehearsed*." "Familiar is not the same thing as true."
- **The "area of possible":** a circle holding what feels *realistically* available to you. Most people chase more *inside* the circle (new CRM, new funnel, more calls); the work is to **expand the circle.** "You can't optimize your way around an internal limitation."
- **Don't lower the bar — expand the person under it.** Better than "what if I fail?": "who could I become by attempting something I'm not guaranteed to succeed at?" Rather occasionally fail at your potential than consistently succeed beneath it.
- **Sometimes self-image is too *old*, not too small** — trying to build a future through an outdated picture of yourself.
- **The merchant parable:** you can't build the second store as the person the first store required. **Embody it before the evidence arrives** — become someone capable of stewarding what you asked for.
