# Vija Williams

Chief Growth Officer at Realtor.com (seller and cash-offer leads); taught #teamgogo's Nov 4, 2026 training on converting cash-offer seller leads.

## Overview
Seattle-based agent and eXp agent. Co-founder of Activated Agent and Do North Collective; former head of industry at Place and president of Ben Kinney's brokerages (as introduced by [[Kristy Waker]]). Sells cash-offer leads through Realtor.com and discloses it; her team buys them too. Reachable by Instagram DM.

## Key Facts
- Core message: agents went "from the first call to the last opinion." Get in front of homeowners early, be first ("you don't have to be best"), and win on follow-up.
- Teaches the golden minute (contact within 60–120 seconds), a respond-to-the-request opener instead of a brokerage intro, 3+1 pre-qual questions, and 24–72 hour appointments.
- Pushes "multiple exits": a packaged menu of listing programs (traditional, as-is investor special, renovation paid at closing, iBuyer such as Homeward or Zudelio/Express Offers, local investors).
- Her 2008 lesson: she missed the short-sale market because her brokerage steered her away from it.
