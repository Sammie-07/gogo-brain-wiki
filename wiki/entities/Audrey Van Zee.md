# Audrey Van Zee

Community leader at HeyGen (AI avatar and video company); taught #teamgogo's Oct 2026 Open to the Public on AI twins and avatar videos.

## Overview
Runs HeyGen's onboarding and webinar trainings (two years at the company as of Oct 2026). Doesn't like being on camera and uses her own avatar. Gave Team Gogo a first-month-free code and a deck of real estate prompt templates.

## Key Facts
- Teaches the three-part avatar (motion, looks, voice), "when in doubt, go back to neutral" filming, Video Agent for drafts and AI Studio for fixes, and the Brand Glossary for pronunciation.

- On goals: staying relevant and in front of your audience, not going viral.
