# Jason Dupree

#teamgogo's mortgage partner (Texana Bank) who opens the weekly community calls with a "Mortgage Minutes" market update and runs the REMLO program.

## Overview

Jason Dupree is a loan officer at Texana Bank and the recurring mortgage partner for [[Gogo Bethke]]'s [[GoGet'Em Community]] / [[#teamgogo]] calls. He delivers a short weekly rate/market update at the top of the Wednesday trainings and champions the **REMLO** program, which lets real estate agents become licensed loan originators in all 50 states under the bank's federal charter.

## Key Facts

- Loan officer at **Texana Bank**; recurring "Mortgage Minutes" presenter on #teamgogo calls.
- Runs the weekly **"What is REMLO?"** info session (typically 3pm ET the same day as the Wednesday call); link: gogobethke.com/remlo.
- Spring 2026 rate commentary: average ~6.5–6.65%, down roughly 40 bps from a year prior; repeatedly predicted "the biggest summer in real estate in years."
- Tracks REMLO performance for the team: #teamgogo is the **#1 REMLO group at Texana Bank**; in one month team REMLOs earned $21K+ in lender commission (company-wide REMLO commission $107K+, $21M / 65 units).

## REMLO program (Real Estate Mortgage Loan Originator)

- Agents become licensed loan officers in all 50 states **day one** under the bank charter — no 20-hour course, no SAFE exam (you become a bank employee).
- **Qualification:** 6 buy-side transactions in the prior 12 months.
- **Pay:** ~0.5% of the mortgage amount (~$1,500 on a $300K loan); per-file averages cited at ~$1,600–$2,200. Also earns on HELOCs/bridge loans previously referred out.
- Program topped **450+ agents** (~463 by Jul 2026) nationwide; framed as a "4-year head start" on competitors.
- REMLO-of-the-month examples: Melissa Albert (+$5,800 in a month; nearing ICON), Macy (5 closings, 7th of ~463 REMLOs), J.R. St. Julian, Kelly LaFave, [[Jennifer Worthington]], Morgan Tower.

**Aug 26, 2026 mortgage minute (Kirby Chan / AI-SEO call):** **active inventory up 3.6% YoY — the highest since Nov 2019**; a **buyers' market developing** (heaviest in the Southeast — Florida, Texas); average listing price **down 1.3% YoY to ~$424,500**; mortgage credit **fundamentally healthy** (low defaults, unlike the early-year default worry). Framed it as a rhyme of the late-2019 buyer market that COVID + plunging rates flipped back to a sellers' market ("be ready"). **Coaching pivot — stop cutting price, negotiate seller concessions / temporary rate buydowns:** a $25K price cut saves a buyer only ~**$150/mo**, whereas a **$14K seller concession → 2-1 buydown saves ~$771/mo year 1 and ~$395 year 2**, and a **1-0 buydown (~$4,740) saves ~$395/mo** in year one (the most expensive year of ownership). Temporary buydown = "the bridge over the rainbow" until they refinance (everyone refis within a couple years, so a *permanent* buydown is wasted money). REMLO recap: ~0.5% of the loan (~$1,800–$2,200/closing), 6 buy-side transactions to qualify, full-service agent, Texana, all 50 states.

**Sep 2, 2026 mortgage minute (Halo / AI-website call):** jobs report "blah," rates steady ~2 weeks, no new Middle East escalation — a holding pattern. Opened by tying into Gogo's AI theme with **LendingTree** data: **72% of consumers would use AI for at least one part of buying/selling; 37% would let AI manage the entire home purchase** with minimal human involvement — so agents must provide **Trust + Ease** (break the fourth wall on social; "never a news desk"). **Non-QM is the fastest-growing mortgage segment: $108B last year → ~$175B expected (+62%)** — self-employed / bank-statement / P&L loans, **DSCR** (the big investor product), and **renovation HELOCs** (borrow against post-reno value). DSCR rates can beat Fannie/Freddie on investment properties (the agencies pour resources into owner-occupied). **3-step investor onboarding** to ease a homeowner into investing: (1) start an **LLC** (recommends **Wyoming** — cheap, Delaware-like protections); (2) open a **bank account in the LLC's name** (avoid commingling); (3) start a **HELOC on the primary residence** as down payment (closes in as little as 5 days, but wait to close until under contract so no interest accrues) — DSCR allows closing in the LLC. REMLO recap: full-service agent + licensed lender all 50 states, **~0.5% of the loan (~$1,800–$2,200/file)**.
