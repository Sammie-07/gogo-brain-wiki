# Jesse Dau

Real estate YouTube coach and co-founder of **Channel Junkies**; **7× eXp ICON** (brokerage-agnostic coach who happens to license at eXp). Has done **$450M+ in residential real estate sales** *and* built a **$450M+ YouTube business**; no longer in personal production — refers all leads to his agents. GoGet'Em / #teamgogo guest (Aug 2026, invited by Gogo).

## Overview

Jesse Dau teaches agents to generate inbound clients from **long-form organic YouTube** — no ads, no shorts, no chasing virality. He and his business partner **Jackson** built the model over nearly a decade of trial and error, and now coach ~**100 agents** worldwide, most doing a **minimum of $300K GCI exclusively from YouTube.** He spoke at **Inman** on a social-media panel ("the only agent up there that actually generated sales from my content"). Team: wife **Rachel** (an agent on the channel), agency director **Ken**, and **Perry** (works questions/support), plus Andre, Quinton, Randall.

## Key Facts

- **Rally cry: "We sell homes, not views and subscribers."** Optimize for **average view duration** (the viewer), not view count. An agent with 1,000 subscribers in a small market can out-earn one with 50,000 in a big market.
- **Avatar = relocation buyers** — the most scalable YouTube audience; one relo client is worth ~4 transactions and they buy/sell ~every 3 years.
- **The 3-stage funnel:** top (cost-of-living / pros-and-cons search videos), middle (where-to-live map tours / best-5-suburbs, titled with the **driver city**), bottom (**18–28-min home tours + vlog tours** that trigger browse & suggested traffic — *the* algorithm, not SEO/search). Full method: [[Social Media and Email Marketing#YouTube for Real Estate — Long-Form Organic (Jesse Dau)]].
- **Algorithm checklist:** 1 video/week, 7-min avg view duration (→ 18–28-min videos), 50%+ views from TV, 70%+ browse & suggested, **no ads, no shorts**; "favored" = 15 videos with 50+ views in the last 7 days. Post **Saturday (#1) / Wednesday (#2)**.
- **Anti-AI stance for content:** AI scripts / AI voice / AI avatars get channels throttled ("the plague") — YouTube measures AI visual + language and has removed tens of thousands of AI accounts. "Everyone can smell AI on the scroll." See [[AI for Real Estate#The AI-Content Throttle — "Everyone Can Smell AI on the Scroll" (Jesse Dau)]].
- **Refer-off / out-of-production model:** YouTube is the only platform where the agent needn't close the deal; many clients refer every lead at a **50/50 split** ("if Zillow can charge 30–40%, I can charge 50"), some living abroad / part-time. "A YouTube lead is better than a referral." See [[Lead Generation#YouTube = Relocation-Buyer Pipeline + the Refer-Off Model (Jesse Dau)]].
- **Tools / offers:** **Hyros** lead-attribution tracking; free **Channel Grader** (channeljunkies.com/grader, read-only, Google-authorized); **14-day sprint** (channeljunkies.com/sprint); recurring **5-day challenge** (challengejunkies.com/challenge, promo code **GO**). Coaches Alex Hormozi's brand one-on-one and cited it as a virality-vs-revenue case study.
