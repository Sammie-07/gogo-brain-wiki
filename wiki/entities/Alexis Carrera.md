# Alexis Carrera

Michigan [[eXp Realty]] listing agent and [[Jason Dupree|REMLO]] in [[#teamgogo]]; a recurring GoGet'Em contributor and host. Creator of a **Marketing Services Agreement** ("skin in the game") that shifts listing marketing-cost risk to the client. Instagram: @thealexiscarrera.

## Overview

Alexis Carrera teaches listing-side business protection. After absorbing ~$1,200 in marketing costs when a seller fired her mid-listing, she built a contract so clients — not the agent — bear the marketing-cost risk, framing marketing as an out-of-pocket expense separate from the commission.

## Key Facts

- **Marketing Services Agreement — two options:** (A) agent advances costs and the seller **reimburses documented third-party marketing expenses** on cancellation/withdrawal/termination (optionally **escrowed with a title company** up front to guarantee payment); (B) the **client pays vendors directly** (agent may offer a commission credit at closing).
- **Rules she teaches:** always specify **a dollar amount AND a payment deadline**; clear the agreement with your broker and state before using. "You get the Porsche, you pay for the Porsche" — commission pays for negotiation/representation; marketing is a separate external cost.
- A [[Jason Dupree|REMLO]] agent; active on the #teamgogo Niche Finder and agent-attraction content (shares her process on Instagram).

## CRM & AI (Lofty)

- **Hosted the Aug 12, 2026 GoGet'Em training** on Lofty lead nurture, bringing in her own CRM coach [[Adam Gillespie]] (found him on YouTube, reached out on social). Candid that CRM discipline is a top-producer weak spot: "two months go by and I don't even remember the last thing I did in my CRM."
- **Switched to [[Lofty]] ~a year ago** (as soon as eXp's CRM-of-choice option opened) over BoldTrail — chose the "better one" she'd actually use, after research, because of Lofty's AI capabilities and freedom vs. BoldTrail's "cookie-cutter, blocky" feel. Also moved client texting/calling off her personal number into the CRM.
- **Building her own AI clone** with Adam — trained on her voice/writing via [[Claude]] and ChatGPT, then attached to Lofty so nurture messaging sounds like her ("it's me — the way that I talk, text, sound"). On Adam's advice she **canceled a paid Lofty AI sales-assistant** ($30–50/mo) in favor of building something that's actually her.
