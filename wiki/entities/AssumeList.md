# AssumeList

Assumable-mortgage **system** (search + education + transaction management + mailer tooling) that surfaces homes with sub-5% assumable loans and shepherds the assumption to close. Presented to #teamgogo by Head of Education [[Nora Simpson]]. Co-founders: **Mike Lorino** (CEO) and **Bethany Soldier** — a married Realtor team in Northern Virginia (Nora coached them 40–50 → 80–90 transactions/yr).

## Overview

AssumeList aggregates listings with **assumable FHA/VA/USDA loans below 5%** (many at 2.4–3% from 2020–21), including off-market properties via skip-trace and mailer campaigns, and provides a transaction-management team that handles the lender-side assumption process most agents/consumers can't navigate alone. See [[Assumable Mortgages]] for the underlying mechanics.

## The Five Parts of the System (Sep 2026)

Nora's framing: the search platform is "a really nice part, but not even close to the majority of the value."
1. **Property search** — US-wide, once logged in; custom-polygon map search; on-market + off-market in separate sections.
2. **Client/buyer sub-accounts** — give buyers gated search access (investors can't see off-market data).
3. **Learning Center** — regulation-based "ground truth" (VA vs FHA, closing-cost comparisons, VA entitlement videos) to counter bank-funded misinformation.
4. **Transaction-management team** — shepherds the assumption to close; never a turndown, never >60 days.
5. **Mail-campaign tool** — handwritten, hand-addressed, real-stamp "golden letters" + skip-trace owner contact (email/mobile/landline/Do-Not-Call), with templates.

## Key Facts

- **Scale (Sep 2026):** **57 MLSs across 27 states** (Northwest MLS / WA took >1 year — MLS territorialism + compliance). **Not New York, not Alaska.** Only surfaces assumable loans **below ~5%**. **Off-market data added monthly** for new counties — email the counties you want to **nora@assumelist.com**.
- **eXp enterprise license:** [[eXp Realty|Leo Pareja]] pays for every eXp agent's free account. **983 eXp agents signed up (Sep 2026); "running out of seats soon."** Register at AssumeList.com/eXp with a **daily-expiring code** (by design — agents must attend a Q&A, so they "pay with attention," not money).
- **The four obstacles it solves:** (1) hard to find; (2) bank-funded misinformation; (3) bank obstruction (illegal "we don't do assumptions" runaround → escalated to the **VA Inspector General**, who once threatened a bank's license to service **$1T+** of VA debt → that bank now closes in **≤25 days**); (4) cash-gap financing. See [[Assumable Mortgages]].
- **Manual-underwriting fight:** banks use manual (not automated) underwriting to hunt for a decline and sometimes miscalculate DTI; AssumeList corrects the math and threatens FHA/VA regulatory reports.
- **Preferred lenders:** **Ryan & Kelly at Barrett Financial** (Arizona; licensed in 49 states, not NY) for the second/gap mortgage. Nora has interviewed 150+ lenders and fired 7.
- **VA entitlement calculator on every VA property** (unique): quantifies the 4× buying-power math (see [[Real Estate Math]]).
- **Referral directory:** agents nationwide list a coverage area; Nora routes referrals personally; **AssumeList takes no fee** on agent-to-agent referrals.
- **Nora's daily Q&A:** 5 days/week, 11am ET (homes.assumelist.com/learn); bring buyers/sellers/LOs.
- **Scale (Jul 2026 baseline):** 44,000+ assumable listings across **55 MLSs / 26 states**.
- **eXp enterprise license:** [[eXp Realty|Leo Pareja]] purchased an enterprise license → **every eXp agent gets free access** (otherwise ~$80/mo). Agents issue **buyer sub-accounts** and see all client search activity.
- **Access:** AssumeList.com/exp → "I already have a code" → eXp registration code **BZEUEVKM** → register with business + eXp email. Codes rotate ~every 2 days to drive agents to a live training.
- **Auto-listing:** a property in a covered area (e.g., South Florida / Beaches / Miami MLS) is **auto-added once listed on the MLS**, if its loan is assumable and below 5%.
- **Tools:** VA entitlement calculator (PDF report), second-mortgage calculator, and a preferred lender offering the lowest second-mortgage rates for the equity gap.
- **Fees (paid by buyer/seller, not the agent):** **$2,300 flat TC fee** = **$200 upfront setup** + $2,100 at clear-to-close; **no charge beyond $200 if the assumption isn't approved**. Claim: "never a turndown, never longer than 60 days."
- **Integrations:** Follow Up Boss (live); **BoldTrail** native API in progress. Daily Q&A Mon–Fri 11am ET at homes.assumelist.com/learn; support@assumelist.com.
- **Advocacy:** Mike Lorino has pushed the VA Inspector General to pressure non-compliant servicers that stall legally-required FHA/VA assumptions.
