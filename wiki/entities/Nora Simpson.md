# Nora Simpson

Head of Education for [[AssumeList]] and the recurring #teamgogo presenter who teaches agents how to find, market, and close assumable mortgages.

## Overview

Nora Simpson leads education and the daily Q&A for [[AssumeList]] — the assumable-mortgage system [[eXp Realty|Leo Pareja]] licensed free for all eXp agents. She is a 15+-year sales and mindset coach (18 years coaching realtors, 3,000+ agents) who teaches the **neuroscience of sales and team growth**, and she built AssumeList's Learning Center, mail-campaign tooling, and coaching cadence. Blunt, high-energy, and relentless about pushing agents to act before they feel "ready."

## Key Facts

- **Role:** Head of Education, [[AssumeList]] (not a co-founder). Co-founders are **Mike Lorino** (CEO) and **Bethany Soldier** — a married Realtor team in Northern Virginia whom Nora coached from ~40–50 to ~80–90 transactions/year.
- **Background:** 18 years coaching real estate agents; worked with 3,000+ agents before AssumeList; helped **100+ teams double** their closed deals and revenue. Based in Manhattan; lives in Europe; observes Jewish holidays (no Q&A on Rosh Hashanah).
- **Daily Q&A:** leads a live training **5 days/week, 11am ET** (homes.assumelist.com/learn). Agents may bring buyers, sellers, or loan officers; early arrivals are prioritized. She will **co-host a webinar** for an audience an agent gathers.
- **Coaching stance:** don't wait to become a "master" — a day-one agent already knows more than 99% of the market; get familiar in a week, then "go crazy." Reach out to everyone you know (they know people you don't); use the **"burning platform"** (the cash gap grows every month).
- **Email discipline:** responds only to emails naming the **counties** to add to the monthly off-market download; other emails should CC support@assumelist.com or come to the Q&A.
