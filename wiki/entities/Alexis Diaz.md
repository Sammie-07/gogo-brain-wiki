# Alexis Diaz

Central Florida (Orlando / Ocala) agent and **[[GoGet'Em Community]] member**; runs the **Evolve Estates Group**. Built a **163-transaction / $52M+ business since 2023 (15,000+ YouTube subscribers), essentially 100% from social/YouTube.** Known as **"the I got you girl."** GGTC member-taught guest (Aug 2026).

## Overview

Alexis Diaz teaches the **trust → consultation → client → raving-fan** side of YouTube for real estate — the complement to [[Jesse Dau]]'s algorithm/funnel system. Originally from Staten Island, NY (half Puerto Rican / half Dominican), she moved to Central Florida ~13 years ago, has **6 kids** (3 biological + an adopted sibling group of 3 via foster care), and was a Walt Disney World secretary before becoming an agent 7 years ago during COVID. She started on a team cold-calling (hated it), learned YouTube from a class at her office, and has run a home-tour channel for ~3.5 years. Her editing is handled by a team in Colombia.

## Key Facts

- **Avatar = relocation buyers**, especially New Yorkers moving to Central FL (shared language/culture) — she steers away from hand-holding first-timers.
- **"Views aren't the win — trust is the strategy."** People hire the agent they've binge-watched for months; consistency *compounds* (dependable, not daily). Posts **same day & time** (2×/week, Mon & Tue 8pm) so it feels like a favorite TV show.
- **Signature brand:** the tagline **"look no further, I got you,"** the **cow-vs-buffalo** storm analogy ("I'm your buffalo"), and a **"Monarch School of Real Estate"** end-of-video segment (estimated down payment / closing costs / builder incentives).
- **Authenticity + educate-don't-sell:** leaves bloopers in, calls out builder flaws, turns features into benefits, "be memorable not neutral"; always recommends a **third-party inspection even on new builds** and warns buyers not to visit a builder community without their own agent.
- **Community over audience:** a **weekly YouTube Live for 2 years** (30–60 attendees) with value guests (lender, credit specialist) and open-ended questions; turns viewers into **raving fans** who refer and defend her.
- **The 5-step video blueprint:** hook → intro (who you are + who you help) → tease value → story/tour (with **AI staging** of empty rooms) → low-friction CTA (book the calendar). Plus **measure the business not the views**, **A/B test one thing at a time**, and a **first-30-days plan** (define → 4-video cushion → 1×/week routine → review). Full method: [[Social Media and Email Marketing#Trust → Consultation — YouTube That Converts (Alexis Diaz)]].
- Uses **Claude / ChatGPT** to craft avatar-specific hooks.
