# Eben Pagan

Veteran direct-response / information marketer and former real estate agent; a guest teacher in [[Gogo Bethke]]'s [[GoGet'Em Community]] on email marketing and AI copywriting.

## Overview

Eben Pagan (ebanpagan.com) is an "OG" internet marketer who built a 100%-virtual info-marketing business (~80 employees, ~$30M/yr) starting in 2001, has done an estimated $100M+ in personal lifetime sales, and taught tens of thousands of entrepreneurs. He began his career in real estate before moving online. Gogo met him through a shared coaching program and brought him in as a (non-selling, "retired") guest teacher for GGTC's September email-marketing month.

## Key Facts

- **Core thesis:** *"Nobody follows up"* — the durable edge in any business is consistent follow-up until the lead is ready. Only ~1–3% of leads are ready now; ~50% buy within a year (buyer-readiness model he credits to **Dean Jackson**).
- **Email is the channel:** cheapest, text-only, "the language of business," and it builds a database you *own*. He estimates email drove a 10–20× return over his career.
- **The FF/WA avatar sprint:** map your ideal client's **Fears & Frustrations** vs **Wants & Aspirations**, concretely — and prompt AI for both **toward and away-from motivations** (people are ~2× more loss-averse; **Kahneman & Tversky**).
- **Step-by-step AI newsletter method:** blank chat → dump the avatar → deeper psych profile → **topics first** (~80 → pick favorites) → **"checkpoints/waypoints,"** one desired reader action → outline → 3-page draft → rewrite in your voice → **hand-edit.** ~100× leverage. (Full method in [[Social Media and Email Marketing]] and [[AI for Real Estate]].)
- Influences he cites: Dean Jackson, Joe Stumpf, Napoleon Hill; interviewed by Tony Robbins and Larry King.
