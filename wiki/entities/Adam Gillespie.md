# Adam Gillespie

Denver / NE Colorado [[eXp Realty]] Realtor and **AI & CRM coach**; **2024 Inman AI Award** winner; founder of **Hivo AI** and **Apex Elite AI** (apexeliteai.com); speaker/coach/tech strategist with the **Wolfpack at eXp Realty**. GoGet'Em guest (Aug 2026), brought in by [[Alexis Carrera]] as her CRM coach.

## Overview

Adam Gillespie teaches agents to run [[Lofty]] (and any auto-email/auto-text CRM) so it works *for* them — automating lead nurture while the agent stays the "human in the loop." A self-described geek and ex-touring musician, he came up through call centers and automotive-claims work, went through **10 brokerages and every major CRM since 2017**, and now coaches full-time while staying in production (**8 transactions last year in <60 hours, capped at eXp** — "my CRM does all the heavy lifting"). Certified prompt engineer; certified in AI ethics, machine learning, and LLMs.

## Key Facts

- **The "5 Ingredients to Success Pie"** — the automations every lead gets the moment it enters the CRM (see [[Lead Generation#The 5-Ingredient Nurture System — "Success Pie" (Adam Gillespie)]]): (1) **property alerts** / market reports, (2) **informational email drip** (2/mo × 2 yrs, educate not sell), (3) **automated text campaigns** (get the appointment, 5-min first text, auto-pause on reply), (4) **personality-driven holiday e-cards** (~20/yr, AI-generated), (5) **call schedule** (36 calls / 603 days; avg lead answers on the 7th attempt).
- **Doctrine:** "The lead is not the win. The booked appointment is the win." **AI = back of house, human = front of house** — never let AI voice/chatbots talk to leads; use AI to draft the messaging, then jump in personally. Kill the canned Lofty library smart plans ("canned spam"); **clone yourself into a custom GPT** trained on your voice.
- **Lofty > BoldTrail:** Lofty is built for the individual agent and non-techy people (smart plans are a simple trigger→wait→action flow chart, built-in co-pilot AI, unlimited landing pages, custom-HTML websites); BoldTrail/KVCore is clunky and built for brokerages. Not employed by Lofty (turned down job offers so he can keep criticizing it).
- **Coaching = "done-with-you"** — weekly 60–90-min one-on-one sessions building smart plans inside your Lofty until you no longer need him. Free "Getting Started with Lofty" series on his YouTube channel; sells a **white-label Lofty** (~$100/mo under Lofty's direct ~$399/mo). Sponsor: **Jeremy Kane** (5-time Icon, 40–60 transactions/yr, never bought a lead).
- Uses **Gemini** for AI holiday-card images, **HeyGen** for video translation, custom GPTs for multi-language client comms, **Gamma.app** for decks, and vibe-codes custom tools (branded mortgage calculator) on ChatGPT.
