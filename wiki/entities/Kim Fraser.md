# Kim Fraser

Top-producing eXp Realty solo agent (introduced on #teamgogo as eXp's #1 solo agent), 20+ years in the business, known for farming, open houses and listing marketing.

## Overview
Featured guest at the Oct 14, 2026 [[#teamgogo]] training. Licensed in Washington, Arizona and Texas. Built her business on three lanes: farming, open houses and past clients. [[Gogo Bethke]] first met her at an eXp corporate event in New Orleans.

## Key Facts
- Farms 4 communities with ~15,000 mailers a month; 20 of her 52 listings in 2026 came from the farm.
- Prints in-house on a leased commercial printer, cutting monthly marketing spend from $15–16K to under $8K.
- Holds 80–90 open houses a year with up to 20 directional signs.
- Sold 101 homes in 2005 and 19 in 2009. eXp volume: $81M (2021), $57M (2022), $36M (2023), $52M (2024), $57M (2025), $110–120M projected (2026).
- First month at eXp (July 2021): two $4M listings sold to buyers from her open houses.
- Runs a lean family micro-team (husband on showings, daughter as transaction coordinator) rather than a traditional team; a living example of the [[Real Estate Math|solo-agent percentile ladder]].
- Sep 2026 (#teamgogo Roundtable): cited by Gogo as eXp's #1 solo agent worldwide; ~$81M closed YTD plus ~$18M pending.
- Signature lines: "Do not do anything halfway." "You're not a tree" (on multi-state licensing).
