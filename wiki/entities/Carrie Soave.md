# Carrie Soave

International AI coach, consultant, and speaker; 15-year realtor; official AI adviser to eXp Realty and a Team Gogo agent partner.

## Overview

Carrie Soave is an AI educator who teaches real estate agents and entrepreneurs to move from using AI as a chatbot to building AI *infrastructure* that runs their business. A 15-year realtor, she reports building a $1M business in ~5 months with a single employee by leaning on AI systems. She is an official AI adviser to eXp Realty and a recurring trainer inside Gogo's [[GoGet'Em Community]].

## Key Facts

- **Signature framework:** *"ChatGPT is a Conversation. Claude is a Command Center."* — positions ChatGPT as a superior chatbot but Claude as an AI *operating system* for business automation.
- Teaches the **Autonomy Spectrum** (Level 0 chatbot → Level 1 assistant w/ connectors → Level 2 agentic Cowork/Dispatch → Level 3 fully autonomous scheduled agents).
- **Operators vs. Spectators** thesis: operators own infrastructure and scale without headcount; spectators rent tools and manually copy-paste — and the gap widens weekly.
- Maps the **five layers of Claude**: Claude Chat + connectors, Claude in Chrome, Claude Cowork (desktop "employee"), Claude Dispatch (mobile), and Claude Code.
- Advocates a **Cowork folder structure** (About Me / Outputs / Projects / Templates) plus global instructions, and markdown files for token efficiency.
- Official **AI adviser to eXp Realty**; built a $1M business in ~5 months with 1 employee using AI.

## The AI-Run Company (Sep 2026 #teamgogo training)

Second appearance — a masterclass on running a real estate business as a company staffed by AI agents (see [[How to Triple Your GCI with AI Agents — The AI-Run Company (Carrie Soave, teamgogo Sep 2026)]]).

- **Departments model:** treat your business as **7 income-producing departments** (Market Intelligence, Marketing & Visibility, Database & Relationships, Lead Conversion, Sales, Transaction Delivery, Client Expansion) + **4 capacity-protecting departments** (Operations, Finance, HR, Management). Her own company runs ~9 departments at ~$250K/month with 3–4 humans; won't hire another human until $500K/month.
- **Implementation gap:** *"82% of agents use AI, only 17% say it made an impact — we don't have an adoption problem, we have an implementation problem."*
- **"Don't build on rented land":** the model layer is volatile, so your systems, context, data and guardrails must live **outside any single model** (desktop + personal cloud) so you can swap models without downtime.
- **Build-your-AI-brain exercise:** have Claude/ChatGPT interview you about your whole business and generate a personal wiki → save to desktop + cloud → connect your tools → run it as a Cowork/work project that auto-updates the wiki monthly (first autonomous workflow) → then ask for the top 5 highest-impact workflows. (Directly parallels this Obsidian brain.)
- **Client experience is the moat**, delivered at scale by AI; **speed-to-lead** (<5 min) solved with AI voice callers/bots. Cost framing: ~$30K/mo human payroll ≈ $300–$1,000/mo in tech.
- Her content engine (Friday research → Monday generation, human approval gate) is documented in [[Social Media and Email Marketing]]; the departments/rented-land/build-your-brain frameworks in [[AI for Real Estate]].
