# Halo

An AI "marketing agency" for real estate agents that you operate entirely by **text message** — it builds and continuously optimizes AEO/GEO/SEO real estate websites. Founded/architected by [[Eric Post]]; brought to #teamgogo by AI strategist [[Nick Krem]].

## Overview

Halo is not a chatbot — it's a **chat assistant that commands ~30 "AI employees"** (web designer, video production crew, graphic designer, copywriter, researcher, SEO/AEO/GEO strategists, marketing coordinator). You delegate by text ("here's my new listing"; "translate my site to Spanish"; "turn this selfie into a headshot") and it does the work, running through **14 compliance gates** (fair housing, fair lending, broken links, redirects) before anything publishes. It used **51 different AIs in the last 30 days**, choosing the best model per task, so the user needs no APIs or separate subscriptions.

Its core promise is being found by the **AI-powered consumer**: sites are "built by AI for AI," handling `llms.txt`, JSON, and schema so agents surface in ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude answers — the [[AI for Real Estate|GEO/AEO]] reality that most brokerage/CRM sites (which "block AI") miss.

## Key Facts

- **Interface:** text message (also a mirrored web dashboard). ~92 incoming texts/user; ~300–400K messages over 3 months.
- **Pricing:** **$99/mo** = 2 full sites, **1,000 pages**, unlimited edits, unlimited automations. **Free trial via Gogo:** live hosted site + **5 edits** (text "Gogo" / scan the QR).
- **Three site types:** niche, authority, property (landing pages, event & recruiting/downline sites coming).
- **Strategy:** the **hub-and-spoke authority graph** — `yourname.com` as hub, all other pages cross-linked spokes → AI recognizes and recommends you.
- **Marketplace add-ons:** lead quiz (300+ built; top converter), animations, hero video, CRM webhook, GoHighLevel, Zapier, Google Analytics.
- **Also a coding agent + translator:** rebuilds from a pasted sitemap; connect Google Search Console, paste errors, it fixes them; one-toggle full-site translation; 21 style systems; accessibility-ready; auto mobile-optimized.
- **Copyright-safe:** first-gen images are AI-generated (full license); swap in your own by texting them.
- **Does NOT auto-post to social** (syndication penalty) — it makes the asset; you copy-paste.
- **Persistent memory / privacy:** builds your profile over time; user IP is not used to train models.

## Proof Points

- Agents closed **$100M+** in transactions in 3 months from Halo sites / AI discovery; **1,135+** leads delivered to CRM + phone.
- "Smarty's" week-old site: 4,500 human visits, **cited by AI 9,000+ times.**
- 700-page site: connected Google Search Console, Halo **fixed ~14,000 bugs**, **doubled traffic in a week.**
- Origin for #teamgogo: Gogo built a full website by text in ~2.5 min at **Project 42** (Canada AI event; Eric spoke, Nick organized).
