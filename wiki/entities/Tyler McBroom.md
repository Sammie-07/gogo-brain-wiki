# Tyler McBroom

CPA and founder of TRM CPA (trm.cpa); guest tax trainer at Gogo's #teamgogo open-to-public training.

## Overview

Tyler McBroom runs his own CPA firm (about ten years in), works with many Realtor clients, and says he partners with Tony Robbins. [[Gogo Bethke]] first saw him on stage at a Tony Robbins event and reached out on Instagram afterwards. He taught the September 2026 open training on reducing taxable income.

## Key Facts

- **Philosophy:** the accountant should be a strategist and profit center. Plan during the year and before you earn the income, not after Dec 31 (the "oh-you-did conversation").
- **His five steps:** S-corp election; the Augusta Rule; hiring your kids; cost segregation with bonus depreciation and Real Estate Professional Status; and his advanced "Freedom Fund" deferral structure. Plus the 6,000-lb vehicle rule, meal records and the home office. Full detail and caveats in [[Tax Strategy]].
- **Claims from his free reviews:** 97% of clients overpaid or had the wrong structure; 76% paid roughly double.
- **Offer:** free 15–20 minute tax assessment; most clients pay about $1,500/month all-in (planning, questions, prep), with bookkeeping extra.
