# Google Workspace for Real Estate Agents

Running a real estate business as its CEO on **Google Workspace** instead of "manic chaos" — [[Kalie Kinsella]]'s "Chaos-Free Agent" operating system. The point isn't more software; it's **centralization + habits** so everything is reachable in **three clicks or less**.

## Core Idea

Agents drown in notebooks, sticky notes, and a pile of paid tools. Consolidate onto Workspace (which most agents already pay for), pair each tool with one behavioral habit, and reinvest the saved subscription money into revenue. Foundational rules: use a **custom-domain Workspace** (not `@gmail.com`), **Business Standard** tier (Starter can't mass-email), the **Chrome** browser, and the **official Google apps**. Don't hire an assistant to fix a "you" problem — fix the systems first; and **never train your own assistant** (operations people train operations people).

## The Stack (tool → habit)

- **Gmail** — auto-routing **filters** (not manual dragging) + nested "mommy labels" (Reference / In Progress / Back Office); **Unread First** inbox; **Starred = to-do list**; auto-forward Skyslope notices to your TC; never delete transactional mail.
- **Google Tasks** — "if it's not on my task list or calendar, it doesn't exist"; capture a to-do the instant it appears; one-click from an email (stays linked); surfaces atop Calendar.
- **Google Keep** — sticky-note ideas with no deadline (prospect "sniper list," templates, links, meeting notes).
- **Google Drive** — central storage; native **Request e-signature** replaces DocuSign/Dotloop; replaces Dropbox/Trello/Asana.
- **Google Calendar** — everything on it, integrated & color-coded; **Appointment Schedule = free unlimited Calendly replacement**; **#1 hack = Sunday-night calendar blocking** so the scheduler never double-books.
- **Google Forms** — one intake form with **Sections + branching** (Buy vs Sell) → one linked Sheet; response email alerts; custom confirmation = a follow-up touch. Replaces Jotform/Typeform.
- **Google Sites** — free responsive **resource center** (incl. **2–3 lenders, never one = steering**) and **per-listing landing pages** you own; can out-rank a brokerage-hosted site.
- **Contacts** — a CRM backup **you own** (leaves the brokerage with you).
- **Mass email** — HTML **Layouts** in Compose with auto-unsubscribe; BCC a named list; ~2,500/day. Replaces Constant Contact/Flodesk.
- **Gemini + Google AI Studio** — included AI; AI Studio builds automations from plain-English outcomes (preferred over coder-oriented Google Labs). AI replaces the **mundane**, not you.

## Gogo's Take

> "Everything should be done in two clicks or less." · "Take down a forest one tree at a time" — implement one tool for two weeks, then add the next.
