# Public Speaking and Stage Presentations

Gogo's reusable system for structuring a talk or stage presentation so it earns authority fast, stays easy to deliver, and converts attention into follow.

## Core Idea

A talk is a marketing asset, not an information dump. Go **deep on one topic**, make the slides do the least work possible, and end by moving the audience somewhere (a CTA, or your social).

## Frameworks / Steps

- **Ask the host two questions first:** how long am I speaking, and what topic do you want? Then **zone in on ONE topic** and go deep — don't cram a hundred topics into one session.
- **Draft the deck with AI:** tell it the topic, the length, and **"one slide per minute."** A minute on stage is long — *"like holding a plank"* — so a 20-minute talk is ~20 slides, not 60.
- **Bullets only, no paragraphs:** a title plus a couple of bullets per slide; you fill in the story out loud. (Critical if you can't read dense slides and present at the same time.)
- **Structure:** a short, punchy **authority-earning intro** tied directly to the topic (lead with a striking stat + your relevant credential so you earn the right to be heard) → body slides → a **clear call to action.**
- **If you can't sell from stage,** drive people to your social media instead — "you'll follow me, and eventually I'll sell you something."

## Selling from Stage (Circle coaching, Oct 27)
- Confirm the organizer allows selling first. ~1 minute per slide.
- **Offer in the middle, not the end,** nonchalant ("if you want more, scan this"), on screen ~30 seconds while you keep talking.
- Collect email or phone through a form and send the link (e.g. GoHighLevel) instead of relying on a QR link that vanishes. Add a deadline when the price isn't available elsewhere.
- End with a free gift tied to a 7-day email follow-up back to the offer; a 10% coupon on day 7 is the last touch.
