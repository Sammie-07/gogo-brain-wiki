# Downline Community Architecture

How [[Gogo Bethke]] structures the community around a downline organization so agents know what they have, stay engaged, and don't leave.

## Core Idea

Agents who don't know what they have find it easy to leave. Set up the community before you build a website: a Facebook group for announcements and chats for conversation, split so the right people see the right things. Then lead by example, because *"people don't do what you say, people do what you do."*

## Frameworks / Steps

**1. Two channels.**
- **Facebook group** for reminders, brokerage news, celebrations and monthly recognition, with key posts pinned. "Pretty much everybody's on Facebook."
- **Messenger chats** for conversation.

**2. Two levels of access.**
- **Frontline chat:** only you and the agents you personally sponsored. They get your cell number and your direct attention.
- **General community chat:** everyone, run with your assistant or VA. You join when you can.

**3. Sub-chats so nobody gets flooded.**
- A main chat for anything.
- One chat per event, so people not attending aren't spammed.
- A "generals" chat for agents with at least five personally sponsored agents, focused on attraction.
- State chats once a state reaches roughly 50+ agents, for local help such as covering open houses.
- Chats for referrals, the events calendar, celebrations, special interests and tech.

**4. Celebrate people personally.** Gogo sings happy birthday to each frontline agent in a private chat and posts it in the group, and teaches each level to do the same for their own frontline.

**5. Show up.** If you skip company events, your agents will too.

**6. Access rule.** Organization benefits (the group and chats) are only for agents you sponsored or co-sponsored. Public events can be shared with anyone.
