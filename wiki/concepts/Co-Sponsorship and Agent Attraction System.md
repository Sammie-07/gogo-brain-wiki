# Co-Sponsorship and the Agent-Attraction Website System

Gogo's turnkey way for an agent to partner with [[#teamgogo]] to attract agents — the eXp sponsor/co-sponsor mechanics, the revenue-share math behind them, and the AI-built website funnel that runs it. This is the "CoWithGogo" system.

## Why Co-Sponsor

eXp lets an incoming agent name **two** uplines: a primary **sponsor** and a **co-sponsor**. Co-sponsorship lets any agent plug into Team Gogo's ready-built machine instead of building their own. It exists for agents who feel they have nothing to offer a recruit: **"orphan" agents** with no upline, an upline that left, an upline that does no trainings / masterminds / events / community, or who joined so long ago that only Glenn Sanford sits above them. Gogo's pitch: "I already have it all built — plug in, and I do the heavy lifting."

> "The platform is eXp. The multiplier is the Team Gogo family." Same company benefits everywhere; the *family* you join is the differentiator.

## The Sponsor vs Co-Sponsor Mechanics (get these exact)

Structure: **primary Sponsor → Co-sponsor → oncoming agent.**

- **The oncoming agent gets access to the SPONSOR's upline**, not the co-sponsor's. So to give a new agent Team Gogo's benefits (trainings, [[Honey Badgers]]/Fast Forward, onboarding, etc.), **Gogo must be the primary sponsor** on that agent.
- **Revenue share pays fully to BOTH sponsor and co-sponsor** on the oncoming agent — position does not change rev share, and the agent lands in *both* uplines' downlines.
- **The real difference:** the **sponsor earns the FLQA**; the **co-sponsor earns a $4,000 bonus** if the agent caps in year one. (This is the main first-year-bonus difference.)
- **Gogo prefers the CO position** — she and her husband hold ~70 FLQAs (among the most in the company), so she doesn't need more; she'd rather take the $4,000 cap bonus. "Put me whatever position you want."
- **The one-time access rule:** if the *partnering agent* wants Team Gogo access **for themselves**, make Gogo primary **one time** (the agent goes CO under Gogo, the recruit under the agent → all three connected, everyone gets access). The agent gives up that single FLQA but gets the bonus. Every time after, Gogo goes CO and the agent keeps their FLQAs. If the agent only wants the **recruit** to have access, Gogo goes CO every time.
- **Already in Gogo's downline?** No co-sponsorship needed — anyone you sponsor auto-inherits Team Gogo benefits. And **you cannot co-sponsor with anyone already in your own downline.** Nobody changes their existing sponsor; co-sponsorship only concerns *new* agents you bring.

See [[Real Estate Math#Revenue-Share Levels — FLA, FLQA and the Bonus Structure|the FLA/FLQA level math]] for what the FLQAs actually unlock.

## The Agent-Attraction Website

Self-serve at **jointeamgogo.com/create**, two paths:
- **Team Gogo agent** → a personal *partner page* (must register with the `first.last@exprealty.com` eXp email; free).
- **Not Team Gogo** → a *co-sponsor page*.

Fill a short questionnaire (name, email, phone, a few questions) → an **AI builds a live, hosted website in seconds**. Men get a blue accent, women pink. Personalized URL: **jointeamgogo.com/[yourname]** (that slug is how the system attributes every recruit to the right agent).

**Page structure (and why each piece exists):**
1. **Your intro video** — who you are, how long in the business, why partner with you.
2. **Gogo's video** behind yours ("so excited you found me") — signals the co-sponsorship.
3. **"Let's Talk eXp" button** → your Calendly for eXp conversations.
4. **3-way call button** → you + Gogo + the prospect; used only when you can't close on your own. Gogo presents, handles objections, closes. She is strict on time: the prospect must have watched the videos, know their numbers, and be punctual. ("Watch the videos, book the call, make the decision.")
5. **Step 1 & Step 2 apply buttons** → the Team Gogo application (free; no cut ever taken) + the eXp application.
6. **Built-in Open-to-Public training + Why Not eXp**, always current on the page.

## The Monthly Loop ("well-oiled machine")

1. **Open to the Public** — last Wednesday of every month, **1pm ET**, a real value training (e.g. Sept 30, 2026: [[Tyler McBroom]], Tony Robbins' recommended CPA, on realtor tax savings). Anyone at any brokerage can attend — "try the shoes on before you buy them." Use it as bait; when you post about it, **don't give the link — tell people to reach out to you**, so you capture who's interested.
2. **Follow up** — the inviting agent stays in touch. (**Gogo never follows up with anyone.**)
3. **Why Not eXp** — the **same night, 8pm ET**. Gogo presents the whole eXp model, runs the prospect's numbers (before/after cap, ICON, stock, revenue share), handles objections. The end slide says **"reach out to the person who invited you here today."**
4. **Prospect returns to the inviting agent** → agent closes (or books a 3-way call) → sends them to Step 1 & 2 to sign up.
5. **Rinse and repeat monthly.**

## The Sponsor Onboarding System (Circle coaching)

*Source: [[The Circle Coaching — Post-Close Farming, Sponsor Onboarding and Brand Naming (de-identified)]] (NDA, de-identified).*

Attracting the agent is only half the job — **the sponsor owns onboarding to their own value-adds** (team trainings, resources, community, calendars, referral map); the brokerage only onboards its own tools. Run it so recruits don't drink from a fire hose:
- **Stagger it over ~4 weeks:** wk 1 brokerage onboards → wk ~2 your team benefits → wk ~3 broader upline/community → **wk ~4 a personal catch-up call** ("do you understand what you have, how to use it, and why?"). **Run overlapping onboarding calls separately** (combining them = "deer in the headlights").
- **VA runs the motion; a long-tenured person owns the relationship** — recruits always know who to go to ("a partnership, not just a VA").
- **Application → hand-holding funnel:** an intake form names you as sponsor/co-sponsor → on submit, the sponsor + relationship-lead get an instant text and **start a group thread with the recruit from day one** (prospects take *months* and get cold feet — hold their hand to "active"). **Celebrate at "active"** with a personal welcome.
- Sponsor-switch workaround: you can't switch an existing sponsor, but you can sponsor a **different household member** (e.g., a spouse).

## The Attraction Video, Enrollment Call & Free-Training Funnel (Circle coaching)

*Source: [[The Circle Coaching — Agent-Attraction Video, Enrollment Calls, Speaking and Team Leadership (de-identified)]] (NDA, de-identified).*

**The attraction video + landing page.** Record a simple horizontal YouTube-style intro (don't over-edit; clean quiet room, soft window light). **Segment and name your audience in the video** — new/struggling agents ("I'll make you producing"), established producers who can't earn their fees back (cap-back/icon), and top producers ready to build a team — positioning yourself as someone who walked each stage: *"just like you have an ideal client, you have an ideal agent."* End with **one CTA**; layer a short intro video on top of a longer "watch this next" model-explainer so the first stays short.

**The enrollment call.** Book it as a **video call, not phone** ("harder to say no to a face"), **45 min** (15 can't cover model + numbers + objections + Q&A), titled for what it is. Write a blunt filtering description (licensed agents intending to join and name you sponsor; must watch the videos first; **no-shows aren't rescheduled** — "if you have no consideration for my time, we don't need to be business partners"). Close with **"By when are you planning to make this decision?"** Ready-now agents get the recorded full presentation immediately; month-end prospects get invited to the live.

**Free-training-as-bait funnel.** Open a members-only training to the public **once a month** as an "appetizer" / "try the shoes on," paired with a recorded model-explainer for the ready. Tell attendees "whoever invited you would love for you to attend" — then **the inviter follows up; the leader never chases guests** (helps via a three-way call but doesn't track others' results). Don't post the public link — have prospects **DM you for it** to capture who raised their hand. Skip live recruiting events right before major holidays. Newer partners keep booking [[Real Estate Teams#The Three-Way Call System|three-way calls]] until they can pitch solo, and keep a running partner-target list (including strong agents met across their own deals).


## What the Attraction Page Must Do (Circle coaching)

*Source: [[The Circle Coaching — Peer-Led Calls, the Attraction Page, Downline Community and Delegation Rules (de-identified)]] (NDA, de-identified).*

- **Required:** a video on who you are and why partner with you (about what *they* gain, not your life story); your achievements **in numbers**; and **one page with three steps: watch the videos → book a call → sign up.** After the call, send them back to the same page.
- **Name the booking calendar "Let's Talk eXp"**, never "free coaching call" or "30 minutes with [name]", which attract brain-pickers and product pitches. Start at 30 minutes.
- **Team application form** that texts you and your assistant on submit and asks for their birthday, so agents who sign up months later aren't lost in onboarding.
- **Don't gate the videos** behind a lead form unless you'll really follow up (Gogo collected contacts for years and never did).
- Show a **platform-vs-multiplier comparison** and link to public events and the referral map. Co-sponsors build their page **once** and route later edits through Gogo's support team, since a new site per edit creates duplicates.
- **Cadence and focus:** post attraction content at least weekly; ease from production into attraction (agents want someone still active); recruit **nationwide** rather than locally (local agents feel you're taking their deals); build a weekly habit that puts you with agents, such as a regular golf foursome.
- **Product ladder:** free downline organization → low-ticket recurring digital product → high-ticket coaching. If your upline already runs strong weekly trainings, send people there and add your own monthly session.
- Community setup after sign-up: see [[Downline Community Architecture]].

## The Fishing Framework and the $2M–$7M Avatar (Circle coaching)
- **To catch a whale, fish in the ocean, not a backyard pond.** Define the fish (ideal agent avatar), the bait (content), where and when you fish, and how often.
- **Avatar questions:** gender, age, new or near retirement, team builder or not, monolingual or bilingual, location, buy-side / listing-side / investor, transactions only or open to a second income stream, or aiming to be a top solo agent.
- **Gogo's avatar is the $2M–$7M producer.** At $2M she can add ~2 transactions via referrals, trainings, events and emails; at $7M two more makes them ICON. She no longer takes brand-new agents: she matches them with a local team to reach $2M first. Partners must know the basics (addenda, broker contact, state meetings, running their own open house, PITI).
- **Content by level:** ~$2M wants six figures ("six figures in under eight months," "cap in under three months when you partner with me"); ~$7M wants leverage (first VA, TC from day one, local support team, time with family, not paying ~40% in taxes).
- **Cadence:** 4 days of educational content, 3 days of "fishing" (asking agents to partner). "I teach, I ask."
- **Balance production with recruiting:** don't go all-in on attraction while you still depend on transactions.
- Co-sponsorship is a major entry point for agents outside her communities.

## Keyword for Life and the Two-Click Partner Funnel (Circle coaching)
- **Pick keywords you'll keep for life.** Gogo's "partner" has run ~4 years. Add as many keywords to as many pages as you want ("and," not "or").
- **Write the DM as if texting by hand,** about them, not you: "So excited you're thinking about joining eXp. Can't wait to see you in my calendar."
- **Two clicks:** button, then calendar. Gogo's flow: "So excited you're interested in changing brokerages" → "I'm in" → watch the partner-page video → calendar.
- **A partner page needs:** who you are, why partner with you, ICON status, location, what you provide (weekly trainings, direct line, group chat, Facebook group), a video of you and a direct calendar link.
- Test every funnel from a real account. Per Gogo, eXp now pays $8,000 (instead of $4,000) if an agent you attract caps in their first year (unverified).
